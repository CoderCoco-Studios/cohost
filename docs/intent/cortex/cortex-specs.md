# cortex specs

Design: [cortex-design.md](cortex-design.md). Prefix `CTX`. Verified against the code at git `49ba935` (audit pass 2026-11-01). Owner decisions of 2026-11-01 applied at git `1b57790`. `[x]` = implemented, `[ ]` = intended but not yet implemented (gap), `[D]` = deferred. Test paths are relative to the repo root; a `<!-- test: -->` pointer names the test that carries the matching `@spec` annotation. `[proposed]` inside a line marks a value or choice awaiting the owner's confirmation (viewer notes, CTX-NOTES-*; spoken fallback line, CTX-FALL-*).

## Event handling

- [x] **CTX-EVT-001**: The cortex shall subscribe to the bus in consumer group `cortex` with start mode `latest` (the group is reset to the stream's newest entry on every start, so events published while the cortex was not subscribed, including stale `attention.focus`, are never delivered) for `chat.message`, `speech.heard`, `vision.summary`, `brain.say`, `voice.done`, `attention.focus`, `memory.recall.reply`, `memory.notes.reply`, `memory.viewer`, `affect.state` and `control`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/startup.test.ts:22 -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-002**: When a `chat.message`, `speech.heard` or `vision.summary` arrives, the cortex shall add it to its working-memory window of `brain.working_memory_events` events. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:35 -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-003**: When a `brain.say` arrives from any source, the cortex shall add it to its working memory as a self line with its text and emotion. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:152 -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-004**: When a `brain.say` arrives whose bus source is not `cortex`, the cortex shall record its `id` as the speaking id so that thinking is deferred while it plays. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context (Foreign line) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:137, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:152 -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-005**: When a `voice.done` arrives whose `id` equals the speaking id, the cortex shall clear the speaking id and publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if the cortex is muted or paused; a `voice.done` with any other `id` shall be ignored. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context (voice.done) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:137 -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-006**: When an `attention.focus` arrives, the cortex shall start thinking without awaiting the result, so the subscription loop can still deliver the `memory.recall.reply` it is waiting for. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:97 -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-007**: When an `affect.state` arrives, the cortex shall keep it as the current mood for later prompts. <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:275 -->
- [ ] **CTX-EVT-008**: The cortex shall also subscribe to `voice.failed` in its `cortex` group subscription (CTX-EVT-001).

## Think gating

- [x] **CTX-GATE-001**: When a focus arrives while a think is already in flight, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `thinking` with the new focus reason and shall not call the LLM. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating (Overlapping focus) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:106 -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-002**: While the cortex is muted or paused, a focus shall publish `brain.status` `muted` and make no LLM call. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating (Muted); add-service-control § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:125, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:305 -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-003**: While another line (or its own) is still playing, a focus shall publish `brain.status` `speaking` and make no LLM call. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:137, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:172 -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-004**: When `brain.cortex.speaking_timeout_ms` (default 20000) has elapsed since a line began without a matching `voice.done`, the cortex shall treat the voice as free, so the next focus can proceed to think; the timeout itself schedules no focus and invokes no think. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:161 -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-005**: When the LLM call returns while the cortex has become muted or paused, the cortex shall drop its reply and publish `brain.status` `muted`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:195 -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-006**: When the LLM call returns after another line started speaking, the cortex shall drop its reply and publish `brain.status` `speaking`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control (Spoken over while thinking) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:183 -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-007**: The cortex shall clear its thinking flag on every exit from a think, including errors, so a later focus can run. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:106, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:215 -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-008**: If publishing `brain.status` fails on a gated early-return path (muted, paused or speaking), the cortex shall log the failure and return, instead of raising an unhandled promise rejection from the unawaited think. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:353 -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-009**: When `brain.cortex.speaking_timeout_ms` elapses for a line without a matching `voice.done`, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if it is muted or paused, at that moment. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:377 -->

## Memory recall

- [x] **CTX-RECALL-001**: Before calling the LLM, the cortex shall publish `memory.recall.request` with a fresh `id`, `k = brain.memory.recall_k` and, as the query, the focus text when it is non-empty or else the last vision summary, and shall skip the request when both are empty. <!-- openspec: cortex § Memory recall --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:48, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:63, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:400 -->
- [x] **CTX-RECALL-002**: Where `brain.memory.recall_k` is 0 or less, the cortex shall not publish a recall request. <!-- openspec: cortex § Memory recall (Disabled recall) --> <!-- test: none (the setup default recall_k 0 at cortex.region.test.ts:24 relies on it, but no test asserts the absence of a request) -->
- [x] **CTX-RECALL-003**: When no matching `memory.recall.reply` arrives within `brain.cortex.recall_timeout_ms` (default 2500), the cortex shall log a warning and continue with no memories. <!-- openspec: cortex § Memory recall (Timeout); add-isolated-backends § Isolation preset and closure --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:76, services/cortex/test/mock.test.ts:32 -->
- [x] **CTX-RECALL-004**: If publishing the recall request fails, the cortex shall continue with no memories immediately. <!-- openspec: cortex § Memory recall --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:87 -->
- [x] **CTX-RECALL-005**: When a `memory.recall.reply` arrives, the cortex shall resolve only the recall with the same `id` and ignore replies for other ids. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:48 -->

## Viewer notes

- [x] **CTX-VIEWER-001**: When a `memory.viewer` arrives, the cortex shall store the note `"<user> is new here (first message)."` if `isNew`, `"<user> is a regular (N messages)."` if `regular` is true, and no note otherwise; the note carries no viewer-notes text (those come from `memory.notes.reply`, CTX-NOTES-*). <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:268, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:295 -->
- [x] **CTX-VIEWER-002**: The cortex shall key viewer notes by lowercased user, let a later `memory.viewer` replace or clear the earlier note, and keep at most 50 notes by evicting the oldest. <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:266 (cap untested) -->
- [x] **CTX-VIEWER-003**: When a think's focus names a viewer who has a note, the cortex shall put the note in that reply's prompt and delete it only after the reply's `brain.say` is published, so it is used once, never in another viewer's reply, and kept for the next reply when this one is vetoed, dropped or fails. <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood (One-shot use) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:244, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:411 -->

## Viewer notes from memory

- [x] **CTX-NOTES-001**: When a think passes its gates, the cortex shall publish `memory.notes.request` with a fresh `id` and `users` listing the focus's `who` first (when present) followed by the distinct users of the `chat` events in its working-memory window, newest first, each name once, and shall skip the request when that list is empty `[proposed]`. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:503, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:514, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:530, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:538 -->
- [x] **CTX-NOTES-002**: The cortex shall publish the notes request at the same time as the recall request and wait for both replies within one `brain.cortex.recall_timeout_ms` deadline; when no matching `memory.notes.reply` arrives in time, or publishing the request fails, it shall continue with no viewer notes `[proposed]`. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:551, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:570, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:578, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:590, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:598 -->
- [x] **CTX-NOTES-003**: When a `memory.notes.reply` arrives, the cortex shall resolve only the notes request with the same `id` and ignore replies for other ids. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:609 -->
- [x] **CTX-NOTES-004**: When the notes reply lists at least one viewer, the cortex shall add a `## People here` block to the system message with one entry per listed viewer in reply order, naming the viewer, with no regular marker (the familiarity line of CTX-VIEWER-001 already carries it), and listing that viewer's `general` notes as plain facts in reply order; it shall add no block when the reply lists no viewer `[proposed format]`. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:538, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:623, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:56, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:71, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:101 -->

- [x] **CTX-NOTES-006**: The cortex shall not render a viewer's subscriber status, or any other money-related detail, in the prompt. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:635, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:95 -->
- [x] **CTX-NOTES-007**: The cortex shall use viewer notes only in the prompt of the think that requested them and keep none of them afterwards, so a note the owner deletes is absent from the next reply and notes are shown for every viewer, regular or not. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:538, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:644 -->
- [x] **CTX-NOTES-008**: The cortex shall not write viewer-note text to logs at `info` level or above, nor into any `brain.status` detail; it may log the number of viewers and notes received. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:661 -->

## Prompt and reply

- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-001**: The cortex shall build a two-message prompt: a system message with the trimmed personality, local time in `cohost.timezone`, streamer, co-host name, the emotion-tag output instruction, and a user message with the recent transcript (or `(quiet)`) and a closing nudge. <!-- openspec: cortex § Prompt and reply parsing --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:25, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:62 -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-002**: The cortex shall include the on-screen line, mood line, viewer note and remembered-memories block in the system message only when each has content, rendering memories as `- (YYYY-MM-DD) who: text`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Prompt and reply parsing --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:34, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:56 -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-003**: The cortex shall describe the current mood to the model in words derived from valence and arousal thresholds, not raw numbers. <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:46 -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-004**: The cortex shall choose the closing nudge from the focus reason and kind (idle chatter, mention, speech, unmentioned chat, vision, none). <!-- openspec: cortex § Prompt and reply parsing --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts:70 -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-005**: When parsing a model reply, the cortex shall use only the first line, read a leading `[emotion]` and optional `[gesture:name]`, strip other bracketed text, quotes and `Name:` prefixes, and map an unknown emotion to `neutral`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Prompt and reply parsing --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.test.ts:6 -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-006**: When the parsed reply text is shorter than two characters, the cortex shall publish no `brain.say` and publish `brain.status` `idle`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition (text >= 2 chars) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:204 -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-007**: When the LLM replies, the cortex shall log the elapsed time, focus reason and memory count at info level and the raw reply text only at debug level. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:437 -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-008**: When the cortex calls `llm.chat`, every transcript line it renders through working-memory for another speaker (`[chat]`, `[voice]`, `[screen]`) shall begin with a tag that is one of model-backends' transcript stop strings (`LLM-STOP-006`), and its own lines, which begin with the co-host name, shall not match any stop string; a cortex test renders each line kind through `Context.transcript` and checks it against the provider's stop list. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/transcript-stops.test.ts:30 -->

## Fallback acknowledgement

Design: LLD § Fallback acknowledgement. "The base chance" is `brain.cortex.fallback_ack_chance` (default 0.25, CFG-SCH-019). "A pending glitch" is the intended text and fallback text the cortex keeps after a fallback line played.

- [ ] **CTX-FALL-001**: When a `brain.say` arrives from any source, the cortex shall add it to working memory with its `id` (`pushSelf(text, emotion, id)`, WM-PUSH-007).
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-002**: When a `voice.failed` carrying `fallback` arrives, the cortex shall mark the working-memory line with that `id` as replaced by `fallback.text` (`markFallback`, WM-PUSH-008).
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-003**: When a `voice.failed` carrying `fallback` arrives and working memory still holds the line with that `id`, the cortex shall keep a pending glitch holding that line's text and `fallback.text`, replacing any earlier pending glitch `[proposed]`.
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-004**: When a think builds its prompt while a glitch is pending, its line is still in working memory (WM-WIN-007) and the base chance is above 0, the cortex shall add one line to the system message's `## Now` block that quotes the intended text and the fallback text, states the base chance as a whole percentage, and invites the co-host to react in character only if it fits her `[proposed wording]`.
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-005**: The cortex shall clear the pending glitch when the reply of the think that carried its hint is published as `brain.say`, on `control` `forget_context`, or when its line is no longer in working memory, and shall keep it after a vetoed, empty or failed reply `[proposed]`.
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-006**: The cortex shall not draw a random number for, add or withhold the acknowledgement hint by, or check the reply against, the base chance: the model decides in character whether to acknowledge.
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-007**: When a `voice.failed` without `fallback` arrives, the cortex shall change neither working memory, the pending glitch nor its speaking state `[proposed]`.

## Critic

- [x] **CTX-CRITIC-001**: When a reply has two or more characters, the cortex shall pass `{text, emotion, reason, transcript}` through the `cortex.critic` module configured by `brain.cortex.critic`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:227 -->
- [x] **CTX-CRITIC-002**: When the critic returns `ok: false`, the cortex shall log the note, publish no `brain.say` and publish `brain.status` `idle`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition (Veto) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:227 -->
- [x] **CTX-CRITIC-003**: When the critic returns `text` or a valid `emotion`, the cortex shall speak the replacement text and use the replacement emotion. <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition (Rewrite) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:227 -->
- [x] **CTX-CRITIC-004**: Where the critic kind is `rules`, the cortex shall pass every line through unchanged. <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts:34 -->
- [x] **CTX-CRITIC-005**: When an `llm` or `http` critic output is unusable (no boolean `ok`), the cortex shall fall back to pass-through (as it shall when the critic throws or times out), and from a usable output shall trim `text`, drop a blank `text` or an unknown `emotion`, and keep `note` only when it is a string. <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition --> <!-- code: critic.ts:16-24 --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts:41, services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts:51, services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts:58 -->
- [x] **CTX-CRITIC-006**: Where the critic kind is `llm`, the cortex shall ask the model, as the co-host's inner editor and within 160 tokens, to block a line that is hateful, doxxes anyone, breaks character, narrates instead of speaking, repeats something said moments ago or is longer than one short spoken line (the length rule of the reply prompt, `CTX-PROMPT-001`), to return a fixed line when a small edit fixes it, and to answer with JSON only. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts:67 -->

## Control

- [x] **CTX-CTRL-001**: When a `control` event has a `target` other than `cortex`, the cortex shall ignore it; an event with no target applies to the cortex. <!-- openspec: add-service-control § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:305, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:322 -->
- [x] **CTX-CTRL-002**: When `control` `pause` is addressed to the cortex, the cortex shall stop starting LLM calls, publish `brain.status` `muted` with detail `paused`, and keep ingesting events; `resume` shall return it to `idle` (or `muted` if also muted). <!-- openspec: add-service-control § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:305 -->
- [x] **CTX-CTRL-003**: When `control` `mute` or `unmute` is addressed to the cortex, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `muted`, or `idle` (`muted` if still paused) respectively, and make no LLM call while muted. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating (Muted) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:125 -->
- [x] **CTX-CTRL-004**: When `control` `forget_context` is addressed to the cortex, the cortex shall clear its working-memory window, the last vision summary and its viewer notes, keeping the mood and any pending recall. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:283, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:449 -->
- [x] **CTX-CTRL-005**: When `control` `reload_personality` is addressed to the cortex, the cortex shall re-read the personality file for later prompts. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:283 -->
- [x] **CTX-CTRL-006**: When `control` `say` with text is addressed to the cortex, the cortex shall publish `brain.say` with that text, emotion `neutral` and reason `operator` as the operator override: it shall not check mute, pause or voice busy; `say` without text shall do nothing. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control (Operator say) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts:283 (gate bypass not asserted) -->
