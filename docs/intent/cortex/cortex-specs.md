# cortex specs

Design: [cortex-design.md](cortex-design.md). Prefix `CTX`. `[x]` = implemented, `[ ]` = intended but not yet implemented (gap), `[D]` = deferred. Test paths are relative to the repo root; a `<!-- test: -->` pointer names the test that carries the matching `@spec` annotation.

## Event handling

- [ ] **CTX-EVT-001**: The cortex shall subscribe to the bus in consumer group `cortex` with start mode `latest` (the group is reset to the stream's newest entry on every start, so events published while the cortex was not subscribed, including stale `attention.focus`, are never delivered) for `chat.message`, `chat.removed`, `speech.heard`, `vision.summary`, `brain.say`, `brain.clause`, `voice.utterance`, `voice.playing`, `voice.done`, `voice.stop`, `attention.focus`, `memory.recall.reply`, `memory.notes.reply`, `memory.viewer`, `affect.state` and `control`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/startup.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-002**: When a `chat.message`, `speech.heard` or `vision.summary` arrives, the cortex shall add it to its working-memory window of `brain.working_memory_events` events. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-003**: When a `brain.say` arrives from any source, the cortex shall add it to its working memory as a self line with its text and emotion. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-004**: When a `brain.say` arrives whose bus source is not `cortex`, the cortex shall add its `id` to the lines pending on the voice (a line that has begun and not yet ended), so that thinking is deferred while any pending line plays. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context (Foreign line) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-EVT-005**: When a `voice.done` arrives whose `id` is a pending line without `more`, the cortex shall end that line and, once no line is pending, publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if the cortex is muted or paused; a `voice.done` for an id that is not pending shall end no line and publish no `brain.status`; the cortex shall not publish a status for a `voice.done` while a think is in flight. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context (voice.done) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.dispositions.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-006**: When an `attention.focus` arrives, the cortex shall start thinking without awaiting the result, so the subscription loop can still deliver the `memory.recall.reply` it is waiting for. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-007**: When an `affect.state` arrives, the cortex shall keep it as the current mood for later prompts. <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-008**: The cortex shall also subscribe to `voice.failed` in its `cortex` group subscription (CTX-EVT-001). <!-- test: services/cortex/test/startup.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-009**: The cortex shall not subscribe to `speech.partial`, and a `speech.partial` shall never be added to its working-memory window or start a think: only `speech.heard` is speech to the cortex.
- [x] **CTX-EVT-010**: When a `voice.stop` arrives, the cortex shall end every pending line whose `id` it names (every pending line when `ids` is absent) and, once no line is pending, publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if the cortex is muted or paused; a `voice.stop` that ends no pending line shall publish no `brain.status`.
- [ ] **CTX-EVT-011**: When a `voice.done` flagged `interrupted` with `spokenText` arrives, the cortex shall record `spokenText` as the heard text of the working-memory line with that `id` (`markHeard`), whether or not that line is still pending.
- [ ] **CTX-EVT-012**: When a `voice.done` that is flagged neither `interrupted` nor `final` arrives, the cortex shall clear the heard record of the working-memory line with that `id`.

## Think gating

- [x] **CTX-GATE-001**: When a focus arrives while a think is already in flight, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `thinking` with the new focus reason and shall not call the LLM. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating (Overlapping focus) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-002**: While the cortex is muted or paused, a focus shall publish `brain.status` `muted` and make no LLM call. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating (Muted); add-service-control § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-003**: While any line (another source's or its own) is pending on the voice, a focus shall publish `brain.status` `speaking` and make no LLM call. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-GATE-004**: When `brain.cortex.speaking_timeout_ms` (default 20000) has elapsed without progress (CTX-REL-001) on a line that has a clause outstanding, the cortex shall release that line (a streamed line with CTX-STREAM-036), so once no other line is pending the next focus can proceed to think; the timeout itself schedules no focus and invokes no think. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-GATE-005**: When the cortex's final re-check runs before the first clause (after the critic has returned), and the cortex has become muted or paused, the cortex shall drop its reply and publish `brain.status` `muted`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-GATE-006**: When the cortex's final re-check runs before the first clause (after the critic has returned), and another line has started speaking, the cortex shall drop its reply and publish `brain.status` `speaking`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control (Spoken over while thinking) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-007**: The cortex shall clear its thinking flag on every exit from a think, including errors, so a later focus can run. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-008**: If publishing `brain.status` fails on a gated early-return path (muted, paused or speaking), the cortex shall log the failure and return, instead of raising an unhandled promise rejection from the unawaited think. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-GATE-009**: When `brain.cortex.speaking_timeout_ms` elapses without progress on a line (CTX-REL-001) and no other line is then pending, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if it is muted or paused, at that moment, except that it shall not publish while a think is in flight. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-GATE-010**: When a `voice.stop` arrives, the cortex shall not cancel, restart or start a think that has not published its first clause: such a think in flight shall complete and its reply shall be subject to CTX-GATE-005 and CTX-GATE-006 as if no stop had been read, and no think shall start for the stop itself.
- [ ] **CTX-GATE-011**: When a `voice.done` flagged `interrupted` arrives for a line that a `voice.stop` has already ended, the cortex shall change no pending line and publish no `brain.status`, and shall still record its `spokenText` (CTX-EVT-011).
- [ ] **CTX-GATE-012**: When a think ends without a line (an empty reply, a critic veto, a repetition hit on the first clause, or a mute or busy re-check that fails before the first clause), the cortex shall publish no `brain.say` and shall publish `brain.status` `muted` if it is muted or paused, else `speaking` while any line is pending or playing, else `idle`.
- [ ] **CTX-GATE-013**: When a think has a clause to publish, the cortex shall run these steps in this order: the critic (by its kind, CTX-STREAM-012 and CTX-STREAM-013), the repetition check, the mute and busy re-check, then publish the clause.

## Deferred: dynamic speech (REPLAN)

Design: LLD Decisions & Alternatives, "Interruptible, dynamic speech (deferred)"; [HLD](../../high-level-design.md) Future / ideal state. Prerequisites still to build: a playback position (MOUTH-POS-001), the interrupted-thought stack (WM-STACK-001), a re-think call inside the latency budget and live vision for screen events. Clause-chunked speech and streaming model output are built on (the Streamed replies section below, VOICE-CHUNK-001, VOICE-CHUNK-002).

- [D] **CTX-REPLAN-001**: Where speech is dynamic, the cortex shall, at each clause boundary of a line being spoken and when a key event has arrived (a final `speech.heard`, or a screen event once live vision exists), decide whether to continue the line, adapt its remainder, pivot to the event, or resume a thought it interrupted earlier, and shall make that decision within the speech path's latency budget.

- [x] **CTX-RECALL-003**: When no matching `memory.recall.reply` arrives within `brain.cortex.recall_timeout_ms` (default 2500), the cortex shall log a warning and continue with no memories. <!-- openspec: cortex § Memory recall (Timeout); add-isolated-backends § Isolation preset and closure --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/mock.test.ts -->

## Viewer notes

- [ ] **CTX-VIEWER-001**: When a `memory.viewer` arrives, the cortex shall store the note `"<name> is new here (first message)."` if `isNew`, `"<name> is a regular (N messages)."` if `regular` is true, where `<name>` is the name as CTX-VIEWER-004 renders it, and no note otherwise; the note carries no viewer-notes text (those come from `memory.notes.reply`, CTX-NOTES-*). <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-VIEWER-002**: The cortex shall key viewer notes by the `@cohost/shared/untrusted` `viewerKey` of the `memory.viewer`'s `platform`, `userId` and `user` (a `memory.viewer` that carries no `platform` makes no note), let a later `memory.viewer` replace or clear the earlier note, and keep at most 50 notes by evicting the oldest. <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.dispositions.test.ts (the 50-note cap) -->
- [ ] **CTX-VIEWER-003**: When a think's focus identifies a viewer (by the `@cohost/shared/untrusted` `viewerKey` of the focus's `platform`, `userId` and `who`) who has a note, the cortex shall put the note in that reply's prompt and delete it only after the reply's `brain.say` is published, so it is used once, never in another viewer's reply, and kept for the next reply when this one is vetoed, dropped or fails. <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood (One-shot use) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-VIEWER-004**: When the cortex stores the familiarity note for a `memory.viewer`, it shall write the viewer's name as `speakerLabel(user, viewer_text.max_name_chars)`.

- [ ] **CTX-NOTES-001**: When a think passes its gates, the cortex shall publish `memory.notes.request` with a fresh `id` and `viewers` listing the focus's viewer first (when present) followed by the distinct viewers of the `chat` events in its working-memory window, newest first, each viewer once by `viewerKey`, each entry carrying the display name as `user` and the `platform` and `userId` (when present) of its event, and shall skip the request when that list is empty. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-NOTES-002**: The cortex shall publish the notes request at the same time as the recall request and wait for both replies within one `brain.cortex.recall_timeout_ms` deadline; when no matching `memory.notes.reply` arrives in time, or publishing the request fails, it shall continue with no viewer notes. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-NOTES-003**: When a `memory.notes.reply` arrives, the cortex shall resolve only the notes request with the same `id` and ignore replies for other ids. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-NOTES-011**: When the cortex renders the viewers of a `memory.notes.reply`, it shall match each reply entry to a requested viewer by `platform` and `userId` when the entry carries them, and by `user` only for an entry that carries none, so notes of one viewer are never shown as another's.
- [ ] **CTX-NOTES-004**: When the notes reply lists at least one viewer, the cortex shall add a `## People here` block to the system message with one entry per listed viewer in reply order, naming the viewer as `speakerLabel(user, viewer_text.max_name_chars)`, with no regular marker (the familiarity line of CTX-VIEWER-001 already carries it), and listing that viewer's `general` notes as quoted facts in reply order (CTX-NOTES-009); it shall add no block when the reply lists no viewer. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-NOTES-005**: When the notes reply includes a `health` or `family` note, the cortex shall render only its handling hint in that viewer's entry, as guidance for how to speak to the viewer (for example a warm, gentle check-in), never in the plain-facts list; such notes carry no text (MEM-SURF-004), and the cortex shall not ask for or add any. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts -->

- [x] **CTX-NOTES-007**: The cortex shall use viewer notes only in the prompt of the think that requested them and keep none of them afterwards, so a note the owner deletes is absent from the next reply and notes are shown for every viewer, regular or not. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-NOTES-008**: The cortex shall not write viewer-note text to logs at `info` level or above, nor into any `brain.status` detail; it may log the number of viewers and notes received. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-NOTES-009**: When the cortex lists a `general` note in the `## People here` block, it shall write the note's text as `quoteUntrusted(text, viewer_text.max_chars)`, which flattens it to one line and bounds it, whatever was stored.
- [ ] **CTX-NOTES-010**: When the cortex renders the handling hint of a `health` or `family` note, it shall write the hint as `boundText(hint, viewer_text.max_chars)`, not quoted.

## Prompt and reply

- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-001**: The cortex shall build a two-message prompt: a system message with the trimmed personality, local time in `cohost.timezone`, streamer, co-host name, the emotion-tag output instruction, and a user message with the recent transcript (or `(quiet)`) and a closing nudge. <!-- openspec: cortex § Prompt and reply parsing --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-002**: The cortex shall include the on-screen line, mood line, viewer note and remembered-memories block in the system message only when each has content, rendering each memory as `- (YYYY-MM-DD) <label>: <text>`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Prompt and reply parsing --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts, services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-003**: The cortex shall describe the current mood to the model in words derived from valence and arousal thresholds, not raw numbers. <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-004**: The cortex shall choose the closing nudge from the focus reason and kind (idle chatter, mention, speech, unmentioned chat, vision, none). <!-- openspec: cortex § Prompt and reply parsing --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/prompt.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-005**: When parsing a whole model reply (a reply collected for an `llm` or `http` critic, or a critic's replacement text), the cortex shall use only the first line, read a leading `[emotion]` and optional `[gesture:name]`, strip other bracketed text, quotes and `Name:` prefixes, and map an unknown emotion to `neutral`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Prompt and reply parsing --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-006**: When the parsed reply text is shorter than two characters, the cortex shall publish no `brain.say` and end the think without a line (CTX-GATE-012). <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition (text >= 2 chars) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-PROMPT-007**: When the LLM replies, the cortex shall log the elapsed time, focus reason and memory count at info level and the raw reply text only at debug level. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-008**: When the cortex calls `llm.stream`, every transcript line it renders through working-memory for another speaker (`[chat]`, `[voice]`, `[screen]`) shall begin with a tag that is one of model-backends' transcript stop strings (`LLM-STOP-006`), and its own lines, which begin with the co-host name, shall not match any stop string; a cortex test renders each line kind through `Context.transcript` and checks it against the provider's stop list. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/transcript-stops.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-009**: When the cortex builds the system message, it shall place the `@cohost/shared/untrusted` `untrustedNotice` with the subject `what a viewer wrote in chat, or what is remembered about a viewer` immediately before the `## Output` block.
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-010**: When the cortex builds the user message, it shall use the rendered transcript lines from `Context.transcript` as they are, and shall not quote them again.
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-011**: When the cortex renders a recalled memory, it shall write the memory's `who` as `speakerLabel(who, viewer_text.max_name_chars)`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-012**: When the cortex renders a recalled memory of kind `chat`, it shall write its text as `quoteUntrusted(text, viewer_text.max_chars)`, whatever length the stored text has.
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-013**: When the cortex renders a recalled memory of kind `speech` or `self`, it shall write its text as `boundText(text, brain.input_limits.speech_max_chars)`, not quoted.
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-014**: When the cortex writes the closing nudge, it shall write the focus's `who` as `speakerLabel(who, viewer_text.max_name_chars)`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROMPT-015**: When parsing a model reply, the cortex shall strip `«` and `»` from the text.

## Fallback acknowledgement

Design: LLD § Fallback acknowledgement. "The base chance" is `brain.cortex.fallback_ack_chance` (default 0.25, CFG-SCH-019). "A pending glitch" is the intended text and fallback text the cortex keeps after a fallback line played.

- [x] **CTX-FALL-001**: When a `brain.say` arrives from any source, the cortex shall add it to working memory with its `id` (`pushSelf(text, emotion, id)`, WM-PUSH-007). <!-- test: services/cortex/test/fallback.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-002**: When a `voice.failed` carrying `fallback` arrives for a line without `more` and no other sink has published `voice.playing` for it, the cortex shall mark the working-memory line with that `id` as replaced by `fallback.text` (`markFallback`, WM-PUSH-008). <!-- test: services/cortex/test/fallback.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-003**: When a `voice.failed` carrying `fallback` arrives for a line without `more`, no other sink has published `voice.playing` for it and working memory still holds the line with that `id`, the cortex shall keep a pending glitch holding that line's text and `fallback.text`, replacing any earlier pending glitch. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/fallback.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-FALL-004**: When a think builds its prompt while a glitch is pending, its line is still in working memory (WM-WIN-007) and the base chance is above 0, the cortex shall add one line to the system message's `## Now` block that quotes the intended text and the fallback text, states the base chance as a percentage rounded to the nearest whole number, and invites the co-host to react in character only if it fits her. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/fallback.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-FALL-005**: The cortex shall clear the pending glitch when the reply of a think whose prompt carried that same glitch's hint is published as `brain.say` (a newer glitch that replaced it stays pending), on `control` `forget_context`, or when its line is no longer in working memory, and shall keep it after a vetoed, empty or failed reply. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/fallback.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-FALL-006**: The cortex shall not draw a random number for, add or withhold the acknowledgement hint by, or check the reply against, the base chance: the model decides in character whether to acknowledge. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/fallback.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-007**: When a `voice.failed` without `fallback` arrives for a line without `more` and no other sink has published `voice.playing` for it, the cortex shall record the working-memory line with that `id` as heard by nobody (`markHeard` with the empty string) and shall change neither the pending glitch nor its speaking state. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/fallback.test.ts -->

## Critic

- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-001**: When the reply, or with the `rules` critic its first clause, has two or more characters, the cortex shall pass `{text, emotion, reason, transcript}` through the `cortex.critic` module configured by `brain.cortex.critic`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-002**: When the critic returns `ok: false`, the cortex shall log the note, publish no `brain.say` and end the think without a line (CTX-GATE-012). <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition (Veto) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-003**: When the critic returns a valid `emotion`, the cortex shall use it as the line's emotion, and when it returns `text` the cortex shall speak the parsed replacement (CTX-CRITIC-007). <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition (Rewrite) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-CRITIC-004**: Where the critic kind is `rules`, the cortex shall pass every line through unchanged. <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-CRITIC-005**: When an `llm` or `http` critic output is unusable (no boolean `ok`), the cortex shall fall back to pass-through (as it shall when the critic throws or times out), and from a usable output shall trim `text`, drop a blank `text` or an unknown `emotion`, and keep `note` only when it is a string. <!-- openspec: cortex § Critic inhibition --> <!-- code: critic.ts:16-24 --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts, services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts, services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-CRITIC-006**: Where the critic kind is `llm`, the cortex shall ask the model, as the co-host's inner editor and within 160 tokens, to block a line that is hateful, doxxes anyone, breaks character, narrates instead of speaking, repeats something said moments ago or is longer than one short spoken line (the length rule of the reply prompt, `CTX-PROMPT-001`), to return a fixed line when a small edit fixes it, and to answer with JSON only. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/critic.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-007**: When the critic returns `text`, the cortex shall parse it with the reply parser used for the model's reply (first line only, bracketed text and the delimiters `«` and `»` stripped) and use the parsed text as the line.
- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-008**: If the parsed critic replacement is shorter than two characters, then the cortex shall publish no `brain.say` and end the think without a line (CTX-GATE-012).
- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-009**: When the critic returns `text`, the cortex shall ignore an emotion tag inside that text and take the emotion from the critic's `emotion` when it is valid and otherwise from the model's reply.
- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-010**: Where the critic kind is `llm`, the cortex shall carry the `@cohost/shared/untrusted` `untrustedNotice` with the subject `what a viewer typed in chat` in the critic's system prompt.
- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-011**: When the cortex builds the `llm` critic prompt, it shall use the `transcript` of the critic input as it is, and shall not quote it again.
- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-012**: When the critic returns `text`, the cortex shall keep the gesture of the model's original reply, and shall strip a `[gesture:...]` inside the critic's text like any other bracketed span.
- [ ] **CTX-CRITIC-013**: Where the critic kind is `http`, the cortex shall send the `transcript` as `Context.transcript` rendered it, with its `«` and `»` marks, and no notice.

## Saying

- [ ] **CTX-SAY-001**: When the cortex has a first clause to publish, it shall publish `brain.say` with a fresh `id`, the clause as text, emotion, gesture and the focus reason, and then publish `brain.status` `speaking` with the text as detail. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->

## Repetition check (REPEAT)
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-001**: When a think has a clause about to be published after the critic, the cortex shall run the repetition check on the reply so far, every clause up to and including that one, before the mute and busy re-check; with an `llm` or `http` critic it shall run the check once, on the whole reply, before any clause is published.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-002**: When the cortex runs the repetition check, it shall compare the text against every `self` event in its working-memory window, whatever the source of that line, except the event of the line being streamed.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-003**: When the cortex takes the text of a recent line for the repetition check, it shall use the event's `heard` text when it has one, else its `fallback` text, else its text.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-004**: When a recent line's `heard` text is the empty string, the cortex shall leave that line out of the repetition check.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-005**: When the cortex computes the word set of a line for the repetition check, it shall lower-case the line with `toLowerCase()` and no locale argument, remove apostrophes (U+0027 and U+2019) inside words, and take as words the maximal runs of characters in the Unicode categories L, M and N, each distinct word once.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-006**: If both lines of a comparison have an empty word set, then the cortex shall not count them as a repeat.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-007**: If either line of a comparison has fewer than three words, then the cortex shall count the two lines as a repeat only if their word sets are equal and non-empty.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-008**: When both lines of a comparison have at least three words, the cortex shall count them as a repeat if the size of the intersection of the two word sets divided by the size of their union is at least `brain.cortex.repeat_similarity`.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-009**: Where `brain.cortex.repeat_similarity` is 0, the cortex shall not run the repetition check.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-010**: When the repetition check finds a repeat on the first clause, the cortex shall publish no `brain.say`, end the think without a line (CTX-GATE-012) and log the similarity and the matching line.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-011**: When the repetition check finds a repeat on the first clause, the cortex shall keep the viewer note and the pending glitch for the next think.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-012**: When the repetition check finds a repeat, the cortex shall make no further LLM request for that focus.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-013**: The repetition check shall not withhold a line that did not come from a think: a salience reflex line, an operator `say` line or a spoken fallback line.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-014**: If the working-memory window holds no `self` event, then the repetition check shall find no repeat.
- [ ] **CTX-REPEAT-015**: The repetition check shall use no model and perform no I/O.

## Streamed replies (STREAM, REL)

Design: LLD Streaming the reply, Releasing a streamed line, Stopping its own line on a mute or pause. A reply is one line with one id; its `brain.say` carries the first clause and its `brain.clause` events the rest (event-bus Streamed lines). Cortex's own words are: the reply is being streamed while the model stream is read, closed once its closing `brain.clause` is published; a line is pending until the cortex releases it.

- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-001**: The cortex shall treat as a streamed line a line whose `brain.say` carried `more`, and shall release a line without `more` as CTX-EVT-005 says, whatever other sinks play it.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-002**: The cortex shall treat a think as in flight until its reply closes (its closing `brain.clause` is published) or, when it publishes no line, until the think ends.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-003**: When a think reaches the model, the cortex shall call `llm.stream` with the reply messages and an `AbortSignal` of its own, offering no tools, and shall ignore any `tool_call` event.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-004**: When the cortex reads the reply stream, it shall ignore leading whitespace and shall treat the first newline after the first non-whitespace character as the end of the reply, stop reading and abort the stream.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-005**: While the leading tags of a streamed reply are incomplete, the cortex shall hold the start of the stream and publish nothing, until an optional `Name:`, a `[emotion]` and an optional `[gesture:name]` are each closed, or the text after an optional `Name:` does not begin with `[`, or 80 characters have arrived.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-006**: If 80 characters have arrived with a `[` still unclosed, then the cortex shall take the emotion `neutral` and no gesture, keep the bracketed text as ordinary reply text, and start the first clause at the start of the buffer.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-007**: When the leading tags are decided, the cortex shall take the line's emotion from the `[emotion]` tag (`neutral` when it is absent or unknown) and its gesture from the `[gesture:name]` tag, and shall start the first clause after the tags.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-008**: When the cleaned text of the reply reaches a boundary and the clause so far has at least `brain.cortex.clause_min_chars` characters, the cortex shall end the clause there; a boundary is one of `. ! ? … ; : ,` or `。！？、，；：` followed by whitespace, with a directly following closing quote or bracket kept in the clause.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-009**: A punctuation mark inside a bracketed span, or not followed by whitespace, shall not be a clause boundary.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-010**: When `brain.cortex.clause_max_chars` cleaned characters, those that will be spoken with bracketed text excluded, have arrived without a boundary, including while a bracket is unclosed, the cortex shall end the clause at the last whitespace before that length, or at that length when there is none, and shall keep dropping the rest of the bracketed span when it closes.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-011**: When the stream ends or the first newline is read, the cortex shall take the text left, whatever its length, as the last clause.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-012**: Where the critic is `rules`, the cortex shall check and publish each clause as it arrives, shall ask the `cortex.critic` hook once, on the first clause, and shall not ask it for later clauses.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-013**: Where the critic is `llm` or `http`, the cortex shall read the stream to its end, parse and criticise the whole reply as one text (CTX-CRITIC-002, CTX-CRITIC-003, CTX-CRITIC-007), run the repetition check once on the result, cut it into clauses by the clause rule and publish them in order, each behind the mute and busy re-check, and shall stop publishing as soon as the line is released or a `voice.stop` covers it.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-014**: When the cortex cleans a clause it shall remove bracketed spans, the delimiters `«` and `»` and surrounding quotes from every clause, and a leading `Name:` from the first clause only.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-015**: When a clause has no word character after cleaning, the cortex shall not publish it and shall drop its text.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-016**: Where the critic is `rules`, the cortex shall run the repetition check on the first clause and then the mute and busy re-check before publishing it.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-017**: When the first clause has passed its checks, the cortex shall publish `brain.say` with the clause as `text`, the line's `emotion` and `gesture`, the focus `reason`, `timing` and the propagated injection flags.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-018**: When the cortex has published the first clause, it shall publish `brain.status` `speaking` with that clause as detail.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-019**: When the first clause is published and the stream has not ended with nothing but whitespace after it, the cortex shall set `more` to `true` on the `brain.say`; when the whole reply was that one clause it shall publish the `brain.say` without `more` and publish no `brain.clause`.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-020**: When a later clause passes its checks, the cortex shall publish `brain.clause` with the line's `id`, the next `seq` from 1, the clause as `text` and no `last`, and shall publish no `brain.status` for it.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-021**: When the repetition check finds a repeat on a later clause, the cortex shall not publish that clause and shall close the line with `cut` `repeat` (CTX-STREAM-026).
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-022**: When the cortex is muted or paused at the re-check of a later clause, it shall not publish that clause and shall close the line with `cut` `muted`.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-023**: When a line from someone else is pending or playing at the re-check of a later clause, the cortex shall not publish that clause and shall close the line with `cut` `busy`; the reply's own line shall not count, and an operator `say` line shall count as someone else's.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-024**: When a clause to publish would have a `seq` of `brain.cortex.max_clauses` or more, the cortex shall not publish it and shall close the line with `cut` `length`.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-025**: A cut of a reply shall fall on a clause boundary: a clause is published whole or not at all.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-026**: When the cortex closes a line with a `cut`, it shall abort the model stream if it is being streamed, then publish the closing `brain.clause` with empty `text`, `last` true, that `cut` and `through` set to the last published clause.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-027**: When the stream ends normally and a line published with `more` has text left that passes its checks, the cortex shall publish it as the closing `brain.clause` with `last` true, no `cut` and `through` equal to its `seq`; when nothing is left it shall publish the closing `brain.clause` with empty `text`, `last` true, no `cut` and `through` the last published clause.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-028**: The cortex shall publish exactly one closing `brain.clause` for every line whose `brain.say` carried `more`, whatever ended the reply, and shall publish none when the reply had already closed.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-029**: When a reply closes, or a think ends without a line, the cortex shall clear the thinking flag.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-030**: If the stream fails before the cortex has published a clause, then the cortex shall log `think failed`, publish `brain.status` `idle` and publish no `brain.say`, even when text had arrived.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-031**: If the stream fails after the cortex has published a clause, then it shall close the line with `cut` `failed`, speak no fallback line and attempt no repair.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-032**: When the stream ends with a `finish` of `refusal` and nothing is published, the cortex shall end the think without a line (CTX-GATE-012); when a clause was published it shall close the reply normally.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-033**: The cortex shall read `brain.cortex.clause_min_chars`, `brain.cortex.clause_max_chars` and `brain.cortex.max_clauses` from configuration, as the config segment defines them.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-034**: When a `brain.clause` of the cortex's own arrives, the cortex shall add its text to the working-memory line with that `id` (`extendSelf(id, seq, text)`), and shall change nothing when the window no longer holds that line.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-035**: When a `voice.stop` names a streamed line whose closing `brain.clause` has not been published (its reply is being streamed, or its clauses are still being published), or carries no `ids`, the cortex shall close the line with `cut` `stopped`.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-036**: When the no-progress valve ends a streamed line whose closing `brain.clause` has not been published (its reply is being streamed, or its clauses are still being published), the cortex shall close the line with `cut` `timeout`.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-037**: When the cortex stops, it shall close each streamed line whose reply had not closed with `cut` `stopped`, as far as the bus still accepts the publish.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-001**: The cortex shall count as progress of a line every `voice.utterance`, `voice.playing` and `voice.done` for its `id` from any publisher, and the publication of a clause while none of the line's clauses is outstanding; a clause is outstanding from its publication until every player of the line has reported a `voice.done` for it or a `final` done, so a player that never completes keeps the no-progress valve running.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-002**: When a `voice.playing` for a pending streamed line arrives, the cortex shall record its `sink` as a player of the line.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-003**: The cortex shall record a player of a streamed line as having completed it when the player has reported a `voice.done` for the line's last produced clause, or a `voice.done` flagged `final` or `interrupted`.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-004**: The cortex shall take a streamed line's last produced clause to be the highest `seq` it published, and, when a `voice.done` flagged `final` arrives from the sink `error` with `seq` k, to be k minus 1.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-005**: Whenever a streamed line's last produced clause changes, the cortex shall judge each player's completion again against the new value.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-006**: When a streamed line has at least one player, its reply has closed and every player of the line has completed it, the cortex shall release the line.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-007**: When a streamed line's reply has closed, no sink has published `voice.playing` for it, and either its failure condition (CTX-REL-012) holds or a `voice.done` flagged `final` from the sink `error` for clause 0 has been read, the cortex shall release the line.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-008**: While another player of a streamed line has not completed it, a `voice.done` flagged `final` from one sink shall not release the line.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-009**: When completion or a `voice.stop` releases a streamed line and leaves no line pending and no think is in flight, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if it is muted or paused (a release by the valve is CTX-GATE-009).
- [ ] **CTX-REL-010**: When a think ends or its reply closes and no line is then pending, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if it is muted or paused; when a line is pending it shall publish no status.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-011**: The cortex shall treat a streamed line's claim window as ended 1500 ms (the claim window of VOICE-SINK-002) after it read the line's first `voice.utterance`.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-012**: The failure condition of a streamed line shall hold when at least one `voice.failed` or `final` `voice.done` has been read for it, its claim window has ended and every sink that published `voice.playing` for it has ended it with a `final` done or a `voice.failed`; with no such sink it shall hold when a failure has been read and the window has ended.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-013**: When the failure condition of a streamed line holds, the cortex shall close the line with `cut` `failed`.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-014**: While the failure condition of a streamed line does not hold, a `voice.failed` or `final` `voice.done` from one sink shall cut nothing: the cortex shall keep reading the stream and publishing clauses.
- [ ] **CTX-REL-015**: When a `voice.done` flagged `final` arrives from the sink `error` for a streamed line whose reply has not closed, the cortex shall close the line with `cut` `failed` and `through` set to the clause before the failed one (0 when that is clause 0).
- [ ] **CTX-REL-016**: When a `voice.failed` arrives for a streamed line, the cortex shall apply the working-memory mark and the pending glitch (CTX-FALL-008) once the line's closing `brain.clause` has been read back, or at once when the reply closed earlier.
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-008**: When a `voice.failed` carrying `fallback` arrives for a streamed line and no other sink has published `voice.playing` for it when the cortex applies it, the cortex shall mark the working-memory line with the failed `seq` and the fallback text (`markFallback`), and shall keep a pending glitch holding the text of the clauses published from that `seq` on, the fallback text and that `seq`, replacing any earlier pending glitch.
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-009**: When a `voice.failed` arrives for a line and another sink has published `voice.playing` for it when the cortex applies it, the cortex shall not mark the working-memory line, keep no glitch and record no heard text, because the line was heard on another output.
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-010**: Where a pending glitch began after the first clause of its line, the prompt hint shall say "the rest of your line" in place of "your line" and quote only the clauses that were not heard.
- [ ] **CTX-FALL-011**: When a `voice.failed` without `fallback` arrives for a streamed line and no other sink has published `voice.playing` for it, the cortex shall record the clauses before the failed `seq` as the heard text of the working-memory line (`markHeard`), the empty string when that `seq` is 0, and shall change neither the pending glitch nor its speaking state.
- [ ] **CTX-CTRL-008**: When `control` `mute` or `pause` is addressed to the cortex while it has a reply line of its own pending (a line a think published, streamed or not), the cortex shall stop that line, closing it with `cut` `muted` if it is streamed and its reply had not closed.
- [ ] **CTX-CTRL-009**: When `control` `mute` or `pause` is addressed to the cortex while it has reply lines pending, it shall publish one `voice.stop` whose `ids` name those lines and whose `reason` is `muted` for a mute and `paused` for a pause, and shall release those lines.
- [ ] **CTX-CTRL-010**: An operator `say` line, a salience reflex line and a line of another source shall not be stopped by a `mute` or `pause`.
- [ ] **CTX-CTRL-011**: When the cortex has no reply line pending, `control` `mute` or `pause` shall publish no `voice.stop`.
- [ ] **CTX-EVT-013**: When a `voice.stop` the cortex published for a mute or pause is read back, the cortex shall release the lines it names as it does for any `voice.stop`.

## Failure on clause 0

Design: LLD Releasing a streamed line. Voice publishes no `voice.utterance` for a clause that fails to synthesize, so the claim window never starts.

- [ ] **CTX-FALL-012**: When a `voice.done` flagged `final` from the sink `error` for clause 0 of a streamed line is read, the cortex shall record the working-memory line as heard by nobody (`markHeard` with the empty string), set no pending glitch, and close the line with `cut` `failed` and `through` 0.
