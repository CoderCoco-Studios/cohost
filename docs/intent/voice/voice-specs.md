# voice specs

Design: [voice-design.md](voice-design.md). Prefix `VOICE`. Audited against the code on 2026-11-02; owner decisions of 2026-11-01 implemented. `[x]` = implemented, `[ ]` = intended but not yet implemented (gap), `[D]` = deferred. Test paths are relative to the repo root.

## Synthesis

- [x] **VOICE-SYN-001**: When the voice service starts, it shall subscribe with consumer group `voice` and the event-bus `latest` start mode (BUS-SUB-010) to `brain.say`, `voice.playing` and `voice.failed`, reading events one at a time in stream order, so that the group is moved to the newest stream entry on every start and no `brain.say` published while voice was down is spoken. <!-- openspec: voice § Synthesis --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:48 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SYN-002**: When `voice.engine` is `kokoro` and a `brain.say` arrives, the voice service shall POST JSON `{model: "kokoro", input: <text>, voice: voice.voice, speed: voice.speed, response_format: "wav"}` to `<TTS_URL>/v1/audio/speech` and use the response body as the utterance WAV. <!-- openspec: voice § Synthesis --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:105 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SYN-003**: If the TTS server returns a non-2xx status, then the voice service shall raise the error "tts <status>: <body>", which is handled as a synthesis failure. <!-- openspec: voice § Synthesis --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:105 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SYN-004**: When a `brain.say` has been synthesized, the voice service shall store the WAV under the event `id` before publishing `voice.utterance`. <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:269 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SYN-005**: The voice service shall synthesize every `brain.say` it reads, whatever its `reason`, `injected` or `remember` fields, and shall not subscribe to `control`: mute and pause are applied upstream by cortex and salience. <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:283 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:48 (the subscribed types exclude `control`) -->
- [x] **VOICE-SYN-006**: If any voice service startup step after the configuration loads fails (binding the HTTP port, positioning the consumer group) or the voice service's bus subscription rejects (a failure the bus's own retry, BUS-SUB-009, does not absorb), then the voice service shall log the error with message `fatal` and exit with a non-zero status instead of leaving an unhandled rejection; a configuration that does not load is reported by the shared bootstrap (SHARED-BOOT-002), which logs the loader's message and exits with status 1. <!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:94 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:105 -->

- [x] **VOICE-SYN-007**: When the voice service reads a `brain.say`, it shall put the line on an internal queue and return to reading the bus at once, and shall synthesize queued lines one at a time in arrival order, so that a `voice.playing` or `voice.failed` that follows is read and applied while a line is still being synthesized; if handling a queued line throws past its own `tts failed` handling, then the voice service shall log `synthesis failed` and go on with the next line. <!-- test: services/voice/test/queue.test.ts -->
- [x] **VOICE-SYN-008**: When the voice service stops, it shall drop every queued `brain.say`, publish nothing for a line that was being synthesized, and take no `brain.say` read afterwards. <!-- test: services/voice/test/queue.test.ts -->

## Stub engine

- [x] **VOICE-STUB-001**: Where `voice.engine` is `stub`, the voice service shall make no HTTP request to the TTS service and shall synthesize a zero-filled mono 16-bit WAV at `voice.sample_rate` lasting `max(400, 60 * text.length)` milliseconds. <!-- openspec: voice § Stub engine --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/stub.test.ts:11 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/stub.test.ts:21 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/stub.test.ts:30 -->
- [x] **VOICE-STUB-002**: Where `voice.engine` is `stub`, when a `brain.say` arrives, the voice service shall publish a real `voice.utterance` with `durationMs`, `sampleRate`, an all-zero 30 Hz `envelope` and a fetchable `audioUrl`. <!-- openspec: voice § Stub engine --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/stub.test.ts:30 -->
- [x] **VOICE-STUB-003**: Where `voice.engine` is `stub`, the voice service shall log a startup warning that utterances are silent. <!-- openspec: voice § Isolation preset and mode --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:131 -->

## Utterance publication

- [x] **VOICE-UTT-001**: When a `brain.say` is synthesized, the voice service shall publish `voice.utterance` carrying `id`, `text`, `emotion`, `gesture`, `durationMs` (rounded from the WAV), `envelopeHz` 30, `envelope`, `audioUrl` and `sampleRate` (from the WAV). <!-- openspec: voice § voice.utterance publication --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:21 -->
- [x] **VOICE-UTT-002**: The voice service shall set `audioUrl` to `<urls.voice()>/audio/<id>.wav`, where `urls.voice()` is `VOICE_URL` or else `http://127.0.0.1:<VOICE_PORT>` (default port 8700). <!-- openspec: voice § voice.utterance publication --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:21 --> <!-- note: the loopback default is deliberate (commit e53badc, tested at packages/config/test/config.test.ts:207); compose sets VOICE_URL=http://voice:8700. The frozen OpenSpec baseline still says http://voice:<VOICE_PORT>. -->
- [x] **VOICE-UTT-003**: If synthesis, WAV parsing, envelope extraction or publishing of `voice.utterance` fails for a `brain.say`, then the voice service shall log the error "tts failed", publish `voice.done` with that `id` and `sink` `error`, and publish no `voice.utterance`. <!-- openspec: voice § Failure handling --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:61 (synthesis and parse failures only) -->

## WAV and lip-sync envelope

Retired, numbers not reused: VOICE-ENV-001 and VOICE-ENV-002 (now shared-runtime SHARED-AUDIO-001 and SHARED-AUDIO-002).

- [x] **VOICE-ENV-005**: The voice service shall parse every WAV it synthesizes with the shared `parseWav` (SHARED-AUDIO-001 to SHARED-AUDIO-003), and a WAV it rejects shall take the `tts failed` path (VOICE-UTT-003). <!-- test: services/voice/test/queue.test.ts (a WAV with no `fmt ` chunk) -->
- [x] **VOICE-ENV-003**: When computing a lip-sync envelope, the voice service shall emit one value per window of `floor(sampleRate / 30)` frames of the first channel (including a partial last window), each `min(1, (rms / 0.22) ^ 0.8)`, smoothed so each value is at least 0.6 times the previous one, rounded to 2 decimals. <!-- openspec: voice § Envelope extraction --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/wav.test.ts:21 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/wav.test.ts:35 -->
- [x] **VOICE-ENV-004**: If a WAV is not 16-bit, then envelope extraction shall throw "unsupported bit depth <n>". <!-- openspec: voice § Envelope extraction --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/wav.test.ts:55 -->

## Sink arbitration

- [x] **VOICE-SINK-001**: When the voice service reads a `voice.playing` whose `sink` is not `virtual`, it shall record the event `id` as claimed by a real sink. <!-- openspec: voice § Virtual sink --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:51 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-002**: When 1500 ms have passed since the voice service published a `voice.utterance` and its `id` has not been claimed, the voice service shall publish `voice.playing` with that `id` and `sink` `virtual`. <!-- openspec: voice § Virtual sink --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:37 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-003**: When the voice service has published a virtual `voice.playing`, it shall publish `voice.done` with the same `id` and `sink` `virtual` after the utterance `durationMs`, counted from when the `voice.playing` publish resolved (or failed), so a slow publish never puts the two events back-to-back. <!-- openspec: voice § Virtual sink --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:37 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:195 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-004**: If an utterance `id` has been claimed by a non-virtual sink when its 1500 ms claim window ends, then the voice service shall publish no virtual `voice.playing` or `voice.done` for it. <!-- openspec: voice § Virtual sink --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:51 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-005**: The voice service shall not treat its own `sink: "virtual"` `voice.playing` as a claim. <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:234 (observable only with a re-used id, because the virtual voice.playing is published only after the claim check) -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-006**: If synthesis fails for an utterance, then the voice service shall publish `voice.done` with `sink` `error` and shall arm no virtual-sink timers, so that cortex is released without any audio. <!-- openspec: voice § Failure handling --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:61 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-007**: While the voice service is running, it shall bound the set of claimed utterance ids by dropping a claim when its audio is evicted from the store, and shall record a claim only for an id whose audio is stored. <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:250 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-008**: When a non-virtual sink's `voice.playing` for an utterance `id` arrives after the voice service has published its virtual `voice.playing` for that `id` and before the virtual `voice.done`, the voice service shall cancel the pending virtual `voice.done`, so that the only `voice.done` published for the `id` afterwards is the real sink's own; a claim that arrives while the virtual `voice.playing` publish is still pending shall mean no virtual `voice.done` is published at all; a claim that arrives after the virtual `voice.done` has been published shall change nothing, and a claim for one `id` shall not cancel another `id`'s pending virtual `voice.done`. <!-- openspec: voice § Virtual sink --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:134 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:148 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:160 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:217 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-009**: If publishing a virtual `voice.playing` or `voice.done` fails, then the voice service shall log the failure (`virtual sink publish failed`) and keep running. <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:173 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-010**: The voice service shall also read `voice.failed` on its one `voice` loop (VOICE-SYN-001), in stream order with `brain.say` and `voice.playing`. <!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:47 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-011**: When the voice service reads a `voice.failed` without `fallback` for a stored utterance `id` that no real sink has claimed and whose 1500 ms claim window has not ended, and that window then ends with the `id` still unclaimed, the voice service shall, in place of VOICE-SINK-002 and VOICE-SINK-003, publish `voice.done` with that `id` and `sink` `virtual` at once and publish no virtual `voice.playing` for it. <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:294,311 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-012**: When the voice service reads a `voice.failed` without `fallback` for an utterance `id` whose virtual `voice.playing` has been published and whose virtual `voice.done` is still pending, the voice service shall cancel the pending timer and publish that virtual `voice.done` at once, in place of VOICE-SINK-003's wait for `durationMs`. <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:322,338 -->
- [x] **VOICE-SINK-013**: When the voice service reads a `voice.failed` that carries `fallback`, or names an `id` already claimed by a real sink, or names an `id` whose audio voice does not hold, the voice service shall change no claim, timer or published event because of it (a fallback's `voice.playing` claim is handled by VOICE-SINK-001 and VOICE-SINK-008). <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:357,386 -->

## Audio store and HTTP

- [x] **VOICE-HTTP-001**: The voice service shall keep at most the newest 50 synthesized WAVs in memory, evicting the oldest first. <!-- openspec: voice § Audio store and HTTP --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:91 -->
- [x] **VOICE-HTTP-002**: When `GET /audio/<id>.wav` is requested for a stored id, the voice service shall respond 200 with `content-type: audio/wav`, the WAV length and `access-control-allow-origin: *`. <!-- openspec: voice § Audio store and HTTP --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:71 -->
- [x] **VOICE-HTTP-003**: If `GET /audio/<id>.wav` names an unknown or evicted id (ids are limited to word characters and hyphens), then the voice service shall respond 404 with body `gone`, and for any other path it shall respond 404 with an empty body. <!-- openspec: voice § Audio store and HTTP --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:71 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:91 (status codes only; no test reads the 404 bodies) -->
- [x] **VOICE-HTTP-004**: When `GET /health` is requested, the voice service shall respond 200 with body `ok`. <!-- openspec: voice § Audio store and HTTP --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/voice.test.ts:71 -->
- [x] **VOICE-HTTP-005**: The voice service shall listen for HTTP on `VOICE_PORT` (default 8700). <!-- openspec: voice § voice.utterance publication --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:81 -->
- [x] **VOICE-HTTP-006**: When the voice service's HTTP server receives a request whose method is neither `GET` nor `HEAD`, it shall respond 405 with `allow: GET, HEAD` whatever the path, and for `HEAD` on a path that `GET` serves it shall respond with the same status and headers and no body. <!-- test: services/voice/test/queue.test.ts -->
- [x] **VOICE-HTTP-007**: The voice service shall match `/audio/<id>.wav`, `/fallback/manifest.json`, `/fallback/<name>` and `/health` against the request's path without its query string. <!-- test: services/voice/test/queue.test.ts -->
- [x] **VOICE-HTTP-008**: When the voice service stores a WAV under an id it already holds, it shall replace the stored WAV and make the id the newest entry, so the store holds each id once and evicts the oldest distinct id first. <!-- test: services/voice/test/queue.test.ts -->

## Fallback clips

Design: LLD § Fallback Clips. "The clip store" is voice's own directory `<dataDir()>/voice/fallback-clips` (CFG-DIR-006, CFG-DIR-007). "The phrase list" is `voice.fallback.lines` (CFG-SCH-017). "The committed set" is the set the store's `manifest.json` names.

- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-001**: Where `voice.engine` is `kokoro`, when the voice service starts it shall compute the clip fingerprint as the SHA-256 of a canonical JSON of the phrase list (in order, each phrase trimmed), `voice.engine`, the TTS model name, every `voice` setting its synthesizer sends to the TTS server (today `voice.voice` and `voice.speed`) and a clip-format version number. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:56 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-002**: If the computed fingerprint equals the `fingerprint` in the clip store's `manifest.json` and every clip file the manifest lists exists and is not empty, then the voice service shall make no TTS request for fallback clips and leave the clip store unchanged. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:113,127 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-003**: When the computed fingerprint differs from the manifest's, or the clip store has no readable manifest, the voice service shall regenerate the clips after reporting `ready` and without awaiting the regeneration on its bus loop, synthesizing one phrase at a time in list order with the same TTS request as an utterance (VOICE-SYN-002), and shall run at most one regeneration at a time. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:74,97,142 --><!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:174 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-004**: When a regeneration has synthesized every phrase it keeps, the voice service shall write the clips into a new temporary directory inside the clip store, rename it to a new set directory `sets/<generatedAt>-<random suffix>` that no other regeneration uses, and then replace `manifest.json` by renaming a fully written temporary file over it, so that the committed set is always complete. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:156,180 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-005**: If a TTS request fails with a connection error or a 5xx status, or writing the clip store fails, during a regeneration, then the voice service shall abandon that regeneration, leave `manifest.json` and the committed set unchanged, log a warning, and retry the regeneration after a delay starting at 60 s and doubling to a 15 min cap until one succeeds or voice stops. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:189,203,236,258 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-006**: If one phrase's TTS request returns a 4xx status, or its reply does not parse as a 16-bit WAV, or the clip is shorter than 100 ms, then the voice service shall leave that phrase out of the set, log a warning naming the phrase's index, and continue with the remaining phrases; the committed manifest carries the computed fingerprint, so the skipped phrase is not retried until the fingerprint changes. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:272,302 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-007**: When the voice service has committed a new manifest, it shall delete every set directory other than the committed set, and every temporary directory in the clip store older than one hour. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:314 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-008**: Where `voice.engine` is `stub`, the voice service shall neither generate fallback clips nor create, modify or delete anything in the clip store, and shall answer `GET /fallback/manifest.json` and every `GET /fallback/<name>` with 404. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:400 --><!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:156 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-009**: The voice service shall write `manifest.json` as JSON holding `version`, `fingerprint`, `generatedAt` (epoch ms), `set` (the set directory relative to the clip store) and `clips`, a list in phrase order of `{name, text, durationMs, bytes, sha256}`, where `name` is `<two-digit index>-<first 8 hex characters of sha256>.wav`. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:74 --><!-- test: services/voice/test/mouth-sync.test.ts:25 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-010**: When the phrase list is empty, the voice service shall commit a manifest whose `clips` list is empty, making no TTS request. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:335 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-011**: When `GET /fallback/manifest.json` is requested and a set is committed and `voice.engine` is not `stub`, the voice service shall respond 200 with `content-type: application/json` and the committed manifest without its `set` field; when no set is committed, it shall respond 404. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:360 --><!-- test: services/voice/test/mouth-sync.test.ts:25 -->
- [x] **VOICE-CLIP-012**: When `GET /fallback/<name>` is requested for a clip `name` listed in the committed manifest and `voice.engine` is not `stub`, the voice service shall respond 200 with `content-type: audio/wav`, `content-length` and the clip's bytes; for any other name it shall respond 404. <!-- test: services/voice/test/clips.test.ts:376 --><!-- test: services/voice/test/mouth-sync.test.ts:25 -->

## Health

- [x] **VOICE-HLTH-001**: When the voice service's HTTP server is listening and its consumer group has been positioned (so it can receive `brain.say`), it shall report heartbeat state `ready` with `service` `voice` and `deps` `{kokoro}` (mode `live` with the `kokoro` engine), and shall not report `ready` before both conditions hold. <!-- openspec: voice § Heartbeat (add-service-health) --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/health.test.ts:6 --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:62 -->
- [x] **VOICE-HLTH-002**: When a Kokoro synthesis fails, the voice service shall set `deps.kokoro` to `down` (heartbeat `degraded`) and set it back to `ok` when the next synthesis succeeds. <!-- openspec: voice § Heartbeat (add-service-health) --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/health.test.ts:13 -->
- [x] **VOICE-HLTH-003**: Where `voice.engine` is `stub`, the voice service shall report heartbeat mode `mock` with `deps.kokoro` `n/a` and state `ready`. <!-- openspec: voice § Isolation preset and mode --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/stub.test.ts:52 -->
- [x] **VOICE-HLTH-004**: When the voice service receives SIGINT or SIGTERM, it shall publish one `stopping` heartbeat, close its HTTP server, stop the bus and exit with code 0. <!-- openspec: voice § Heartbeat (add-service-health) --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/health.test.ts:6 (the single stopping heartbeat) --> <!-- test: services/voice/test/boot.test.ts:122 (server close and bus stop; signal handling and exit are injected) -->
- [x] **VOICE-HLTH-005**: When the voice service stops, it shall clear every pending claim-window timer and virtual `voice.done` timer, so that no virtual `voice.playing` or `voice.done` is published afterwards. <!-- test: services/voice/test/queue.test.ts -->
