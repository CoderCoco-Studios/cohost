# Commercial licensing

- **To the public:** the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only](LICENSE)
  (`AGPL-3.0-only`).
- **Commercially:** a separate licence that CoderCoco alone grants, case by
  case, without the AGPL obligations.

## What the AGPL already allows

The AGPL is an open source licence. It does not forbid commercial use. Anyone
may use, modify and run cohost, for profit too, including offering it to
other people over a network as a service, provided they release the complete
corresponding source of the version they run or distribute, with their
modifications, under the AGPL. If you can do that, you do not need anything
from this page.

## When you need the commercial licence

You need a commercial licence from CoderCoco if you want to use cohost in a
way the AGPL does not allow, for example:

- running a modified cohost as a hosted service without publishing your
  changes;
- shipping cohost, or code derived from it, inside a closed-source product;
- combining cohost with code whose licence is incompatible with the AGPL.

CoderCoco can offer these terms because every outside contribution is made
under the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md), which lets CoderCoco
license the whole codebase on terms other than the AGPL.

## How to ask

Open an issue on the
[cohost repository](https://github.com/CoderCoco/cohost/issues) titled
"Commercial licence enquiry". Say who you are and how you intend to use
cohost; leave out anything confidential, since issues are public.
