# ears specs

Segment `ears` (prefix `EARS`). LLD: [ears-design.md](ears-design.md). Markers: `[x]` implemented, `[ ]` intended but not yet implemented (gap), `[D]` deferred. Ears holds no Discord client and consumes connector sources ([connections](../connections/connections-specs.md)).

The playback sink and the spoken fallback line belong to [mouth](../mouth/mouth-specs.md).

## Startup plans

- [x] **EARS-START-001**: When `ears.sources` is empty (heartbeat mode `off`), the ears service shall create no Discord client, require no Discord environment variables, publish no `speech.heard` other than injected ones, and stay running idle with heartbeat mode `off` and state `ready`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:16 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Off source -->
- [x] **EARS-START-003**: The ears service shall subscribe its consumer group `ears` to `inject.request` only, in every startup plan, and shall publish no `voice.playing`, `voice.done` or `voice.failed`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:34 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Off source -->
- [x] **EARS-START-004**: The ears service shall choose its startup plan with the pure function `earsPlan(sources, hasDirPath)`, which for a single enabled source returns `off` for no source, the tag itself for a connector source, and the plans of EARS-CAP-004 for `http` and `dir`, and shall create no Discord client and load no `discord.js` whatever the sources (EARS-CON-002). <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/no-discord.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-START-005**: When the process receives a shutdown signal, the ears service shall, in the `off` plan, clear the idle warning timer, stop the bus and exit with code 0 (the sequence with sources is EARS-SRC-004). <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-START-006**: The ears service shall subscribe its consumer group `ears` with start mode `latest`, so the group is reset to the newest entry on every start and no `inject.request` published while ears was down is handled. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:76 -->

- [x] **EARS-START-008**: The ears service shall start through the shared service bootstrap (`runService`, SHARED-BOOT-*) and shall run its `off` and `no-dir` plans through the shared idle plan (`startIdle`, SHARED-IDLE-*), with the idle reason text of EARS-CAP-010. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->


## Listening and transcription

- [x] **EARS-HEAR-001**: When `audio.in.<source>` frames for a speaker id (`spk`) arrive from a connector source, the ears service shall collect them into one utterance buffer per `spk`, close the utterance when no frame from that `spk` has arrived for `ears.silence_ms` (default 700), and hand it to transcription labelled with the frames' `name` (the `spk` when empty) and `speakerId` set to `spk`; it shall hold at most one open buffer per `spk`. Excluding bot accounts is the Discord connector's (CONN-DISC-007). <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-002**: When a speaker's utterance is closed (EARS-HEAR-001), the ears service shall treat the collected 16 kHz mono PCM as a clip for `Hearing.heardClip` with the source tag of the stream, with no further conversion. <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-004**: When a captured utterance is shorter than `ears.min_speech_ms` (default 400), the ears service shall neither call speech-to-text nor publish anything. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:29 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-005**: When a captured utterance is long enough, the ears service shall convert it to 16 kHz mono WAV and POST it as multipart form data to `<STT_URL>/v1/audio/transcriptions` with fields `file`, `model` set to `ears.stt_model` (default `Systran/faster-whisper-small`), `language` set to `en`, and `response_format` set to `json`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:21,48 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-007**: If the transcript is empty or contains no word character (a Unicode letter or number), then the ears service shall publish nothing. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:29 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-006**: When the transcript contains at least one word character (a Unicode letter or number, in any script) and is not a drop phrase (EARS-HEAR-010), the ears service shall publish `speech.heard` with `speaker`, `speakerId`, `text` and `durationMs` (rounded to whole milliseconds). <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:21 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-011**: The ears service shall publish `speech.heard` `speakerId` as the speaker id (`spk`) the source gave, unmodified (for Discord, the user id), so downstream services can tell two people with the same display name apart; it shall keep no identity mapping of its own. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-008**: If speech-to-text fails (non-2xx response or thrown error), then the ears service shall log the warning "transcription failed", publish no event, and keep running. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:40 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-010**: When a live captured utterance transcribes to a transcript that, compared case-insensitively after trimming whitespace and every leading and trailing non-letter, non-number character and collapsing inner whitespace, equals an entry of `ears.drop_phrases` (default empty, compared the same way), the ears service shall publish nothing for it and log it at debug level. Injected clips are not filtered. <!-- test: services/ears/test/filter.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/transcript.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-009**: The ears service shall not subscribe to `control`: it publishes `speech.heard` regardless of `mute` or `pause`, which cortex and salience enforce upstream. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:44 -->

## Capture sources

- [x] **EARS-CAP-001**: The ears service shall read each clip from a capture source through the capture contract (`CaptureAdapter`, `decodeClip`) and pass it to `Hearing.heardClip`, which shall resample it to 16 kHz mono, drop it when shorter than `ears.min_speech_ms`, transcribe it, drop a transcript with no word character or equal to an `ears.drop_phrases` entry, and otherwise publish `speech.heard` with the given `speaker`, `text` and `durationMs`, without `injected` and without `speakerId`, returning the outcome (`heard`, `too-short`, `empty`, `drop-phrase` or `stt-error`). <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-002**: The ears service shall decode an uploaded clip with the shared `parseWav` when its content type is `audio/wav`, `audio/x-wav` or `audio/wave`, and as raw little-endian 16-bit PCM when it is `audio/L16` or `audio/pcm` with `rate` and `channels` parameters defaulting to 16000 and 1, and shall refuse any other type and any audio that does not decode (including non-16-bit WAV). <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-003**: The ears service shall create no Discord client, load no `discord.js` and require no Discord environment variable in any plan, shall subscribe its consumer group `ears` to `inject.request` only (EARS-START-003, EARS-START-006) and shall handle injection whatever the sources (EARS-INJ-009). <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/no-discord.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-004**: For a single enabled source the ears service shall choose its plan with `earsPlan(sources, hasDirPath)` returning `http` for source `http`, `dir` for source `dir`, `no-dir` for source `dir` with an empty `ears.dir.path`, and the plans of EARS-START-004 otherwise. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-005**: Where the ears plan is `http`, when a `POST /audio` carries a decodable clip, the ears service shall pass it to `Hearing.heardClip` with the `speaker` query parameter (default `http`) and answer 200 with JSON `{ heard, text?, reason? }` after the clip has been processed, and shall answer 404 for another path, 405 for another method, 415 for an unsupported content type, 400 for audio that does not decode and 502 when speech-to-text failed. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-006**: The ears `http` source shall refuse with 413 a body larger than `INJECT_MAX_BYTES` without buffering past that cap, cut off a body that takes longer than `INJECT_READ_TIMEOUT_MS`, process one clip at a time, and refuse with 503 a request that arrives while one clip is being processed and `INJECT_QUEUE_LIMIT` (shared-runtime SHARED-INJ-010) others are waiting. `INJECT_MAX_BYTES` and `INJECT_READ_TIMEOUT_MS` are the limits of EARS-INJ-010. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-007**: Where the ears plan is `http`, the ears service shall listen on `ears.http.host` and `ears.http.port`, report the heartbeat `ready` once it is listening, and, when the port cannot be bound, stay running with the heartbeat `degraded` and `deps.http` `down`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts, services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-008**: Where the ears plan is `dir`, the ears service shall poll `ears.dir.path` every `ears.dir.poll_ms`, create the directory when it is missing, and process each regular file whose name ends in `.wav` (any case) once, only after its size is unchanged between two consecutive polls, through `Hearing.heardClip` with speaker `dir`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-dir.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-009**: After a `dir` file is processed, the ears service shall rename it with the suffix `.done`, or `.rejected` when it is larger than `INJECT_MAX_BYTES` (never read) or does not decode, or `.failed` when speech-to-text failed; if the rename fails it shall remember the name in memory and never read the file again. It shall take at most `INJECT_QUEUE_LIMIT` files per poll, in name order, one at a time. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-dir.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-010**: Where `dir` is an enabled source and `ears.dir.path` is empty, the ears service shall not exit, shall create no poller, and shall idle `degraded` with the detail `ears.dir.path is not set` (shared idle plan), still subscribed to `inject.request`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-011**: Where the only enabled source is `http` or `dir`, the ears heartbeat shall report mode `live` (`mock` with the stub engine), `deps.discord` `n/a` and `deps.stt` as in EARS-HLTH-003, plus `deps.http` or `deps.dir` `ok` while the listener is bound or the directory is being polled and `down` otherwise. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-012**: When the process receives a shutdown signal in the `http` or `dir` plan, the ears service shall close the listener or stop the poller, wait for the clip in flight, and then let `runService` stop the bus (one `onStop` step). <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts, services/ears/test/capture-dir.test.ts -->

## Multiple sources

Several audio sources run at once; each is a capture adapter with its own health, and each clip carries the name of its source. Design: [ears-design.md § Multiple sources](ears-design.md#multiple-sources). The specs above that name a single source (EARS-START-001, EARS-START-004, EARS-CAP-003, EARS-CAP-004, EARS-HLTH-001) read as the one-source case of these.

- [x] **EARS-SRC-001**: The ears service shall read the enabled listening sources from `ears.sources`, a list of names that are `http`, `dir` or a connector source tag matching `[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,31}` (CONN-TAG-001; the set of tags is open, registered by connectors, and ears holds no list of them; default `[discord]`, empty meaning `off`), and shall start every enabled source concurrently; a name repeated in the list shall start one adapter. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts --> <!-- test: packages/config/test/config-ears-sources.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-002**: Where `ears.sources` is unset, the ears service shall read it as `[discord]`. A name that is not `http`, `dir` or a valid tag shall fail config validation (CFG-SCH-030). <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts --> <!-- test: packages/config/test/config-ears-sources.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-003**: The ears service shall obtain its plan from `earsPlan(sources, hasDirPath)` as the set of sources to start, each either runnable or a configuration gap with its reason (a `dir` source with an empty `ears.dir.path`, EARS-CAP-010), and shall log an error for each gap without exiting. A connector source is always runnable: a connector that is not present is a dependency `down` (EARS-CON-005), not a gap. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-004**: If one source fails (its `start()` rejects, its listener cannot be bound, its connector is absent or its capture ends unexpectedly), then the ears service shall mark that source's dependency `down` and keep every other source running, and shall not restart, delay or stop them; on shutdown it shall stop each source independently, so a source whose `stop()` throws does not prevent the others from stopping, before `runService` stops the bus. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-005**: The ears heartbeat shall carry one dependency per enabled source named for it (`http`, `dir` or the connector tag), `ok` while that source is delivering and `down` otherwise, shall report `discord`, `http` and `dir` as `n/a` when not enabled, and shall be `degraded` while any enabled source is `down`; `stt` shall stay shared by all sources (EARS-HLTH-003). <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-006**: When the ears service publishes a live `speech.heard` from a source, it shall include the optional string field `source` set to the source's name (`http`, `dir` or the connector tag), and shall publish an injected transcript without `source`. A `speech.heard` without `source` shall remain valid on the bus (BUS-EVT-013). <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-007**: The ears service shall apply the same minimum duration, word-character and drop-phrase filters (EARS-CAP-001) to a clip from every source, and shall not drop or merge clips because another source is active. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-008**: Where no enabled source is runnable (the `off` plan, or every enabled source is a configuration gap), the ears service shall take the shared idle plan with the reason of each gap and still handle `inject.request` (EARS-START-008, EARS-INJ-009); where at least one source is runnable, it shall subscribe and handle injection as in EARS-START-003 and EARS-START-006. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-009**: Where an enabled connector source's live registration offers `audio-in` with `transport` `http` (CONN-DESK-003; today the `desktop` helper), the ears service shall serve that source through the `http` listener, shall start no audio-device capture and load no native audio dependency, and shall bind one listener for every such source and for `http` alike, starting it when `http` is enabled or when such a registration is first seen. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-010**: The ears `http` listener shall accept the optional `source` query parameter on `POST /audio`, whose value is `http` (the default when absent) or the tag of an enabled source whose live registration has `transport` `http`, shall refuse with 400 any other value (so a stream-served tag, a tag with no live registration and a mistyped tag cannot invent a source), shall pass the accepted value to `Hearing.heardClip` as the clip's source, and shall use it as the default speaker label when `speaker` is absent. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-012**: The ears service shall report `deps.<tag>` of a listener-fed source `ok` while the listener is bound and a live registration offers `audio-in` for the tag with `ready` true (CONN-REG-006), and `down` otherwise, including before the helper first registers; while it is `down` the other sources shall keep running (EARS-SRC-004) and it shall return to `ok` when the registration reappears, without a restart. A clip received for the tag does not by itself make it `ok`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SRC-013**: Where `ears.http.token` is non-empty (set through `EARS_HTTP_TOKEN`), the ears `http` listener shall answer 401 to a `POST /audio` without a matching `Authorization: Bearer` header, and where it is empty shall require none. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts --> <!-- test: packages/config/test/config-ears-sources.test.ts -->
- [ ] **EARS-SRC-014**: The capture helper shall run on Windows (WASAPI loopback of the default render device) and on Linux (the PulseAudio or PipeWire monitor source of the default sink, selectable by name), shall convert the feed to 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM, and on an unsupported host or an unopenable device shall exit non-zero with a message and post nothing.
- [ ] **EARS-SRC-015**: The capture helper shall close a clip after `--silence-ms` of silence and at `--max-clip-ms`, shall not post a clip without a sample above `--silence-level`, shall POST each clip to `--url` (default `http://127.0.0.1:8765/audio?source=desktop`) as `audio/L16` with `rate=16000` and `channels=1`, shall register and keep alive connector `desktop` in Redis (`--redis-url` or `REDIS_URL`, default `redis://127.0.0.1:6379`) as in CONN-DESK-001 and post no ping, shall send the bearer token from `--token` or `EARS_HTTP_TOKEN` when set, and shall retry with increasing delay while ears or Redis is unreachable.
- [D] **EARS-SRC-016**: Where the helper writes frames to `audio.in.desktop` (CONN-DESK-002), ears shall serve `desktop` as a stream connector source (EARS-CON-001), the helper shall stop posting clips, and EARS-SRC-009, EARS-SRC-010 and the clip cutting of EARS-SRC-015 shall no longer apply to it.

## Connector sources

A connector source is a listening source whose audio arrives on `audio.in.<tag>` from a connector (the Discord connector serves `discord`). Design: [ears-design.md § Connector sources](ears-design.md#connector-sources); protocol: [connections-specs.md](../connections/connections-specs.md).

- [x] **EARS-CON-001**: Where an enabled source name is not `http` or `dir`, the ears service shall serve it as a connector source: it shall find the connector through the registry (CONN-REG-006) and, where the registration's `transport` is `stream`, read frames from `audio.in.<tag>` live (CONN-BP-002), or, where it is `http`, take its clips from the listener (EARS-SRC-009); it shall treat the source as an adapter started and stopped independently of the others (EARS-SRC-004). <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CON-002**: The ears service shall create no Discord client, load no `discord.js`, read no `DISCORD_TOKEN`, and join no voice channel. <!-- test: services/ears/test/no-discord.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CON-003**: When a connector source's frames arrive, the ears service shall drop and count a frame whose `rate` or `ch` is not 16000 Hz mono or that is malformed (CONN-AUD-003, CONN-BP-006), shall count missing frames from `seq` gaps (CONN-BP-003) and shall report both counts in its heartbeat detail. <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CON-004**: When a speaker's open utterance is interrupted because the source's connector disappears, the ears service shall close it by the silence rule of EARS-HEAR-001 and transcribe it if it meets `ears.min_speech_ms`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CON-005**: The ears service shall report `deps.<tag>` of a connector source `ok` while a live registration offers `audio-in` for the tag with `ready` true and the ears service is reading its stream (for `transport` `http`, EARS-SRC-012) and `down` otherwise with the detail `<tag> connector not connected`; it shall return to `ok` when the connector returns, without a restart (CONN-ISO-001). <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->


## Audio injection

- [x] **EARS-INJ-005**: When an `inject.request` for ears with `kind` `audio` and an upload id arrives, the ears service shall send the bytes read from Redis unchanged to speech-to-text with the request's `contentType` as hint, without applying `ears.min_speech_ms`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-006**: When an injected clip transcribes to a transcript with at least one word character (a Unicode letter or number), the ears service shall publish `speech.heard` with `speaker` set to `meta.user` or `injected`, `durationMs` from the WAV header (0 for non-WAV), `injected` true and the request's `remember`, followed by `inject.result` with `ok` true and the text as detail. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:27,36,46,91 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-008**: If an injected clip's transcript is empty or contains no word character (a Unicode letter or number), or speech-to-text or the upload read throws, then the ears service shall publish `inject.result` with `ok` false (detail "empty transcript" or the error text) and no `speech.heard`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:54,64 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-009**: Where the ears plan is `off`, or no source is runnable, the ears service shall still handle `inject.request` for ears. <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-010**: The ears service shall handle `inject.request` through the shared inject handler (shared-runtime SHARED-INJ-*) with target `ears`, the single kind `audio`, a byte cap of 26214400 (the dashboard's default audio upload cap) and an upload read timeout of 30000 ms, taking the upload through its bus connection (`Bus.takeUpload`). <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject-limits.test.ts -->

- [x] **EARS-INJ-011**: While a clip from an `inject.request` is being read or transcribed, the ears loop shall already have returned from that request, so the next `inject.request` is read without waiting for speech-to-text. <!-- test: services/ears/test/loop.test.ts -->

## Stub transcriber

- [x] **EARS-STUB-001**: Where `ears.stt_engine` is `stub`, the ears service shall transcribe every captured or injected clip without any network request, returning the text `stub transcript (<n> ms)` where n is the clip's duration (0 when unknown). <!-- test: services/ears/test/stub.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/filter.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-STUB-002**: Where `ears.stt_engine` is `stub`, the ears heartbeat shall report mode `mock` (or `off` when `ears.sources` is empty) and `deps.stt` `n/a`, and the service shall log a startup warning that transcripts are stubbed. <!-- test: services/ears/test/stub.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->

## Health

- [x] **EARS-HLTH-001**: The ears service shall publish heartbeats with `service` `ears`, mode `off` when `ears.sources` is empty, `mock` with the stub engine and `live` otherwise, and `deps` `{discord, stt}` (plus one dependency per enabled source, EARS-SRC-005), where both are `n/a` for the `off` plan. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:7,16,69 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Heartbeat (add-service-health) -->
- [x] **EARS-HLTH-002**: While `discord` is an enabled source, the ears service shall report `deps.discord` `ok` only while the `discord` source is available and delivering (EARS-CON-005) and `down` otherwise. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HLTH-003**: When a speech-to-text call (live or injected) fails, the ears service shall set `deps.stt` to `down`, and when the next call succeeds it shall set it back to `ok`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:69 -->
