## How to accept this Agreement

When you open a pull request, the `cla` check asks every contributor who has
not yet accepted this Agreement to do so. Accept it by posting this exact
comment on the pull request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub username, account id, the pull request and the time of
acceptance are recorded in `.github/cla/signatures.json` on the
`cla-signatures` branch of this repository. That record is public. One
acceptance covers all of your later Contributions.
