# avatar specs

Prefix `AVATAR`. Design: [avatar-design.md](avatar-design.md). `[x]` = implemented, `[ ]` = intended but not yet implemented (gap), `[D]` = deferred. A `<!-- test: ... -->` pointer names the test file; paths are relative to the repo root.

## Bus wiring

- [x] **AVATAR-BUS-001**: When the avatar service has started its engine, the system shall subscribe to the event bus with consumer group `avatar` and the event-bus `latest` start mode (BUS-SUB-010) for `voice.utterance`, `voice.playing`, `voice.done`, `brain.say` and `affect.state`, so that the group is moved to the newest stream entry on every start and events published while avatar was down are not replayed. <!-- openspec: avatar § Bus events consumed --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BUS-002**: When a `brain.say` event arrives, the avatar service shall set the animation engine's emotion to the event's emotion with a 6000 ms hold, before any audio exists. <!-- openspec: avatar § Bus events consumed --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BUS-003**: When a `voice.utterance` event arrives, the avatar service shall register the utterance's `id`, `seq`, emotion, gesture, mouth envelope, envelope rate and duration with the engine, keyed by `id` and `seq`, without starting playback. <!-- openspec: avatar § Bus events consumed --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BUS-004**: When a `voice.playing` event arrives (from any sink; the `sink` field is not examined), the avatar service shall start lip-sync playback of the utterance with that `id` and `seq`. <!-- openspec: avatar § Bus events consumed --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BUS-005**: When a `voice.done` event arrives, the avatar service shall tell the engine that the clause with that `id` and `seq` finished. <!-- openspec: avatar § Bus events consumed --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BUS-006**: When an `affect.state` event arrives, the avatar service shall pass its `mood` to the engine as the resting face. <!-- openspec: avatar § Lip-sync and mood --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BUS-007**: If any avatar service startup step fails, then the service shall end with exit status 1 through the shared bootstrap (SHARED-BOOT-002, SHARED-BOOT-003): a step after the configuration loads (driver creation, expressions load, heartbeat setup, positioning the consumer group) logs the error with message `fatal`, as does a rejecting `driver.connect`; a configuration that does not load logs the loader's message. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BUS-008**: If the avatar service's bus subscription rejects (its consumer group cannot be created or positioned when the subscription starts, for example because Redis is unreachable once the bus's first connect attempt has settled), then the service shall log the failure with message `fatal` and exit non-zero instead of leaving an unhandled rejection. (A failed read after the subscription has started does not reject it: the bus logs it and retries with backoff, BUS-SUB-009.) <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->

## Animation engine

- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-001**: While the engine is started, the system shall compute one animation frame every `1000 / avatar.fps` ms and, in each frame, call `driver.setParams` with the smoothed parameters and report the state to the `onState` callback. <!-- openspec: avatar § Animation loop --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-002**: When the engine's `prepare` is called, the engine shall store the utterance by clause (its `id` and `seq`) and keep at most 50, evicting the least recently prepared clause first; re-preparing a stored clause shall replace its data and make it the most recently prepared, so a re-sent clause is not evicted early. <!-- openspec: avatar § Bus events consumed --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-003**: When the engine's `play` is called with a prepared clause (an `id` and `seq`), the engine shall start lip-sync from that moment, set the utterance's emotion at intensity 1 with the hold of AVATAR-ENG-019, and trigger the utterance's gesture as AVATAR-ENG-021 says. <!-- openspec: avatar § Bus events consumed --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-004**: If the engine's `play` is called with a clause that was never prepared or has been evicted, then the engine shall ignore it. <!-- openspec: avatar § Bus events consumed --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-005**: While an utterance is playing, the engine shall set the mouth-open target to `envelope[floor(elapsedSeconds * envelopeHz)]`, and to 0 once the index is past the end of the envelope. <!-- openspec: avatar § Lip-sync and mood --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-006**: When the envelope index is past the end of the envelope and more than `durationMs + 500` ms have elapsed since playback started, the engine shall clear the playing utterance even if no `done` was received. <!-- openspec: avatar § Lip-sync and mood --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-007**: When the engine's `done` is called with the `id` and `seq` of the playing clause, the engine shall stop playback so that the mouth-open target is 0 from the next frame; when called with any other clause it shall have no effect. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-008**: When the engine's `setEmotion` is called, the engine shall make that emotion current, hold it for the given duration (default 4000 ms) and immediately call `driver.setExpression(emotion, 1)`, or `driver.setExpression("neutral", 0)` for `neutral` (AVATAR-ENG-017). <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-009**: When the engine's `setMood` is called with a mood different from the current mood while no emotion hold is active, the engine shall show the mood immediately via `driver.setExpression` at intensity 0.5, or 0 for `neutral`. <!-- openspec: avatar § Lip-sync and mood --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-010**: While an emotion hold is active, the engine shall store a changed mood without changing the displayed expression until the hold expires; calling `setMood` with the unchanged mood shall do nothing. <!-- openspec: avatar § Lip-sync and mood --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-011**: When an emotion hold expires and the current emotion differs from the mood, the engine shall return to the mood via `driver.setExpression` at intensity 0.5, or 0 for `neutral`. <!-- openspec: avatar § Lip-sync and mood --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-012**: In each frame, the engine shall set the smile target to 0.7 when the current emotion is `happy`, `laugh` or `smug`, and to 0.15 otherwise. <!-- openspec: avatar § Animation loop --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-013**: Where `avatar.idle_motion` is enabled, the engine shall drift head yaw/pitch/roll and eye position with slow sine waves, with head yaw and pitch amplified by 1.6 while an utterance is playing; where it is disabled the engine shall leave head and eye targets at neutral. <!-- openspec: avatar § Animation loop --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-014**: The engine shall blink for 120 ms, the first time about 3 s after construction and thereafter after a random 2.5 to 6 s interval. <!-- openspec: avatar § Animation loop --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-015**: In each frame, the engine shall move every parameter toward its target by `avatar.smoothing`, except mouth-open at `min(1, smoothing * 3)` and blink at 1 (no smoothing). <!-- openspec: avatar § Animation loop --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-016**: In each frame, the engine shall report an intensity of 0 when the current emotion is `neutral`, 1 while an emotion hold is active, and 0.5 otherwise. <!-- openspec: avatar § State publication --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-017**: The engine shall never ask the driver to show `neutral` at an intensity above 0: `neutral`, whether held from a `brain.say` or reached as the resting mood, means no expression, so every driver clears whatever it showed (VTube Studio deactivates the active expression, VMC sends the blendshape at 0). <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-ENG-018**: The engine's frame timer shall not keep the process alive (it is unref'd) and `stop()` shall clear it, so a run that is never stopped, such as a test that fails before its teardown, cannot hold a process open. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/engine.test.ts -->

## Driver contract and selection

- [x] **AVATAR-DRV-001**: The system shall define an avatar driver contract of `name`, `connect`, `disconnect`, `connected`, `setParams` (every frame), `setExpression` (on emotion change) and `trigger` (fire-and-forget gesture), operating on canonical parameters (angles in degrees, other values 0..1, eyes -1..1), with no capability declaration. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts --> <!-- openspec: avatar § Driver contract and selection -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DRV-002**: When the avatar service starts, it shall create the driver named by `avatar.driver` (`vtube-studio`, `vmc`, `browser` or `null`) with that driver's host, port and plugin settings from config (the `browser` driver has none). <!-- openspec: avatar § Driver contract and selection --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DRV-003**: The avatar driver factory shall accept only `vtube-studio`, `vmc`, `browser` and `null`, with no fallback for any other name, because config validation rejects an unrecognised `avatar.driver` before the service starts (AVATAR-DRV-004). <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DRV-004**: If `avatar.driver` holds a value other than `vtube-studio`, `vmc`, `browser` or `null` (written in `config.yaml` or carried by an isolation override), then loading the configuration shall fail with a validation error naming `avatar.driver` before any service starts; an unset or empty `avatar.driver` shall select the null driver (config writes the literal `"null"` and the schema treats a blank driver as `null`; owned by config). <!-- test: packages/config/test/config.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DRV-005**: When the avatar service starts, it shall load `expressions.yaml`, use the block named after the active driver (an empty map when the block is absent), and split its `gestures` sub-map from the emotion-to-expression map. <!-- openspec: avatar § Driver contract and selection --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DRV-006**: When the avatar service connects its driver, it shall pass the emotion map, the gesture map, a logger and the config directory as the driver's state directory. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DRV-007**: The null driver shall report itself connected, ignore parameters, and only log expression and gesture requests. <!-- openspec: avatar § Driver contract and selection --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->

## VTube Studio driver

- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-001**: When the VTube Studio driver connects, it shall open a WebSocket to `vtube_studio.host:port`, authenticate, load the current model's hotkeys, and only then report itself connected. <!-- openspec: avatar § VTube Studio driver --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-002**: While the VTube Studio driver's first connection attempt is in progress or has failed (VTube Studio unreachable, slow, or waiting for the operator to allow the plugin), the avatar service shall already run its engine and bus subscription, the driver's `connect` shall not reject, and the driver shall report itself disconnected until it authenticates. The service starts `driver.connect` without awaiting it (a first-time token request can wait up to 60 s for the operator, and a host that drops packets fails the attempt at the 5 s handshake timeout, AVATAR-VTS-016). <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts (engine and subscription run while connect is pending) --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts (connect resolves on connection refused) -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-003**: When no plugin token is saved in `<config dir>/.vts-token`, the VTube Studio driver shall request one (waiting up to 60 s for the operator to allow it in VTube Studio), save it, and then authenticate with it; when a token is saved it shall authenticate without requesting a new one. <!-- openspec: avatar § VTube Studio driver --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-004**: If VTube Studio rejects the saved token, then the driver shall blank the token file, treat the attempt as failed and close the socket so the next attempt requests a fresh token. <!-- openspec: avatar § VTube Studio driver --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-005**: If any authentication or hotkey-loading request fails (API error, timeout or closed socket), then the driver shall log the error and close the socket. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-006**: If a VTube Studio request receives no reply within its timeout (5 s unless stated), then the driver shall reject that request with a "timed out" error. <!-- openspec: avatar § VTube Studio driver --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.vts.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-007**: If the VTube Studio socket closes while the driver has not been asked to disconnect, then the driver shall mark itself disconnected, reject all pending requests and reconnect after 5 s, repeating every 5 s until connected; a requested `disconnect` shall cancel any pending reconnect. <!-- openspec: avatar § VTube Studio driver --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.vts.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-008**: While the driver is connected, each `setParams` call shall send an `InjectParameterDataRequest` (mode `set`, `faceFound` true) with mouth open/smile, head angle X/Y/Z, eye X/Y for both eyes and eye-open (one minus blink) for both eyes, clamped to the VTube Studio ranges, without waiting for a reply. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-009**: When the driver is asked to show an emotion, it shall take the emotion's mapped name as the target when the intensity is above 0.05 and no expression otherwise; if the target equals the active expression it shall send nothing, otherwise it shall deactivate the active expression (if any), activate the target (if any) and record the target as the active expression. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-010**: When the mapped expression name ends in `.exp3.json`, the driver shall activate or deactivate it with `ExpressionActivationRequest`; for any other name it shall look the name up case-insensitively among the model's hotkeys and send `HotkeyTriggerRequest` for both activation and deactivation (hotkeys toggle). <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-011**: When the driver is asked to trigger a gesture, it shall map it through the gesture map, find the hotkey case-insensitively and trigger it; an unmapped gesture shall be ignored silently and a mapped gesture with no matching hotkey shall log a warning. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-012**: While the driver is not connected (not yet authenticated or after a close), `setParams`, `setExpression` and `trigger` shall send nothing and not throw. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts (before authentication only) -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-013**: When the VTube Studio connection closes or is re-established, the driver shall clear its record of the active expression so that hotkey toggle bookkeeping matches the freshly connected application. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-014**: If a fire-and-forget VTube Studio request (parameter injection, expression activation, gesture trigger) fails or times out, then the driver shall absorb and log the failure (`vts request failed`, at most every 5 s per kind, measured on the monotonic clock) instead of leaving an unhandled promise rejection. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-015**: If an expression's mapped name is not a `.exp3.json` file and matches none of the model's hotkeys, then the VTube Studio driver shall log a warning and send nothing for it. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.vts.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-016**: If the VTube Studio WebSocket handshake has not completed within 5 s, then the driver shall abandon that attempt, report it as a failed connection (the driver stays disconnected) and retry as AVATAR-VTS-007 describes, so a host that drops packets does not wait for the operating system's TCP connect timeout. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.vts.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VTS-017**: When the VTube Studio driver requests or uses a plugin token, it shall identify itself with `avatar.vtube_studio.plugin_name` and `avatar.vtube_studio.plugin_developer` (each default `cohost`) and with nothing derived from `chat.twitch.channel`, so changing the Twitch channel leaves a saved token valid. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [ ] **AVATAR-VTS-018**: The VTube Studio driver shall send `headX`, `headY`, `headZ`, `eyeX` and `eyeY` as `FaceAngleX`, `FaceAngleY`, `FaceAngleZ` and `EyeLeftX/Y` and `EyeRightX/Y` with whatever sign each needs so that a model's face moves in the direction AVATAR-DIR-001 to AVATAR-DIR-005 define, as a viewer of the stream sees it. <!-- manual: needs a real Live2D model in VTube Studio -->

## VMC driver

- [x] **AVATAR-VMC-001**: When the VMC driver connects, it shall open a UDP socket and report itself connected without a handshake; while it is not connected, `setParams` shall send nothing. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VMC-002**: While connected, each `setParams` call shall send to `vmc.host:port`, in order, the head bone rotation as a quaternion, the mouth-open value as blendshapes `A` and `aa`, the blink value as `Blink` and `blink`, the active expression value if any, `/VMC/Ext/Blend/Apply`, and `/VMC/Ext/OK` with int32 1. <!-- openspec: avatar § VMC driver --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VMC-003**: When the VMC driver is asked to show an emotion, it shall clamp the intensity to 0..1, send the previous expression's blendshape at 0 if the mapped name changed, and then include the new expression's blendshape in every following frame. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VMC-004**: The VMC driver shall not support gestures: it shall ignore `trigger`. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VMC-005**: If sending a VMC UDP datagram fails (for example the configured host does not resolve), then the driver shall log the failure (`vmc send failed`, at most every 10 s) and keep running. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VMC-006**: When the VMC driver disconnects, it shall close its UDP socket and report itself disconnected. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-VMC-007**: When the VMC driver computes the head bone quaternion, it shall call `eulerToQuat(headY, headX, headZ)` from `@cohost/shared/head-rotation` (SHARED-ROT-003) and carry no copy of the conversion. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.direction.test.ts -->

## Browser driver (BRW)

- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-001**: Where `avatar.driver` is `browser`, the avatar service shall create the browser driver, whose `name` is `browser` and which takes no host, port or plugin setting from config. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts, services/avatar/test/drivers.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-002**: When the avatar service connects the browser driver, it shall pass in the driver context `frames`, the bus client's `publishAvatarFrame` (BUS-AVF-004), beside the logger, the emotion map, the gesture map and the state directory of AVATAR-DRV-006. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-003**: When the browser driver's `connect` is called, it shall open no connection and resolve at once, and shall never reject. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-004**: When the browser driver's `setParams` is called, it shall offer one avatar frame (BUS-AVF-002) holding the eight canonical parameters of the call rounded to three decimals and otherwise unchanged, the emotion and intensity last given to `setExpression` (`neutral` and 0 before any call) and the `expression` of AVATAR-BRW-006, for writing under AVATAR-BRW-008, so that one frame is offered per Engine tick. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-005**: When the browser driver's `setExpression` is called, it shall record the emotion and intensity for the frames that follow and write nothing. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-006**: While the recorded intensity is above 0.05, the frame's `expression` shall be the value of the recorded emotion in the `browser` block of `expressions.yaml` when the block holds that key (an empty string meaning no expression), else the built-in default of AVATAR-BRW-007; at an intensity of 0.05 or below, and for `neutral`, `expression` shall be the empty string. The names are VRM 1.0 preset names or a custom expression's own name. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-007**: The built-in default expression names shall be `happy` for `happy`, `happy` for `laugh`, `surprised` for `surprised`, `relaxed` for `smug`, `sad` for `sad`, `angry` for `angry`, `sad` for `confused`, and none for `think` and `neutral`. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-008**: While a frame write of the browser driver is in flight, a frame offered by `setParams` shall be dropped without queueing it, and no `setParams` call shall wait for a write, so a slow bus costs frames and never delays or grows the Engine. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-009**: If a frame write fails, then the browser driver shall log `avatar frame publish failed` at most once every 10 s measured on the monotonic clock and keep running. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-010**: The browser driver's `connected()` shall be true from construction, false from a failed frame write until the next successful one, and independent of whether any page is receiving frames; `deps.driver` follows it as AVATAR-HEALTH-003 states. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-011**: When the browser driver's `trigger` is called, it shall do nothing, and the `gestures` map of its block shall be read and ignored. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-012**: When the browser driver's `disconnect` is called, it shall stop writing frames and flush nothing. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-BRW-013**: The shipped `config/expressions.example.yaml` shall hold a `browser` block that maps the nine canonical emotions to the defaults of AVATAR-BRW-007 (an empty string for `think` and `neutral`) and has `gestures: {}`. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/browser.test.ts -->

## Direction of the canonical parameters (DIR)

As a viewer of the stream sees the avatar, whatever app draws it. Design: [avatar-design.md](avatar-design.md) Engine, Direction of the angles.

- [x] **AVATAR-DIR-001**: A positive `headX` shall mean the face turns toward the viewer's left. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.direction.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DIR-002**: A positive `headY` shall mean the face tips down, the chin toward the chest. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.direction.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DIR-003**: A positive `headZ` shall mean the top of the head tilts toward the viewer's right. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.direction.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DIR-004**: A positive `eyeX` shall mean the gaze moves toward the viewer's right. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.direction.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DIR-005**: A positive `eyeY` shall mean the gaze moves up. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/drivers.direction.test.ts -->

## State publication

- [x] **AVATAR-STATE-001**: While the engine is running, the avatar service shall publish `avatar.state` carrying every canonical parameter (`mouthOpen`, `mouthSmile`, `headX`, `headY`, `headZ`, `eyeX`, `eyeY`, `blink`), `emotion`, `intensity`, the driver name and the driver's connected flag, at most once every 200 ms. `mouthSmile` reaches the dashboard preview because the `avatar.state` schema carries it (`packages/bus/src/events.ts`; BUS-EVT-018). <!-- openspec: avatar § State publication --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/state.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/state.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-STATE-002**: If publishing `avatar.state` fails, then the avatar service shall drop that state, log the failure at most once per interval rather than once per frame, and keep running. The log line is `avatar.state publish failed`, at most once per 10 s measured on the monotonic clock, as is the 200 ms spacing of AVATAR-STATE-001; while the bus's shared connection is down each publish rejects at once (BUS-NAT-CONN-005), so the limit applies from the first state after Redis is lost. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/state.test.ts -->

## Health

- [x] **AVATAR-HEALTH-001**: When the avatar service has selected its driver, started its engine and positioned its consumer group (so it can receive events, without waiting for the driver to connect), it shall mark its `avatar` heartbeat ready, and not before; a driver that is still connecting shows as `deps.driver` `down`, not as an unready service. <!-- openspec: avatar § Heartbeat (add-service-health) --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-HEALTH-002**: Where the null driver is selected, the avatar heartbeat shall report mode `off` and dependency `driver` as `n/a`. <!-- openspec: avatar § Heartbeat (add-service-health) --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/health.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-HEALTH-003**: Where a real driver is selected, the avatar heartbeat shall report mode `live` and dependency `driver` as `ok` while the driver is connected and `down` otherwise (making the service state `degraded`). <!-- openspec: avatar § Heartbeat (add-service-health) --> <!-- test: services/avatar/test/health.test.ts -->
- [x] **AVATAR-HEALTH-004**: When the avatar service receives SIGINT or SIGTERM, it shall publish one `stopping` heartbeat, stop the engine, disconnect the driver, stop the bus and exit with code 0. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/boot.test.ts (stop engine, disconnect, stop bus; signal handling and exit are injected) -->
- [x] **AVATAR-DEV-001**: The avatar service's test files shall be part of the workspace type-check (`tsc -b`), so a type error in a test fails the build. <!-- test: services/avatar/test/typecheck.test.ts -->

## Example driver

Terms used here: *the example driver* is the class `UdpJsonDriver` exported by `examples/avatar-driver/udp-json.ts`; *a message* is one UDP datagram whose payload is one JSON object, UTF-8 encoded.
