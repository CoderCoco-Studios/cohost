## Event Intake (IN)

- [x] **AFF-IN-001**: When the affect service starts, it shall subscribe with consumer group `affect` in start mode `latest` (the group is moved to the stream tail on every start, so lines published while affect was down never nudge the mood) to `chat.message`, `speech.heard`, `vision.summary`, `brain.say` and `control`. <!-- openspec: affect § Event subscriptions and nudges --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/main.test.ts:44 -->
- [x] **AFF-IN-002**: When affect receives a `chat.message`, it shall appraise the line as source `chat` (passing `mentionsCohost`) and set the mood cause to `chat: <user>`. <!-- openspec: affect § Event subscriptions and nudges --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:34 -->
- [x] **AFF-IN-003**: When affect receives a `speech.heard` event, it shall appraise the line as source `speech` with `mention` false (a voice line addressed to the co-host gets no mention boost; speech already has the higher base arousal of AFF-APPR-001) and set the mood cause to `voice: <speaker>`. <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:34 -->
- [x] **AFF-IN-004**: When affect receives a `chat.message` with `isSub` true, it shall additionally nudge valence by +0.05 and arousal by +0.05 with cause `sub: <user>`. <!-- openspec: affect § Event subscriptions and nudges --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:45 -->
- [x] **AFF-IN-005**: When affect receives a `vision.summary` with `changed` true, it shall nudge arousal by +0.08 with cause `screen changed`; when `changed` is false it shall not change the mood. <!-- openspec: affect § Event subscriptions and nudges --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:45 -->
- [x] **AFF-IN-006**: When affect receives a `brain.say`, it shall nudge valence by +0.08 for emotion `happy`, `laugh` or `smug`, by -0.08 for `sad` or `angry`, by 0 for any other emotion, and arousal by +0.05, with cause `said: <emotion>`. <!-- openspec: affect § Event subscriptions and nudges --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:45 -->
- [x] **AFF-IN-007**: When affect receives `control` `forget_context` addressed to it, it shall apply the pending decay first and then set valence to `baseline_valence` and arousal to the resting arousal (AFF-MOOD-006) with cause `reset`, so the mood lands exactly on baseline. <!-- openspec: affect § Event subscriptions and nudges --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:68 --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:159 -->

## Mood Model (MOOD)

- [x] **AFF-MOOD-001**: The affect mood shall start at valence `brain.affect.baseline_valence` (default 0.15) and at the resting arousal (AFF-MOOD-006, default 0.1). <!-- openspec: affect § Mood model and decay --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.test.ts:49 -->
- [x] **AFF-MOOD-002**: When the mood is nudged, affect shall clamp valence to [-1, 1] and arousal to [0, 1]. <!-- openspec: affect § Mood model and decay --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.test.ts:34 -->
- [x] **AFF-MOOD-003**: When time elapses, affect shall decay valence toward `baseline_valence` and arousal toward the resting arousal (AFF-MOOD-006) exponentially, halving the distance every `brain.affect.half_life_ms` (default 45000), applying the decay lazily whenever the mood is nudged, appraised or published. <!-- openspec: affect § Mood model and decay --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.test.ts:34 -->
- [x] **AFF-MOOD-004**: When affect labels a mood, it shall map arousal above 0.6 to `laugh` (valence above 0.3), `angry` (valence below -0.3) or `surprised`; arousal above 0.3 to `happy` (valence above 0.2), `confused` (valence below -0.2) or `think`; otherwise to `smug` (valence above 0.35), `sad` (valence below -0.3) or `neutral`. <!-- openspec: affect § Mood label --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.test.ts:22 -->
- [x] **AFF-MOOD-005**: When affect reports its state, it shall round valence and arousal to 3 decimals and include the label and the most recent cause. <!-- openspec: affect § Publishing affect.state --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.test.ts:49 -->
- [x] **AFF-MOOD-006**: The affect service shall take its resting arousal from `brain.affect.baseline_arousal` (0..1, default 0.1) and use it as the initial arousal, the decay target and the `forget_context` reset value. <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:179 -->

## Rule Appraisal (APPR)

- [x] **AFF-APPR-001**: When the rules appraiser appraises a line, it shall start from an arousal delta of +0.15 for speech or +0.03 for chat, and add +0.2 arousal when the line mentions the co-host. <!-- openspec: affect § Rule-based appraisal --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.test.ts:11 -->
- [x] **AFF-APPR-002**: When a line contains a whole word from the positive list (including the single-letter stream slang `w`) or a positive emoji, the rules appraiser shall add +0.12 valence and +0.05 arousal; when it contains a whole word from the negative list (including the single-letter slang `l` and `f`) or a negative emoji, it shall add -0.15 valence and +0.08 arousal; when both match the deltas net together. <!-- openspec: affect § Rule-based appraisal --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/appraiser.test.ts:47 -->
- [x] **AFF-APPR-003**: When a line contains two or more consecutive `!` or five or more consecutive ASCII capital letters (`A`-`Z`), the rules appraiser shall add +0.05 arousal; when it contains `?` it shall add +0.03 arousal. <!-- openspec: affect § Rule-based appraisal --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.test.ts:11 -->
- [x] **AFF-APPR-004**: When a line contains a single emoji, the rules appraiser shall match it against exactly one of the positive or negative lists, and a bare variation selector shall match neither. <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.test.ts:58 -->

## Appraiser Hook and Queue (HOOK)

- [x] **AFF-HOOK-001**: The affect service shall build its appraiser as module `affect.appraiser` from `brain.affect.module` with kind `rules`, `llm` or `http`. <!-- openspec: affect § Appraiser module kinds and queueing --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/appraiser.test.ts:31 -->
- [x] **AFF-HOOK-002**: Where the appraiser kind is `llm` or `http`, affect shall accept the module output only if both `valence_delta` and `arousal_delta` are finite numbers, clamping each to [-0.5, 0.5]. <!-- openspec: affect § Appraiser module kinds and queueing --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/appraiser.test.ts:31 -->
- [x] **AFF-HOOK-003**: If the llm or http appraiser fails or returns output that violates the deltas contract, then affect shall appraise the line with the rules (fallback implemented by the module in region-hooks). <!-- openspec: affect § Appraiser module kinds and queueing --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/appraiser.test.ts:39 -->
- [x] **AFF-HOOK-004**: Where the appraiser kind is `rules`, affect shall apply the appraisal inline in the event handler without queueing. <!-- openspec: affect § Appraiser module kinds and queueing --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:34 -->
- [x] **AFF-HOOK-005**: Where the appraiser kind is `llm` or `http`, affect shall queue each line to a single worker that appraises one line at a time, so the bus handler never waits on the module, and shall appraise a queued line against the mood current when the job runs. <!-- openspec: affect § Appraiser module kinds and queueing --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:97 -->
- [x] **AFF-HOOK-006**: While more than 8 appraisal jobs are waiting for an llm or http appraiser, affect shall discard the oldest waiting jobs. <!-- openspec: affect § Appraiser module kinds and queueing --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:124 -->
- [x] **AFF-HOOK-007**: Where the appraiser kind is `llm` or `http`, affect shall appraise a line shorter than 4 characters (after trimming) that does not mention the co-host with the rules inline, and shall always send a mention to the module. <!-- openspec: affect § Appraiser module kinds and queueing --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:113 -->
- [x] **AFF-HOOK-008**: When affect handles `control` `forget_context` addressed to it, it shall discard every queued llm/http appraisal job; while affect is paused, the appraisal worker shall drop each queued job without applying it; so no job queued before a pause or reset changes the mood afterwards. <!-- openspec: affect § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:200 -->
- [x] **AFF-HOOK-009**: Where the appraiser kind is `llm`, affect shall send a JSON-only system prompt naming the co-host and the streamer that asks for `{"valence_delta":n,"arousal_delta":n}` with each delta within -0.5..0.5 (the validator range of AFF-HOOK-002), and a user prompt carrying the current valence and arousal and the line with its source, mention marker and speaker, capped at 60 output tokens. <!-- test: services/affect/test/appraiser.test.ts:66 -->

## Publishing (PUB)

- [x] **AFF-PUB-001**: While the affect service is running, it shall evaluate whether to publish `affect.state` every `brain.affect.publish_ms` (default 2000); the first evaluation after start shall publish. <!-- openspec: affect § Publishing affect.state --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:77 --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/main.test.ts:67 -->
- [x] **AFF-PUB-002**: When the publish interval fires and the key of mood label plus 2-decimal valence and arousal equals the last published key and less than 15 seconds have passed, affect shall not publish. <!-- openspec: affect § Publishing affect.state --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:77 -->
- [x] **AFF-PUB-003**: When the state key has changed, or 15 seconds have passed since the last publish, affect shall publish `affect.state` with `valence`, `arousal`, `mood` and `cause`. <!-- openspec: affect § Publishing affect.state --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:77 -->
- [x] **AFF-PUB-004**: If publishing `affect.state` fails, then the affect service shall log the failure and continue. <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:232 -->

## Control (CTRL)

- [x] **AFF-CTRL-001**: If a `control` event carries a `target` other than `affect`, then affect shall ignore it; an absent target addresses all services. <!-- openspec: affect § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:133 -->
- [x] **AFF-CTRL-002**: While affect is paused by `control` `pause`, it shall not nudge the mood from any non-control event. <!-- openspec: affect § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:133 -->
- [x] **AFF-CTRL-003**: While affect is paused, it shall continue to decay the mood and publish `affect.state`, so consumers keep a live, decaying mood. <!-- openspec: affect § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:150 -->
- [x] **AFF-CTRL-004**: When affect receives `control` `resume` addressed to it, it shall resume nudging the mood in-process without restarting. <!-- openspec: affect § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/affect.region.test.ts:133 -->
- [x] **AFF-CTRL-005**: The affect service shall hold its mood and paused flag in process memory only, so a restarted affect starts from the baseline mood and unpaused.

## Health and Lifecycle (HEALTH)

- [x] **AFF-HEALTH-001**: The affect service shall publish `service.heartbeat` with `service` `affect`, `mode` `live` and empty `deps` via `startHeartbeat`, emitting `starting` at boot, `ready` once its consumer group has been created and the publish timer set, and one `stopping` on graceful shutdown. <!-- openspec: affect § Heartbeat --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/health.test.ts:6 --> <!-- test: services/affect/test/main.test.ts:44 -->
- [x] **AFF-HEALTH-002**: If any affect startup step fails (loading config, constructing the bus, heartbeat, appraiser or `Affect`, or starting the subscription), then the service shall log the error with message `fatal` and exit with code 1. <!-- test: services/affect/test/main.test.ts:87 -->
