# ears specs

Segment `ears` (prefix `EARS`). LLD: [ears-design.md](ears-design.md). Markers: `[x]` implemented, `[ ]` intended but not yet implemented (gap), `[D]` deferred. Ears holds no Discord client and consumes connector sources ([connections](../connections/connections-specs.md)).

The playback sink and the spoken fallback line belong to [mouth](../mouth/mouth-specs.md).

## Startup plans

- [x] **EARS-START-001**: When `ears.sources` is empty (heartbeat mode `off`), the ears service shall create no Discord client, require no Discord environment variables, publish no `speech.heard` other than injected ones, and stay running idle with heartbeat mode `off` and state `ready`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts --> <!-- openspec: ears § Off source -->
- [x] **EARS-START-003**: The ears service shall subscribe its consumer group `ears` to `inject.request` in every startup plan, and to `voice.playing` and `voice.done` in addition only as EARS-ECHO-001 says, and shall publish no `voice.playing`, `voice.done` or `voice.failed`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts --> <!-- openspec: ears § Off source -->
- [x] **EARS-START-004**: The ears service shall choose its startup plan with the pure function `earsPlan(sources, hasDirPath)`, which for a single enabled source returns `off` for no source, the tag itself for a connector source, and the plans of EARS-CAP-004 for `http` and `dir`, and shall create no Discord client and load no `discord.js` whatever the sources (EARS-CON-002). <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/no-discord.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-START-005**: When the process receives a shutdown signal, the ears service shall, in the `off` plan, clear the idle warning timer, stop the bus and exit with code 0 (the sequence with sources is EARS-SRC-004). <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-START-006**: The ears service shall subscribe its consumer group `ears` with start mode `latest`, so the group is reset to the newest entry on every start and no `inject.request` published while ears was down is handled. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts -->

- [x] **EARS-START-008**: The ears service shall start through the shared service bootstrap (`runService`, SHARED-BOOT-*) and shall run its `off` and `no-dir` plans through the shared idle plan (`startIdle`, SHARED-IDLE-*), with the idle reason text of EARS-CAP-010. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->


## Listening and transcription

- [x] **EARS-HEAR-001**: When `audio.in.<source>` frames for a speaker id (`spk`) arrive from a connector source, the ears service shall collect them into one utterance buffer per `spk`, close the utterance when no frame from that `spk` has arrived for `ears.silence_ms` (default 700), and hand it to transcription labelled with the frames' `name` (the `spk` when empty) and `speakerId` set to `spk`; it shall hold at most one open buffer per `spk`. Excluding bot accounts is the Discord connector's (CONN-DISC-007). <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-002**: When a speaker's utterance is closed (EARS-HEAR-001), the ears service shall treat the collected 16 kHz mono PCM as a clip for `Hearing.heardClip` with the source tag of the stream, with no further conversion. <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/connector-source.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-004**: When a captured utterance is shorter than `ears.min_speech_ms` (default 400), the ears service shall neither call speech-to-text nor publish anything. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-005**: When a captured utterance is long enough and the batch engine is `speaches`, the ears service shall convert it to 16 kHz mono WAV and POST it as multipart form data to `<STT_URL>/v1/audio/transcriptions` with fields `file`, `model` set to `ears.stt_model` (default `Systran/faster-whisper-small`), `language` set to `en`, and `response_format` set to `json`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/engines.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-007**: If the transcript is empty or contains no word character (a Unicode letter or number), then the ears service shall publish nothing. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-006**: When the transcript contains at least one word character (a Unicode letter or number, in any script) and is not a drop phrase (EARS-HEAR-010), the ears service shall publish `speech.heard` with `speaker`, `speakerId`, `text` and `durationMs` (rounded to whole milliseconds). <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-011**: The ears service shall publish `speech.heard` `speakerId` as the speaker id (`spk`) the source gave, unmodified (for Discord, the user id), so downstream services can tell two people with the same display name apart; it shall keep no identity mapping of its own. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts -->
- [ ] **EARS-HEAR-012**: When the ears service publishes `speech.heard`, it shall set `timing.audioEndedAt` to the epoch-millisecond time the last frame classified as speech of a connector utterance arrived under `ears.vad.engine` `silero`, or the last frame of the utterance received under `gap`, or the time ears received the clip (`http` or `dir` capture, injected audio), and for an utterance closed by an end-of-turn signal as EARS-KYU-010 says, not the time the utterance was closed or transcribed.
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-008**: If speech-to-text fails (non-2xx response or thrown error), then the ears service shall log the warning "transcription failed", publish no event, and keep running. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-010**: When a live captured utterance transcribes to a transcript that, compared case-insensitively after trimming whitespace and every leading and trailing non-letter, non-number character and collapsing inner whitespace, equals an entry of `ears.drop_phrases` (default empty, compared the same way), the ears service shall publish nothing for it and log it at debug level. Injected clips are not filtered. <!-- test: services/ears/test/filter.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/transcript.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-009**: The ears service shall not subscribe to `control`: it publishes `speech.heard` regardless of `mute` or `pause`, which cortex and salience enforce upstream. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts -->

## Endpointing (VAD)

Design: [ears-design.md § Utterance endpointing](ears-design.md#utterance-endpointing). These specs apply to connector sources served from `audio.in.<tag>` frames (EARS-CON-001); sources that deliver whole clips (`http`, `dir`, a connector with `transport` `http`) and injection are unaffected.

- [x] **EARS-VAD-001**: Where a connector source is served from frames, the ears service shall cut utterances by the rule `ears.vad.engine` names (`silero`, the default, or `gap`), where `gap` is the frame-gap rule of EARS-HEAR-001 alone and `silero` is EARS-VAD-002 to EARS-VAD-004 in addition to it.
- [x] **EARS-VAD-002**: Where `ears.vad.engine` is `silero`, the ears service shall classify each speaker's frames with a speech-activity detector (a frame is speech when its speech probability is at least `ears.vad.threshold`, default 0.5) and shall close the speaker's open utterance when `ears.silence_ms` (default 700) of received audio has passed since the last frame classified as speech.
- [x] **EARS-VAD-003**: Where `ears.vad.engine` is `silero`, the ears service shall open an utterance for a speaker at the first frame classified as speech, shall include in it the up to 200 ms of audio that arrived just before that frame, shall buffer no other frame of a speaker with no open utterance, and shall hand to transcription only the audio up to and including the last frame classified as speech (the clip of EARS-HEAR-002 is that collected audio), so the trailing silence that closed the utterance is not transcribed; an utterance closed by an end-of-turn signal ends at the frame of EARS-KYU-010 instead. <!-- test: services/ears/test/endpointing.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-VAD-004**: Where `ears.vad.engine` is `silero`, the frame-gap rule of EARS-HEAR-001 shall still close an open utterance when no frame from its speaker has arrived for `ears.silence_ms`, so a stalled source or a connector that disappears (EARS-CON-004) cannot hold an utterance open.
- [x] **EARS-VAD-005**: When the audio buffered for a speaker's open utterance reaches `ears.max_utterance_ms` (a positive integer, default 15000), the ears service shall close that utterance and hand it to transcription whichever engine is set, and shall open a new utterance, with a new `utteranceId` (EARS-PART-003), at that speaker's next frame (at the next frame classified as speech under `silero`).
- [x] **EARS-VAD-006**: Where `ears.vad.engine` is `silero` and the detector cannot be loaded when a connector source starts, the ears service shall mark that source's dependency `down` with a detail that names the source and carries `vad unavailable: <reason>` (`ears <tag> source failed to start: Error: vad unavailable: <reason>`), shall keep every other source running (EARS-SRC-004), and shall not fall back to `gap`.
- [x] **EARS-VAD-007**: The ears service shall keep the utterance buffer, the detector state and the partial-transcript state (EARS-PART-*) of each speaker id (`spk`) of a source separately, so two speakers on one source never share an utterance, an `utteranceId` or a `seq`.
- [x] **EARS-VAD-008**: When frames of a connector source are missing (`seq` gaps, EARS-CON-003), the ears service shall neither close nor reset any open utterance, detector state or partial-transcript state because of the gap, and shall transcribe the frames it received without filling the gap.

## Partial transcripts (PART)

Design: [ears-design.md § Partial transcripts](ears-design.md#partial-transcripts). `speech.heard` remains the one final event (EARS-HEAR-006); a partial is never published as `speech.heard`.

- [x] **EARS-PART-001**: The ears service shall read `ears.partials` (`on` or `off`; default `off`), `ears.stt_gpu` (a boolean, default false, declaring that a batch speech-to-text backend runs on a GPU; it gates only the warning of EARS-PART-012) and `ears.partial_step_ms` (a positive integer, default 1000: the step of EARS-PART-004 for a batch engine and the pacing window of EARS-PART-017 for a streaming engine), and shall publish partials only while `ears.partials` is `on`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-002**: The ears service shall publish partials only for utterances of connector sources served from frames; for a clip from `http`, `dir` or a connector served over `http`, and for injected audio, it shall publish no `speech.partial`.
- [x] **EARS-PART-003**: When the ears service opens an utterance, or receives a whole clip or an injected clip, it shall mint a string `utteranceId` unique to it and shall publish that `utteranceId` on every `speech.partial` and on the `speech.heard` of that utterance.
- [x] **EARS-PART-004**: Where the selected engine is a batch engine, while partials are effective and a speaker's utterance is open and has at least `ears.min_speech_ms` of audio, the ears service shall, every `ears.partial_step_ms`, transcribe the audio of that utterance received so far up to the last frame classified as speech, as one 16 kHz mono WAV with the batch engine's request (EARS-HEAR-005 for `speaches`, EARS-ENG-006 for `openai`). <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-005**: Where the selected engine is a batch engine, when two consecutive successful partial transcriptions of an utterance have a common prefix of words (words compared as in EARS-HEAR-010: case-insensitive, leading and trailing non-letter, non-number characters ignored), the ears service shall take that prefix, in the wording of the later transcription, as the committed text, and shall publish `speech.partial` only when the committed text has more words than the last partial published for that utterance. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-006**: When the ears service publishes `speech.partial`, it shall set `speaker`, `speakerId` (the id the source gave, as in EARS-HEAR-011) and `source` as for `speech.heard`, `text` to the committed text (batch engine) or the session's hypothesis (streaming engine), `utteranceId`, `seq` to 0 for the first partial of the utterance and one more than the previous for each later one, and `timing.audioEndedAt` to the time the last frame in the transcribed (batch) or sent (streaming) audio arrived. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-007**: The ears service shall apply the word-character and drop-phrase filters of EARS-SRC-007 to the committed text of a partial and shall publish nothing for a committed text that fails them, and shall publish no partial for an utterance shorter than `ears.min_speech_ms`.
- [x] **EARS-PART-008**: While a partial transcription of an utterance is in flight, the ears service shall start no further one for that utterance, and a step due in that time shall be skipped, not queued.
- [ ] **EARS-PART-009**: When an utterance closes, the ears service shall, except where EARS-EARLY-016 reuses an early transcript, transcribe it for its `speech.heard` without waiting for a partial transcription in flight, and shall publish no `speech.partial` for that utterance afterwards: a partial result that returns after the close, or after the utterance's `speech.heard`, shall be discarded.
- [x] **EARS-PART-010**: If a partial transcription fails (non-2xx response or thrown error), then the ears service shall log it, publish nothing for that step, forget the earlier transcriptions of that utterance so that agreement needs two new successful ones, leave `deps.stt` unchanged, and let the utterance and its final transcription proceed.
- [x] **EARS-PART-011**: Where `ears.stt_engine` is `stub`, the ears service shall publish no `speech.partial` whatever `ears.partials` says, and shall log a startup warning when `ears.partials` is `on`.
- [x] **EARS-PART-012**: Where `ears.partials` is `on`, the selected engine is a batch engine other than `stub` and `ears.stt_gpu` is false, the ears service shall publish partials as in EARS-PART-004 to EARS-PART-010 and shall log a startup warning that partial transcription may not keep up on a speech-to-text backend that is not on a GPU. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-013**: When a source stops, or its open utterance is closed by the disappearance of its connector, the ears service shall cancel that utterance's partial steps and discard any partial transcription still in flight, and shall publish no `speech.partial` for it afterwards.
- [x] **EARS-PART-014**: If publishing a `speech.partial` fails, then the ears service shall log it, shall not consume that `seq` (the next partial of the utterance carries it), and shall let the utterance and its final transcription proceed.
- [D] **EARS-PART-015**: Where several speakers' utterances are open at once, the ears service shall limit the partial transcriptions in flight across the whole service to a configured number, skipping the steps beyond it; deferred until partial load from several speakers is measured (EARS-PART-008 limits only one utterance).
- [x] **EARS-PART-016**: Where the selected engine is a streaming engine and `ears.partials` is `on`, the ears service shall publish `speech.partial` events from the current hypothesis of an utterance's session, with `text` set to that hypothesis, through the word-character and drop-phrase filters of EARS-SRC-007. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-017**: When the hypothesis of a streaming session changes, the ears service shall publish the first change of the utterance at once, then at most one `speech.partial` per `ears.partial_step_ms`, and when that window ends it shall publish the latest change not yet published. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-018**: Where the selected engine is a streaming engine, the ears service shall publish no `speech.partial` for an utterance before it has `ears.min_speech_ms` of audio. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-019**: Where the selected engine is a streaming engine, the ears service shall publish a `speech.partial` only when the hypothesis differs from the last one published for that utterance, and shall publish a hypothesis that has fewer words than the previous one. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-020**: When a streaming utterance closes, the ears service shall discard a hypothesis that arrives afterwards and publish no `speech.partial` for that utterance after its close. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-021**: Where the selected engine is a streaming engine, the ears service shall publish no `speech.partial` for an utterance that has no session (EARS-SES-005, EARS-SES-008, EARS-SES-010). <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-PART-022**: Where the selected engine is a streaming engine, the ears service shall make no extra speech-to-text call for a partial and shall log no `ears.stt_gpu` warning. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts -->

## Early transcripts (EARLY)

Design: [ears-design.md § Early transcripts](ears-design.md#early-transcripts). These specs apply where `ears.early.enabled` is true, to connector sources served from `audio.in.<tag>` frames; EARS-EARLY-020 and EARS-EARLY-021 apply to every such utterance, whatever `ears.early.enabled` says.

- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-001**: Where `ears.early.enabled` is true and the selected engine is a batch engine, when an utterance of a connector source served from frames is open and `ears.early.after_ms` (default 250) milliseconds have passed since the speaker's last frame classified as speech (`ears.vad.engine` `silero`) or since the speaker's last frame (`gap`), the ears service shall take one early snapshot of that utterance: a transcription of its audio so far that leaves the utterance open.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-002**: The ears service shall take no early snapshot of an utterance that holds less than `ears.min_speech_ms` of audio.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-003**: While an early transcription of an utterance is in flight, or a published early transcript of it is unresolved (neither retracted nor reused as the final), the ears service shall take no further early snapshot of that utterance.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-004**: The ears service shall take at most `ears.early.max_per_utterance` (default 2) early snapshots of one utterance, counting every snapshot whether or not it published a `speech.early`.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-005**: When the ears service takes an early snapshot, it shall send the batch engine the clip of the utterance up to its last frame classified as speech (up to its last frame under `gap`), the clip a final would send, and shall bound the call by `ears.stt_timeout_ms`.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-006**: When an early transcription returns text that is empty, has no word character (a Unicode letter or number), or equals an `ears.drop_phrases` entry as EARS-HEAR-010 compares, or fails, the ears service shall publish no `speech.early` and shall let the utterance close in the ordinary way.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-007**: If an early transcription fails, then the ears service shall leave `deps.stt` unchanged.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-008**: The ears service shall take no early snapshot of an utterance of a source named in `ears.echo_suppress_sources`, of a clip of the `http` or `dir` source, or of injected audio.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-009**: Where `ears.early.enabled` is true and the selected engine is a streaming engine (`nemotron` or `kyutai`), the ears service shall take no early snapshot and publish no `speech.early` or `speech.retracted`, and the config load shall not fail.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-010**: Where `ears.early.enabled` is true and the selected engine is a streaming engine, the ears service shall log one warning at start that names `ears.early.enabled` and the selected engine and says that early reply is off because that engine is streaming.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-011**: Where `ears.early.enabled` is true and the selected engine is a streaming engine, the ears heartbeat detail shall contain the text `early reply off: streaming engine`.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-012**: When an early transcription returns text that passes EARS-EARLY-006, the ears service shall publish `speech.early` with `speaker`, `speakerId` (as EARS-HEAR-011) and `source` as for `speech.heard`, `text`, `utteranceId` equal to the id the utterance's final carries (EARS-PART-003), an integer `seq` equal to the number of `speech.early` events already published for that utterance (0 for the first), an integer `sttMs` equal to the duration in milliseconds of that transcription, and `timing.audioEndedAt` equal to the arrival time of the utterance's last frame classified as speech (its last frame under `gap`).
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-013**: When a frame of the same speaker (a frame classified as speech under `silero`, any frame under `gap`) arrives while the utterance is open and a `speech.early` of it is unresolved, the ears service shall publish one `speech.retracted` with `speaker`, `speakerId`, `source`, `utteranceId` and the `seq` of that `speech.early`, before it publishes any later event of that utterance.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-014**: When a frame of the same speaker (a frame classified as speech under `silero`, any frame under `gap`) arrives while an early transcription of the utterance is in flight, the ears service shall discard that transcription's result and publish no `speech.early`, no `speech.retracted` and no final `speech.heard` from it.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-015**: When a `speech.retracted` has been published for an utterance, the ears service shall keep the utterance open as one utterance with the same `utteranceId`, buffer, detector state and partial-transcript state, shall allow a further early snapshot within EARS-EARLY-004, and shall never reuse the retracted early transcript as a final.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-016**: When an utterance closes other than by `ears.max_utterance_ms` (EARS-EARLY-025) with no speech after its last published `speech.early` that has not been retracted, the ears service shall publish `speech.heard` with that early transcript's `text`, the same `utteranceId`, `earlySeq` equal to its `seq` and `earlySttMs` equal to its `sttMs`, and shall send no transcription request for it.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-017**: When the ears service publishes a `speech.heard` that reuses an early transcript, it shall set `timing.audioEndedAt` to the value its `speech.early` carried and `timing.closedAt` to the time it closed the utterance.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-018**: When an utterance closes while an early transcription of it is in flight and was not discarded (EARS-EARLY-014), the ears service shall wait for that call, bounded by `ears.stt_timeout_ms`, and shall pass its result through the filters of EARS-EARLY-006; a result that passes shall be published as the final `speech.heard` with `earlyLate` true and no `earlySeq`, with no `speech.early`, and a result that is filtered shall publish nothing.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-019**: If the early transcription that an utterance waited for at its close fails, then the ears service shall transcribe the utterance's clip once and publish the result as an ordinary final `speech.heard`.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-020**: When the ears service publishes `speech.heard` for an utterance it closed itself (a connector utterance served from frames), it shall set `timing.closedAt` to the epoch-millisecond time it closed the utterance (the silence rule, the frame-gap rule, the length cap or a Kyutai end of turn firing), and for a clip of `http` or `dir` and for injected audio it shall set no `closedAt`.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-021**: When the ears service publishes `speech.heard` for a connector utterance served from frames, ordinary or reusing an early transcript, and the utterance was not closed by a Kyutai end of turn (which EARS-KYU-010 governs), it shall set `timing.audioEndedAt` to the arrival time of the utterance's last frame classified as speech under `silero`, and of its last frame under `gap`.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-022**: Where `ears.early.enabled` is true and the selected engine is a batch engine, the ears heartbeat detail shall contain the text `early: <published> published, <retracted> retracted, <reused> reused, <late> late` with four integers counted since the service started.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-023**: The ears service shall count an early transcript as published when it publishes its `speech.early`, as retracted when it publishes its `speech.retracted`, as reused when it publishes a `speech.heard` with `earlySeq`, and as late when an utterance closes while its early transcription is still in flight, whatever the outcome of that transcription.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-024**: The ears service shall publish `speech.early` and `speech.retracted` with no injection flags.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-025**: When an utterance closes by `ears.max_utterance_ms` (EARS-VAD-005), the ears service shall transcribe its whole clip and publish `speech.heard` with neither `earlySeq` nor `earlyLate`.
- [ ] **EARS-EARLY-026**: After the ears service has taken an early snapshot of an utterance, whether it published, was filtered or failed, it shall take no further early snapshot of that utterance until a frame classified as speech (any frame under `gap`) has arrived since.

## Capture sources

- [x] **EARS-CAP-001**: The ears service shall read each clip from a capture source through the capture contract (`CaptureAdapter`, `decodeClip`) and pass it to `Hearing.heardClip`, which shall resample it to 16 kHz mono, drop it when shorter than `ears.min_speech_ms`, transcribe it, drop a transcript with no word character or equal to an `ears.drop_phrases` entry, and otherwise publish `speech.heard` with the given `speaker`, `text` and `durationMs`, without `injected` and without `speakerId`, returning the outcome (`heard`, `too-short`, `empty`, `drop-phrase`, `echo` or `stt-error`). <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-002**: The ears service shall decode an uploaded clip with the shared `parseWav` when its content type is `audio/wav`, `audio/x-wav` or `audio/wave`, and as raw little-endian 16-bit PCM when it is `audio/L16` or `audio/pcm` with `rate` and `channels` parameters defaulting to 16000 and 1, and shall refuse any other type and any audio that does not decode (including non-16-bit WAV). <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-003**: The ears service shall create no Discord client, load no `discord.js` and require no Discord environment variable in any plan, shall subscribe its consumer group `ears` to `inject.request` and, only where EARS-ECHO-001 applies, to `voice.playing` and `voice.done` (EARS-START-003, EARS-START-006) and shall handle injection whatever the sources (EARS-INJ-009). <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/no-discord.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-004**: For a single enabled source the ears service shall choose its plan with `earsPlan(sources, hasDirPath)` returning `http` for source `http`, `dir` for source `dir`, `no-dir` for source `dir` with an empty `ears.dir.path`, and the plans of EARS-START-004 otherwise. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-005**: Where the ears plan is `http`, when a `POST /audio` carries a decodable clip, the ears service shall pass it to `Hearing.heardClip` with the `speaker` query parameter (default `http`) and answer 200 with JSON `{ heard, text?, reason? }` (`reason` `echo` for a clip dropped by the echo gate) after the clip has been processed, and shall answer 404 for another path, 405 for another method, 415 for an unsupported content type, 400 for audio that does not decode and 502 when speech-to-text failed. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-006**: The ears `http` source shall refuse with 413 a body larger than `INJECT_MAX_BYTES` without buffering past that cap, cut off a body that takes longer than `INJECT_READ_TIMEOUT_MS`, process one clip at a time, and refuse with 503 a request that arrives while one clip is being processed and `INJECT_QUEUE_LIMIT` (shared-runtime SHARED-INJ-010) others are waiting. `INJECT_MAX_BYTES` and `INJECT_READ_TIMEOUT_MS` are the limits of EARS-INJ-010. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-007**: Where the ears plan is `http`, the ears service shall listen on `ears.http.host` and `ears.http.port`, report the heartbeat `ready` once it is listening, and, when the port cannot be bound, stay running with the heartbeat `degraded` and `deps.http` `down`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts, services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->

## Streaming session (SES)

Design: [ears-design.md § STT engines](ears-design.md#stt-engines) (Where a session lives, When a session cannot serve).

- [x] **EARS-SES-001**: Where the selected engine is a streaming engine, when an utterance of a connector source served from frames opens, the ears service shall open one session for it, one per speaker id of the source. <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-002**: When a session is open for an utterance, the ears service shall send the pre-roll and the frames buffered while the session was connecting first and in order, as fast as the connection takes them, and then every frame as it arrives, including silence frames of the open utterance. <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-003**: If the buffered start of an utterance is not accepted within `ears.stt_timeout_ms` (which also covers a server that cannot take audio faster than real time), then the ears service shall treat the session as failed (EARS-SES-010). <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-004**: When an utterance with an open session closes and passes the minimum-duration check, the ears service shall take the final text from the session's `finish()`, bounded by `ears.stt_timeout_ms`, apply the word-character and drop-phrase checks as for any clip, and publish `speech.heard` with the utterance's `utteranceId` and `timing.audioEndedAt` (EARS-HEAR-012). <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-005**: Where a source is named in `ears.echo_suppress_sources`, the ears service shall open no streaming session for its utterances, shall hold each until it closes, shall let the echo gate decide (EARS-ECHO-004), and shall send an utterance the gate lets through to the batch engine, so no audio of that source reaches a streaming engine. <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-006**: When an utterance with an open session is dropped at close for being shorter than `ears.min_speech_ms`, the ears service shall abort its session without calling `finish()` and publish nothing. <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-007**: When a source stops, the ears service shall abort the open sessions of that source; a session shall not outlive its utterance. <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-008**: The ears service shall keep at most `ears.max_stt_sessions` (an integer of at least 1, default 2) sessions open at once; an utterance that opens while the limit is reached shall open no session, run unstreamed and go to the batch engine when it closes. <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-009**: If a server answers that it has no free channel, then the ears service shall treat it as a capacity refusal of that utterance's session (EARS-SES-010) and leave `deps.stt` unchanged. <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-010**: When a session cannot be opened, is refused for capacity, drops before the close, does not take its buffered start in time (EARS-SES-003) or does not deliver its final within `ears.stt_timeout_ms` of the close, the ears service shall transcribe the utterance's retained clip with the batch engine and publish that text as the final `speech.heard` of the same `utteranceId`, losing only the partials. <!-- test: services/ears/test/partials.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-011**: If an utterance's session cannot serve because of an outage-class failure and there is no batch engine, then the ears service shall drop the utterance with a warning, publish nothing for it, and report the reason in `deps.stt` (EARS-HLTH-006). <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-012**: When a session cannot be opened, the ears service shall make one attempt per utterance and shall run no reconnection loop and keep no queue of failed audio. <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-SES-013**: If an utterance is refused a session for capacity, or an utterance of a source named in `ears.echo_suppress_sources` passes the echo gate, and there is no batch engine, then the ears service shall drop the utterance with a warning, publish nothing for it, and leave `deps.stt` unchanged. <!-- test: services/ears/test/sessions.test.ts -->

## Nemotron engine (NEM)

Design: [ears-design.md § STT engines](ears-design.md#stt-engines), `nemotron`. These lines state what ears sends and does for each server message; the message shapes are those of NeMo-Speech.cpp as researched on 2026-11-16 and may change before it reaches 1.0.

- [ ] **EARS-NEM-001**: Where `ears.stt_engine` is `nemotron`, the ears service shall connect a WebSocket to `<ears.nemotron.url>/v1/audio/transcriptions/realtime`, mapping the URL's `http` scheme to `ws` and `https` to `wss`, with `Authorization: Bearer <ears.nemotron.api_key>` only when that key is non-empty.
- [ ] **EARS-NEM-002**: When a `nemotron` connection is made, the ears service shall wait for the server's `session.created` before sending audio and shall send no `session.update`.
- [ ] **EARS-NEM-003**: The `nemotron` session shall send each audio push as a binary little-endian 16-bit PCM frame at 16 kHz of the length ears has, with no resampling.
- [ ] **EARS-NEM-004**: When a `conversation.item.input_audio_transcription.delta` event arrives, the `nemotron` session shall append its `delta` to the hypothesis where `extends_previous` is true and otherwise make the `delta` the whole hypothesis.
- [ ] **EARS-NEM-005**: When a `nemotron` utterance closes, the ears service shall send `input_audio_buffer.commit`, take the `transcript` of the `...completed` event as the final, and close the connection; a session shall not be reused for another utterance.
- [ ] **EARS-NEM-006**: If a `nemotron` server sends an `error` event, never delivers its final within `ears.stt_timeout_ms`, or the connection drops during `finish()`, then the ears service shall treat the session as failed (EARS-SES-010).
- [ ] **EARS-NEM-007**: Where `ears.stt_engine` is `nemotron`, the ears service shall use `GET <ears.nemotron.url>/ready` as the readiness probe of EARS-HLTH-008.

## Kyutai engine (KYU)

Design: [ears-design.md § STT engines](ears-design.md#stt-engines), `kyutai`. These lines state what ears sends and does for each server message; the message shapes are those of `moshi-server` as researched on 2026-11-16.

- [ ] **EARS-KYU-001**: Where `ears.stt_engine` is `kyutai`, the ears service shall connect a WebSocket to `<ears.kyutai.url>/api/asr-streaming`, mapping the URL's `http` scheme to `ws` and `https` to `wss`, with the header `kyutai-api-key` set to `ears.kyutai.api_key`, or to `public_token` when that key is empty, and shall wait for the server's `Ready` before sending audio.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-002**: The `kyutai` session shall upsample ears' 16 kHz frames to 24 kHz with the shared `resample` and send them as MessagePack `Audio` messages of float32 samples in -1 to 1.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-003**: While a `kyutai` session is open and no frame has arrived for 80 ms, the ears service shall send 80 ms of zeros as fill, and shall not treat fill as speech.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-004**: When a frame arrives after fill, the `kyutai` session shall append it to the stream.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-005**: When a `Word` message arrives, the `kyutai` session shall append its text to the hypothesis once, joined by a single space, and shall ignore `EndWord`.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-006**: When a `kyutai` utterance closes, the ears service shall make sure the server has received at least 0.6 s of silence after the last real audio (sending the shortfall as zeros), send a `Marker`, keep sending silence until the `Marker` is echoed, bounded by `ears.stt_timeout_ms`, and take the hypothesis as the final.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-007**: If the `Marker` is not echoed within `ears.stt_timeout_ms`, then the ears service shall take the words received so far as the final, not treat it as a failure, leave `deps.stt` unchanged and not fall back to the batch engine.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-008**: Where `ears.kyutai.end_of_turn` is `on` (`on` or `off`, blank is `off`), when a `Step` message's third probability exceeds `ears.kyutai.end_of_turn_threshold` (a number above 0 and below 1, default 0.5), the `kyutai` session shall signal an end of turn.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-009**: The `kyutai` session shall signal an end of turn only after at least one `Word` has arrived and the voice-activity detector has classified a frame of the utterance as speech, only before the utterance is closed, at most once per session, and never where `ears.vad.engine` is `gap`.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-010**: When the ears service receives an end-of-turn signal, it shall close the utterance, end the clip at the last frame it had received when the signal arrived, and set `timing.audioEndedAt` to that frame's arrival time.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-011**: When a frame arrives after an end-of-turn close, the ears service shall start a new utterance whose pre-roll holds only frames that arrived after the close, so no audio already sent is sent again.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-012**: If `ears.max_utterance_ms` is reached before the end-of-turn signal, then the max-length close of EARS-VAD-005 shall win and the signal shall do nothing.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-013**: Where `ears.kyutai.end_of_turn` is `off`, the ears service shall ignore the third probability of every `Step` and end utterances by the rules of EARS-VAD-* alone.
- [ ] **EARS-KYU-014**: If a `kyutai` server sends an `Error` message other than the no-free-channel answer, or closes the connection before the close of the utterance or during `finish()`, then the ears service shall treat the session as failed (EARS-SES-010).
