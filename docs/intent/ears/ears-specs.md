# ears specs

Segment `ears` (prefix `EARS`). Mapped 2026-10-31 at git `686cc8f`; verified against the code in the audit pass of 2026-11-01. Owner decisions of 2026-11-01 applied at git `1b57790`. LLD: [ears-design.md](ears-design.md). Markers: `[x]` implemented, `[ ]` intended but not yet implemented (gap), `[D]` deferred. `[proposed]` inside a line marks a value or choice awaiting the owner's confirmation (spoken fallback line).

## Startup plans

- [x] **EARS-START-001**: When `ears.source` is `off`, the ears service shall create no Discord client, require no Discord environment variables, publish no `voice.playing` or `voice.done` and no `speech.heard` other than injected ones, and stay running idle with heartbeat mode `off` and state `ready`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:15 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Off source -->
- [x] **EARS-START-002**: When `ears.source` is `discord` and `DISCORD_TOKEN` is unset, the ears service shall not exit and shall create no Discord client, shall log an error at startup and a warning every 60 s, and shall report heartbeat state `degraded` (detail `DISCORD_TOKEN is not set`) with mode `live` and `deps.discord` `down`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:7 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Discord session -->
- [x] **EARS-START-003**: While the ears startup plan is `off` or `no-token`, the ears service shall subscribe (consumer group `ears`) to `inject.request` only and shall ignore `voice.utterance`, publishing no `voice.playing` or `voice.done`. <!-- openspec: ears § Off source -->
- [x] **EARS-START-004**: The ears service shall choose its startup plan with the pure function `earsPlan(source, hasToken)` returning `off` for source `off` regardless of token, `no-token` for `discord` without a token, and `discord` otherwise, and shall load discord.js only when the plan is `discord`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:23 -->
- [x] **EARS-START-005**: When the process receives a shutdown signal, the ears service shall, in the `off` and `no-token` plans, clear the idle warning timer, stop the bus and exit with code 0 (the `discord` plan's sequence is EARS-SESS-008). <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:110 -->
- [x] **EARS-START-006**: The ears service shall subscribe its consumer group `ears` with start mode `latest`, so the group is reset to the newest entry on every start and no `voice.utterance` or `inject.request` published while ears was down is played or handled. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:97 -->

## Discord session

- [x] **EARS-SESS-001**: If Discord login fails when the ears plan is `discord`, then the ears service shall log the error and exit the process with code 1. <!-- openspec: ears § Discord session -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-002**: When the Discord client is ready in the `discord` plan, the ears service shall join the voice channel `ears.discord.voice_channel_id` of guild `ears.discord.guild_id` (EARS-SESS-007) without self-mute or self-deafen and wait up to 20 s for the connection to reach Ready. <!-- openspec: ears § Discord session -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-003**: If the configured channel is not a voice-based channel, then the ears join attempt shall fail with the error "ears.discord.voice_channel_id is not a voice channel". <!-- openspec: ears § Discord session -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-004**: When the ears voice connection reaches Ready, the ears service shall subscribe the audio player to it, mark `deps.discord` `ok`, and report the heartbeat `ready`. <!-- openspec: ears § Heartbeat (add-service-health) -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-005**: When the ears voice connection is Disconnected and does not enter Signalling or Connecting within 5 s, the ears service shall mark `deps.discord` `down`, destroy the connection, and attempt to rejoin after 5 s. <!-- openspec: ears § Discord session -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-006**: If a voice-channel join fails in the `discord` plan (the initial join or a rejoin after a disconnect), then the ears service shall log the failure, report heartbeat `degraded` with `deps.discord` `down`, keep its bus subscription running, and retry the join with increasing delay until it succeeds. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:12 --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:28 --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:36 -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-007**: When joining in the `discord` plan, the ears service shall select the guild and voice channel from the configuration keys `ears.discord.guild_id` and `ears.discord.voice_channel_id` (which the example configuration fills from `DISCORD_GUILD_ID` and `DISCORD_VOICE_CHANNEL_ID`), not by reading those environment variables directly. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:50 -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-008**: When the process receives a shutdown signal in the `discord` plan, the ears service shall run one shutdown sequence that destroys the voice connection and the Discord client and then stops the bus before exiting. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:110 -->

## Listening and transcription

- [x] **EARS-HEAR-001**: When a user other than the bot itself starts speaking in the ears voice channel and is not already being captured, the ears service shall subscribe to that user's Opus stream, ending the capture after `ears.silence_ms` (default 700) of silence, and shall not open a second subscription while one is active. <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-002**: When a speaker's captured Opus stream ends, the ears service shall decode it to 48 kHz stereo PCM, label it with the speaker's guild display name (or user id when no member is cached), and hand it to transcription. <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-003**: If the Opus decoder for a speaker errors, then the ears service shall remove the speaker from its listening set and log the warning "decoder error". <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-004**: When a captured utterance is shorter than `ears.min_speech_ms` (default 400), the ears service shall neither call speech-to-text nor publish anything. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:85 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-005**: When a captured utterance is long enough, the ears service shall convert it to 16 kHz mono WAV and POST it as multipart form data to `<STT_URL>/v1/audio/transcriptions` with fields `file`, `model` set to `ears.stt_model` (default `Systran/faster-whisper-small`), `language` set to `en`, and `response_format` set to `json`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:77,104 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-006**: When the transcript contains at least one word character, the ears service shall publish `speech.heard` with `speaker`, `speakerId`, `text` and `durationMs` (rounded to whole milliseconds). <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:77 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-007**: If the transcript is empty or consists only of non-word characters, then the ears service shall publish nothing. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:85 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-008**: If speech-to-text fails (non-2xx response or thrown error), then the ears service shall log the warning "transcription failed", publish no event, and keep running. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:96 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->

## Audio conversion

- [x] **EARS-AUD-001**: When converting captured Discord audio for speech-to-text, the ears service shall downsample 48 kHz stereo s16le PCM to a 16 kHz mono WAV by averaging each group of 3 frames and both channels, clamping to the int16 range and dropping a trailing partial group. <!-- test: services/ears/test/audio.test.ts:40,52,58,85 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Audio conversion -->
- [x] **EARS-AUD-002**: When converting an utterance for Discord playback, the ears service shall convert a mono or stereo 16-bit WAV of any sample rate to 48 kHz stereo s16le PCM by linear interpolation, duplicating a mono channel and preserving duration. <!-- test: services/ears/test/audio.test.ts:66,72,79,85 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Audio conversion -->
- [x] **EARS-AUD-003**: When parsing a WAV in the ears service, `readWavHeader` (a thin adapter over the shared `parseWav`, `@cohost/shared`) shall walk the RIFF chunks without requiring the RIFF/WAVE header (skipping unknown and odd-sized chunks), return sample rate, channel count and clamped data offset and length, and throw "bad wav" if there is no data chunk. <!-- test: services/ears/test/audio.test.ts:18,23 --> <!-- test: packages/shared/test/wav.test.ts:39,46,60 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Audio conversion -->
- [x] **EARS-AUD-004**: When a service wraps raw s16le PCM as a WAV, the shared `wavWrap` (`@cohost/shared`, used by ears for the STT clip and by voice for the stub engine) shall prepend a canonical 44-byte PCM16 header carrying the given sample rate and channel count, with byte rate, block align and RIFF and data lengths derived from them, so that the shared `parseWav` reads the same rate, channels, data span and duration back. <!-- test: packages/shared/test/wav.test.ts:71 --> <!-- test: packages/shared/test/wav.test.ts:90 -->

## Playback sink

- [x] **EARS-PLAY-001**: While the ears plan is `discord`, the ears service shall read `voice.utterance` and `inject.request` through one shared loop of consumer group `ears`, started before the voice-channel join (EARS-INJ-007), and shall queue a `voice.utterance` for playback only while the voice connection is ready. <!-- openspec: ears § Utterance playback -->
- [x] **EARS-PLAY-002**: When a `voice.utterance` arrives, the ears service shall queue it and play queued utterances one at a time in arrival order, fetching the WAV from the event's `audioUrl`, converting it, and handing it to the Discord audio player. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:25,56 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Utterance playback -->
- [x] **EARS-PLAY-003**: When the ears service starts playing an utterance, it shall publish `voice.playing` with the utterance `id` and `sink` set to `discord`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:25 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Utterance playback -->
- [x] **EARS-PLAY-004**: When the Discord audio player becomes idle while an utterance is current, the ears service shall publish `voice.done` with that `id` and `sink` `discord` and start the next queued utterance; when idle with nothing current it shall publish nothing. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:25 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Utterance playback -->
- [ ] **EARS-PLAY-005**: If fetching or converting the audio for a queued utterance fails, or handing it to the Discord audio player throws, before any of its audio has started, then the ears service shall log the warning "playback failed", give up on that utterance (playing none of its audio and handling it by EARS-PLAY-006 and the EARS-FALL lines) and only then start the next queued utterance; a player error after audio has started is not a give-up and ends the item through EARS-PLAY-007 and EARS-PLAY-004. <!-- implemented today as a silent skip with no voice.playing: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:44,56 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Utterance playback -->
- [ ] **EARS-PLAY-006**: When the ears playback sink gives up on a queued utterance, it shall publish exactly one `voice.failed` for that utterance with its `id`, `sink` `discord` and `reason` set to the failure's error text `[proposed]`, so voice, avatar and cortex need not wait for voice's 1.5 s virtual sink to time the line.
- [x] **EARS-PLAY-007**: If the Discord audio player emits an error, then the ears service shall log the warning "player error".
- [x] **EARS-PLAY-008**: The ears service shall not subscribe to `control`: it plays every `voice.utterance` it receives and publishes `speech.heard` regardless of `mute` or `pause`, which cortex and salience enforce upstream.
- [x] **EARS-PLAY-009**: If publishing `voice.done` for a finished utterance fails, then the ears service shall log a warning and continue with the next queued utterance. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:56 -->

## Fallback line

Design: LLD § Fallback Line. "Give up" means EARS-PLAY-005. "The clip cache" is the set of fallback clips ears holds in memory, mirrored under `<dataDir()>/ears/fallback-clips` (CFG-DIR-006, CFG-DIR-007). "Voice's manifest" and the clip routes are VOICE-CLIP-011 and VOICE-CLIP-012. A "real sink" is any `sink` other than `virtual`.

- [ ] **EARS-FALL-001**: When the ears playback sink gives up on a queued utterance and none of EARS-FALL-005, EARS-FALL-007, EARS-FALL-008 or EARS-FALL-009 applies, the ears service shall play one fallback clip from the clip cache in that utterance's place, converted as in EARS-AUD-002, before starting the next queued utterance.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-002**: When the ears service has handed a fallback clip to the Discord audio player, it shall publish `voice.playing` with the given-up utterance's `id` and `sink` `discord`, and after that publish the utterance's `voice.failed` (EARS-PLAY-006).
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-003**: While a fallback clip is playing, the ears service shall treat the given-up utterance as the current item, so that when the Discord audio player becomes idle it publishes `voice.done` with that `id` and `sink` `discord` (EARS-PLAY-004) and starts the next queued utterance.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-004**: When the ears service gives up on an utterance, it shall choose the fallback clip from its in-memory clip cache, making no network request and reading no file at that moment.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-005**: If the clip cache is empty or every clip in it is marked bad (EARS-FALL-008), then the ears service shall play no fallback for the given-up utterance.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-006**: When choosing a fallback clip, the ears service shall pick uniformly at random among the cached clips that are not marked bad, excluding the clip whose phrase text matches the last fallback clip it started whenever at least one other eligible clip exists `[proposed]`.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-007**: If the ears service last handed a fallback clip to the Discord audio player successfully (since it started) less than `ears.fallback.min_interval_ms` (default 20000, CFG-SCH-018) before a give-up, then it shall play no fallback for that give-up.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-008**: If converting the chosen fallback clip fails, then the ears service shall play no fallback for that give-up, log a warning naming the clip, and not pick that clip again until the clip cache's `fingerprint` changes; if handing a converted clip to the Discord audio player throws, it shall play no fallback for that give-up without marking the clip `[proposed]`.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-009**: If a real sink other than `discord` has published `voice.playing` for an utterance `id` before the ears service gives up on that utterance, then the ears service shall play no fallback for it.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-010**: While the ears plan is `discord`, the ears service shall also read `voice.playing` on its one `ears` loop (EARS-PLAY-001) and remember, for the newest 50 utterance ids it has seen, which ids a real sink other than `discord` has claimed.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-011**: When the ears service publishes `voice.failed` for a given-up utterance, it shall include `fallback` with the clip's name (`clip`) and phrase text (`text`) from the cached manifest if and only if a fallback clip is playing for that `id` `[proposed]`.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-012**: The ears service shall obtain fallback clips only from voice's fallback HTTP routes (VOICE-CLIP-011, VOICE-CLIP-012) and shall write fallback-clip files only under its own directory `<dataDir()>/ears/fallback-clips`.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-013**: While the voice connection is not ready, the ears service shall publish no `voice.failed` and play no fallback for a `voice.utterance` it skips (EARS-INJ-007).
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-014**: When the ears plan is `discord` and the service has started, the ears service shall request voice's manifest (`GET <urls.voice()>/fallback/manifest.json`) and then repeat the request every 60 s `[proposed interval]`.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-015**: When voice's manifest answers 200 with a `fingerprint` different from the clip cache's, the ears service shall download every listed clip, check each one's byte count and SHA-256 against the manifest and that it parses as a 16-bit WAV, and only when all pass replace the whole in-memory clip cache and its disk mirror with the new set, writing the mirror into a temporary directory that it then renames into place `[proposed]`.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-016**: If the manifest request fails, times out, or answers anything but 200, then the ears service shall keep its clip cache unchanged; a 200 manifest that lists no clips shall empty the clip cache.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-017**: If any clip download fails, or a clip fails the size, checksum or WAV check, then the ears service shall abandon that refresh, keep the previous clip cache, delete the partial download, and retry at the next manifest request.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-018**: When the ears plan is `discord`, at startup and before its first manifest request, the ears service shall load the mirrored set from its cache directory into memory; if the mirror's manifest is unreadable or any listed clip is missing or fails its checks, it shall start with an empty clip cache and log a warning `[proposed]`.
- [ ] **EARS-FALL-019**: If writing the disk mirror fails during a refresh, then the ears service shall still use the verified new set from memory and log a warning.

## Audio injection

- [x] **EARS-INJ-001**: When an `inject.request` with `target` other than `ears` arrives, the ears service shall ignore it and publish nothing. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:81 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-002**: If an `inject.request` for ears is older than 60 s by its event timestamp, then the ears service shall publish `inject.result` with `ok` false and detail `stale` and no `speech.heard`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:81 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-003**: If an `inject.request` for ears has `kind` other than `audio`, then the ears service shall publish `inject.result` with `ok` false and detail "ears cannot handle kind '<kind>'".
- [x] **EARS-INJ-004**: If an `inject.request` URL is not on the origin of `urls.dashboard()` under `/uploads/` (or carries credentials), then the ears service shall fetch nothing and publish `inject.result` with `ok` false and detail "url is not an upload of this dashboard"; an accepted fetch shall refuse redirects and a non-2xx response shall yield `ok` false with detail "fetch upload <status>". <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:72 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-005**: When an `inject.request` for ears with `kind` `audio` and an accepted URL arrives, the ears service shall send the fetched bytes unchanged to speech-to-text with the response content type as hint, without applying `ears.min_speech_ms`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:27,36,46 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-006**: When an injected clip transcribes to a transcript with at least one word character, the ears service shall publish `speech.heard` with `speaker` set to `meta.user` or `injected`, `durationMs` from the WAV header (0 for non-WAV), `injected` true and the request's `remember`, followed by `inject.result` with `ok` true and the text as detail. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:27,36,46,91 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-007**: While the ears plan is `discord`, the ears service shall start handling `inject.request` for ears before it joins the Discord voice channel and keep handling it whether or not the join succeeds; while the voice connection is not ready, a `voice.utterance` read on the same `ears` loop shall be skipped (not queued, no `voice.playing`), so voice's virtual sink covers it rather than ears claiming a line it cannot play. <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio (MUST work when Discord is not connected) --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:69 --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:82 -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-008**: If an injected clip's transcript is empty or non-word, or speech-to-text or the upload fetch throws, then the ears service shall publish `inject.result` with `ok` false (detail "empty transcript" or the error text) and no `speech.heard`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:54,64 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-009**: Where the ears plan is `off` or `no-token`, the ears service shall still handle `inject.request` for ears. <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->

## Health

- [x] **EARS-HLTH-001**: The ears service shall publish heartbeats with `service` `ears`, mode `off` when `ears.source` is `off` and `live` otherwise, and `deps` `{discord, stt}`, where both are `n/a` for the `off` plan. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:7,15,31 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Heartbeat (add-service-health) -->
- [x] **EARS-HLTH-002**: While the ears plan is `discord`, the ears service shall report `deps.discord` `ok` only while the voice connection is Ready and `down` otherwise (always `down` for `no-token`). <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:7,31 -->
- [x] **EARS-HLTH-003**: When a speech-to-text call (live or injected) fails, the ears service shall set `deps.stt` to `down`, and when the next call succeeds it shall set it back to `ok`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:31 -->
