# cortex specs

Design: [cortex-design.md](cortex-design.md). Prefix `CTX`. `[x]` = implemented, `[ ]` = intended but not yet implemented (gap), `[D]` = deferred. Test paths are relative to the repo root; a `<!-- test: -->` pointer names the test that carries the matching `@spec` annotation.

## Event handling

- [ ] **CTX-EVT-001**: The cortex shall subscribe to the bus in consumer group `cortex` with start mode `latest` (the group is reset to the stream's newest entry on every start, so events published while the cortex was not subscribed, including stale `attention.focus`, are never delivered) for `chat.message`, `chat.removed`, `speech.heard`, `vision.summary`, `brain.say`, `brain.clause`, `voice.utterance`, `voice.playing`, `voice.done`, `voice.stop`, `attention.focus`, `attention.resolve`, `memory.recall.reply`, `memory.notes.reply`, `memory.viewer`, `affect.state`, `tool.catalog`, `tool.result` and `control`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/startup.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-002**: When a `chat.message`, `speech.heard` or `vision.summary` arrives, the cortex shall add it to its working-memory window of `brain.working_memory_events` events. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-003**: When a `brain.say` arrives from any source, the cortex shall add it to its working memory as a self line with its text and emotion. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-004**: When a `brain.say` arrives whose bus source is not `cortex`, the cortex shall add its `id` to the lines pending on the voice (a line that has begun and not yet ended), so that thinking is deferred while any pending line plays. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context (Foreign line) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-005**: When a `voice.done` arrives whose `id` is a pending line without `more`, the cortex shall end that line and, once no line is pending, publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if the cortex is muted or paused; a `voice.done` for an id that is not pending shall end no line and publish no `brain.status`; the cortex shall not publish a status for a `voice.done` while a think is in flight. <!-- openspec: cortex § Inputs and context (voice.done) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.dispositions.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-006**: When an `attention.focus` arrives, the cortex shall start thinking without awaiting the result, so the subscription loop can still deliver the `memory.recall.reply` it is waiting for. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-007**: When an `affect.state` arrives, the cortex shall keep it as the current mood for later prompts. <!-- openspec: cortex § Viewer notes and mood --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-EVT-008**: The cortex shall also subscribe to `voice.failed` in its `cortex` group subscription (CTX-EVT-001). <!-- test: services/cortex/test/startup.test.ts -->
- [ ] **CTX-EVT-009**: The cortex shall not subscribe to `speech.partial`, and a `speech.partial` shall never be added to its working-memory window or start a think: `speech.heard` is the only event the cortex adds to its window as speech that happened, the one exception being the unfinished line of CTX-INTJ-002.
- [x] **CTX-EVT-010**: When a `voice.stop` arrives, the cortex shall end every pending line whose `id` it names (every pending line when `ids` is absent) and, once no line is pending, publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if the cortex is muted or paused; a `voice.stop` that ends no pending line shall publish no `brain.status`.
- [x] **CTX-EVT-011**: When a `voice.done` flagged `interrupted` with `spokenText` arrives, the cortex shall record `spokenText` as the heard text of the working-memory line with that `id` (`markHeard`), whether or not that line is still pending.
- [x] **CTX-EVT-012**: When a `voice.done` that is flagged neither `interrupted` nor `final` arrives, the cortex shall clear the heard record of the working-memory line with that `id`.

## Think gating

- [ ] **CTX-GATE-001**: When a focus arrives while an ordinary think is already in flight (a held provisional think is not one, CTX-PROV-018), the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `thinking` with the new focus reason and shall not call the LLM. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating (Overlapping focus) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-002**: While the cortex is muted or paused, a focus shall publish `brain.status` `muted` and make no LLM call. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating (Muted); add-service-control § Targeted pause and resume --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-003**: While any line (another source's or its own) is pending on the voice, a focus shall publish `brain.status` `speaking` and make no LLM call. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-004**: When `brain.cortex.speaking_timeout_ms` (default 20000) has elapsed without progress (CTX-REL-001) on a line that has a clause outstanding, the cortex shall release that line (a streamed line with CTX-STREAM-036), so once no other line is pending the next focus can proceed to think; the timeout itself schedules no focus and invokes no think. <!-- openspec: cortex § Think gating --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-005**: When the cortex's final re-check runs before the first clause (after the critic has returned), and the cortex has become muted or paused, the cortex shall drop its reply and publish `brain.status` `muted`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-006**: When the cortex's final re-check runs before the first clause (after the critic has returned), and another line has started speaking, the cortex shall drop its reply and publish `brain.status` `speaking`. <!-- openspec: cortex § Saying and control (Spoken over while thinking) --> <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-007**: The cortex shall clear its thinking flag on every exit from a think, including errors, so a later focus can run. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts, services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-008**: If publishing `brain.status` fails on a gated early-return path (muted, paused or speaking), the cortex shall log the failure and return, instead of raising an unhandled promise rejection from the unawaited think. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-009**: When `brain.cortex.speaking_timeout_ms` elapses without progress on a line (CTX-REL-001) and no other line is then pending, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if it is muted or paused, at that moment, except that it shall not publish while a think is in flight. <!-- test: services/cortex/test/cortex.region.test.ts -->
- [x] **CTX-GATE-010**: When a `voice.stop` arrives, the cortex shall not cancel, restart or start a think that has not published its first clause: such a think in flight shall complete and its reply shall be subject to CTX-GATE-005 and CTX-GATE-006 as if no stop had been read, and no think shall start for the stop itself.
- [x] **CTX-GATE-011**: When a `voice.done` flagged `interrupted` arrives for a line that a `voice.stop` has already ended, the cortex shall change no pending line and publish no `brain.status`, and shall still record its `spokenText` (CTX-EVT-011).
- [x] **CTX-GATE-012**: When a think ends without a line (an empty reply, a critic veto, a repetition hit on the first clause, or a mute or busy re-check that fails before the first clause), the cortex shall publish no `brain.say` and shall publish `brain.status` `muted` if it is muted or paused, else `speaking` while any line is pending or playing, else `idle`.
- [x] **CTX-GATE-013**: When a think has a clause to publish, the cortex shall run these steps in this order: the critic (by its kind, CTX-STREAM-012 and CTX-STREAM-013), the repetition check, the mute and busy re-check, then publish the clause.

## Provisional think (PROV)

Design: [cortex-design.md § Provisional think](cortex-design.md#provisional-think). "The confirmation" is the release of CTX-PROV-019; a held think is a provisional think the cortex has started and not ended.

- [ ] **CTX-PROV-001**: When an `attention.focus` flagged `provisional` arrives and no think is in flight, the cortex shall start a provisional think through the gates of CTX-GATE-002 and CTX-GATE-003 and shall publish `brain.status` `thinking` with the focus `reason` as detail.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-002**: A provisional think shall publish `memory.recall.request` and `memory.notes.request` as an ordinary think does.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-003**: Until the confirmation of CTX-PROV-019, a provisional think shall publish no `brain.say`, no `brain.clause`, no `tool.request` and no `voice.stop`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-004**: Until the confirmation of CTX-PROV-019, a provisional think shall delete no viewer note, consume no pending glitch and add no `self` entry to the working-memory window.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-005**: A provisional think shall build its prompt from the window as it stood when the think started plus the early text as a transient last transcript line rendered as a `[voice]` entry (WM-TRANS-003), shall not rebuild it, and shall leave events that reach the window during the hold out of it.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-006**: A provisional think shall hold, in order and unpublished, each clause that has passed the critic and the repetition check, and shall keep reading the model stream to its end or to `brain.cortex.max_clauses`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-007**: Where the critic is `llm` or `http`, a provisional think shall collect and criticise the whole reply as CTX-STREAM-013 says and hold it whole.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-008**: When a provisional think's reply is vetoed, empty, or a repetition hit on its first clause, the cortex shall hold the result as no line.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-009**: When the model's round in a provisional think ends in tool calls, the cortex shall hold the text before them, publish no `tool.request`, and wait at that point; after the confirmation the tool loop shall continue, and `tools.reply.filler_after_ms` and `tools.reply.budget_ms` shall count from the first `tool.request` published after the confirmation.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-010**: A provisional think shall be held at most `brain.cortex.hold_limit_ms` (default 5000) milliseconds, counted from the start of the think.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-011**: When an `attention.resolve` with `outcome` `withdraw` arrives for the `utteranceId` and `seq` of a held provisional think, the cortex shall end the think (CTX-PROV-016, CTX-PROV-031 to CTX-PROV-034).
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-012**: When a `mute` or a `pause` arrives while the cortex holds a provisional think, it shall end the think (CTX-PROV-016, CTX-PROV-031 to CTX-PROV-034).
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-013**: When a `forget_context` arrives while the cortex holds a provisional think, it shall end the think (CTX-PROV-016, CTX-PROV-031 to CTX-PROV-034).
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-014**: When `brain.cortex.hold_limit_ms` passes before the confirmation, the cortex shall end the provisional think (CTX-PROV-016, CTX-PROV-031 to CTX-PROV-034).
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-015**: When an `attention.focus` that is not flagged `provisional` and is not the confirmation of CTX-PROV-020 arrives while the cortex holds a provisional think, whether for another utterance or for the same utterance with different text, the cortex shall end the provisional think and take that focus as an ordinary focus through the gates, as if no think were held.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-016**: When a provisional think ends by CTX-PROV-011 to CTX-PROV-014, CTX-PROV-027 or CTX-PROV-015, the cortex shall abort the model stream through the think's `signal` and drop the held clauses.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-017**: A `voice.stop` shall never end a provisional think.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-018**: A held provisional think shall never cause the cortex to decline an ordinary focus as busy (CTX-GATE-001).
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-019**: When an `attention.resolve` with `outcome` `confirm` and no `interjected` arrives for the `utteranceId` and `seq` of a held provisional think, the cortex shall take the final's `timing` members `audioEndedAt`, `closedAt` and `heardAt` from it and stamp `confirmedAt`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-020**: When an ordinary `attention.focus` whose focus is a `speech` event arrives with the same `utteranceId` and the same text as a held provisional think, the cortex shall treat it as the confirmation of CTX-PROV-019.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-021**: When a confirmed provisional think is still reading the model stream, the cortex shall check and publish the clauses that arrive afterwards as an ordinary think does, and the limits of an ordinary think shall apply from the confirmation.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-022**: When the result of a confirmed provisional think is no line, the cortex shall take the no-line exit (CTX-GATE-012).
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-023**: When an `attention.resolve` with `outcome` `confirm` and no `interjected` arrives and the cortex holds no provisional think for its `utteranceId` and `seq`, it shall think on it as on an ordinary `attention.focus` built from its `reason`, `score`, `focus`, `timing`, `injected` and `remember`, with `focusedAt` set to the `ts` of the `attention.resolve` envelope, and the resulting `brain.say` shall carry no `confirmedAt`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-024**: When an `attention.resolve` with `outcome` `withdraw` arrives and the cortex holds no provisional think for its `utteranceId` and `seq`, the cortex shall change nothing.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-025**: When the cortex publishes `brain.say` for a provisional think released by a confirm without `interjected`, it shall keep the think's own `llmStartedAt`, `llmEndedAt` and `criticEndedAt`, shall set `focusedAt` to the `ts` of the provisional `attention.focus` envelope, and shall add `confirmedAt` set to the epoch-millisecond time the confirmation reached the cortex.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-026**: When an `attention.focus` flagged `provisional` arrives while the cortex holds a provisional think or has an ordinary think in flight, the cortex shall ignore it: it shall not take it as an ordinary focus, publish no `brain.status` and make no model call.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-027**: When the cortex publishes a line of its own (an operator `say`) while it holds a provisional think, it shall end the think (CTX-PROV-016, CTX-PROV-031 to CTX-PROV-034).
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-028**: A held provisional think shall count as a think in flight for CTX-EVT-005 and CTX-GATE-009, so that no `idle` is published for a `voice.done` or for the speaking-timeout release during the hold.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-029**: When `brain.cortex.hold_limit_ms` ends a held think, the cortex shall publish the status of CTX-PROV-033, `idle` when no line is pending.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-030**: When a provisional think first becomes ready (its first clause is held, or the whole reply for a reply of one clause) or parks at a tool call, whichever comes first, before it is released, the cortex shall stamp `heldAt` with that epoch-millisecond time, reading of the model stream may continue after it, and the `brain.say` of a reply released later from that think shall carry `heldAt` in its `timing`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-031**: When a provisional think ends, the cortex shall clear the hold timer and the thinking flag.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-032**: When a provisional think ends, the cortex shall publish no closing `brain.clause`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-033**: When a provisional think ends other than by replacement (CTX-PROV-015), the cortex shall publish the status of the no-line exit: `muted` when muted or paused, else `speaking` while a line is pending or playing, else `idle`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-034**: When a provisional think is replaced by an ordinary focus (CTX-PROV-015), the cortex shall publish no `idle` and no other status for the ended think, and the ordinary think shall publish `brain.status` `thinking` at its start as CTX-SAY-002 says.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-035**: When a held provisional think is released, by a confirm or by an interjected confirm, the cortex shall clear the hold limit, and from then the limits of an ordinary think shall apply.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-036**: When a held provisional think is released by a confirmation (CTX-PROV-019 or CTX-PROV-020), the cortex shall run the mute and busy re-check of CTX-GATE-013 and, when it fails, drop the think as an ordinary first-clause check does.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-037**: When a held provisional think is released by a confirmation and passes the re-check, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `thinking`.
- [ ] **CTX-PROV-038**: When a held provisional think is released by a confirmation and passes the re-check, the cortex shall publish the held clauses as an ordinary reply: the first as `brain.say` and each later one as `brain.clause` in order behind that re-check.

## Interjected confirm (INTJ)

Design: [cortex-design.md § Provisional think](cortex-design.md#provisional-think), An interjected confirm.

- [ ] **CTX-INTJ-001**: When an `attention.resolve` with `outcome` `confirm` and `interjected` true arrives, the cortex shall release the held think for its `utteranceId` and `seq` only if, at that moment, the reply's first clause is held (the whole reply when it is one clause), the critic and the repetition check have passed it, and the think is not parked at a tool call.
- [ ] **CTX-INTJ-002**: When the cortex releases an interjected confirm, it shall push the early text into its working-memory window as an unfinished line (WM-UNF-001) before it publishes the `brain.say`, and the `brain.say` shall carry `interjected` true and no `confirmedAt`.
- [ ] **CTX-INTJ-003**: When an `attention.resolve` with `interjected` true arrives and the held think is not ready, is parked at a tool call, holds the result no line, or the cortex holds no think for its `utteranceId` and `seq`, the cortex shall end any such think (CTX-PROV-016, CTX-PROV-031 to CTX-PROV-034), speak nothing, and shall not take the resolve as an ordinary focus.
- [ ] **CTX-INTJ-004**: When the cortex releases an interjected confirm it shall publish `brain.say` for the reply.
- [ ] **CTX-INTJ-005**: A reply released by an interjected confirm shall be an ordinary released reply: the cortex shall publish all its clauses as for any reply, and the `brain.say` shall carry no `confirmedAt`.
- [ ] **CTX-INTJ-006**: When the cortex does not release an interjected confirm, it shall publish a `brain.status` other than `thinking` (the status of the no-line exit), so that salience can tell that nothing was spoken.

## Deferred: dynamic speech (REPLAN)

Design: LLD Decisions & Alternatives, "Interruptible, dynamic speech (deferred)"; [HLD](../../high-level-design.md) Future / ideal state. Prerequisites still to build: a playback position (MOUTH-POS-001), the interrupted-thought stack (WM-STACK-001), a re-think call inside the latency budget and live vision for screen events. Clause-chunked speech and streaming model output are built on (the Streamed replies section below, VOICE-CHUNK-001, VOICE-CHUNK-002).

- [D] **CTX-REPLAN-001**: Where speech is dynamic, the cortex shall, at each clause boundary of a line being spoken and when a key event has arrived (a final `speech.heard`, or a screen event once live vision exists), decide whether to continue the line, adapt its remainder, pivot to the event, or resume a thought it interrupted earlier, and shall make that decision within the speech path's latency budget.

## Prompt and reply

- [x] **CTX-STREAM-008**: When the cleaned text of the reply reaches a boundary and the clause so far has at least `brain.cortex.clause_min_chars` characters, the cortex shall end the clause there; a boundary is one of `. ! ? … ; : ,` or `。！？、，；：` followed by whitespace, with a directly following closing quote or bracket kept in the clause.

- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-011**: When a round ends without tool calls, or the first newline is read in a round that offers no tools, the cortex shall take the text left, whatever its length, as the last clause, the first clause included.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-012**: Where the critic is `rules`, the cortex shall check and publish each clause as it arrives, shall ask the `cortex.critic` hook once, on the first model clause, and shall not ask it for later clauses.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-013**: Where the critic is `llm` or `http`, the cortex shall, for a reply without tool rounds, read the stream to its end, parse and criticise the whole reply as one text (CTX-CRITIC-002, CTX-CRITIC-003, CTX-CRITIC-007), run the repetition check once on the result, cut it into clauses by the clause rule and publish them in order, each behind the mute and busy re-check, and shall stop publishing as soon as the line is released or a `voice.stop` covers it.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-014**: When the cortex cleans a clause it shall remove bracketed spans, the delimiters `«` and `»` and surrounding quotes from every clause, and a leading `Name:` from the first clause only.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-015**: When a clause has no word character after cleaning, the cortex shall not publish it and shall drop its text.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-016**: Where the critic is `rules`, the cortex shall run the repetition check on the first clause and then the mute and busy re-check before publishing it.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-017**: When the first clause has passed its checks, the cortex shall publish `brain.say` with the clause as `text`, the line's `emotion` and `gesture`, the focus `reason`, `timing` and the propagated injection flags.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-018**: When the cortex has published the first clause, it shall publish `brain.status` `speaking` with that clause as detail.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-019**: When the first clause is published and the stream has not ended with nothing but whitespace after it or has ended with a request for a tool, the cortex shall set `more` to `true` on the `brain.say`; when the whole reply was that one clause it shall publish the `brain.say` without `more` and publish no `brain.clause`.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-020**: When a later clause passes its checks, the cortex shall publish `brain.clause` with the line's `id`, the next `seq` from 1, the clause as `text` and no `last`, and shall publish no `brain.status` for it.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-021**: When the repetition check finds a repeat on a later clause, the cortex shall not publish that clause and shall close the line with `cut` `repeat` (CTX-STREAM-026).
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-022**: When the cortex is muted or paused at the re-check of a later clause, it shall not publish that clause and shall close the line with `cut` `muted`.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-023**: When a line from someone else is pending or playing at the re-check of a later clause, the cortex shall not publish that clause and shall close the line with `cut` `busy`; the reply's own line shall not count, and an operator `say` line shall count as someone else's.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-024**: When a clause to publish would have a `seq` of `brain.cortex.max_clauses` or more, the cortex shall not publish it and shall close the line with `cut` `length`.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-025**: A cut of a reply shall fall on a clause boundary: a clause is published whole or not at all.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-026**: When the cortex closes a line with a `cut`, it shall abort the model stream if it is being streamed, then publish the closing `brain.clause` with empty `text`, `last` true, that `cut` and `through` set to the last published clause.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-027**: When the stream ends normally and a line published with `more` has text left that passes its checks, the cortex shall publish it as the closing `brain.clause` with `last` true, no `cut` and `through` equal to its `seq`; when nothing is left it shall publish the closing `brain.clause` with empty `text`, `last` true, no `cut` and `through` the last published clause.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-028**: The cortex shall publish exactly one closing `brain.clause` for every line whose `brain.say` carried `more`, whatever ended the reply, and shall publish none when the reply had already closed.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-029**: When a reply closes, or a think ends without a line, the cortex shall clear the thinking flag.
- [ ] **CTX-STREAM-030**: If the stream fails before the cortex has published a clause and no tool wait has run in the think, then the cortex shall log `think failed`, publish `brain.status` `idle` and publish no `brain.say`, even when text had arrived.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-031**: If the stream fails after the cortex has published a clause, then it shall close the line with `cut` `failed`, speak no fallback line and attempt no repair, except after a tool wait (CTX-TOOL-031).
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-032**: When the stream ends with a `finish` of `refusal` and nothing is published, the cortex shall end the think without a line (CTX-GATE-012); when a clause was published it shall close the reply normally.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-033**: The cortex shall read `brain.cortex.clause_min_chars`, `brain.cortex.clause_max_chars` and `brain.cortex.max_clauses` from configuration, as the config segment defines them.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-034**: When a `brain.clause` of the cortex's own arrives, the cortex shall add its text to the working-memory line with that `id` (`extendSelf(id, seq, text)`), and shall change nothing when the window no longer holds that line.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-035**: When a `voice.stop` names a streamed line whose closing `brain.clause` has not been published (its reply is being streamed, or its clauses are still being published), or carries no `ids`, the cortex shall close the line with `cut` `stopped`.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-036**: When the no-progress valve ends a streamed line whose closing `brain.clause` has not been published (its reply is being streamed, or its clauses are still being published), the cortex shall close the line with `cut` `timeout`.
- [x] **CTX-STREAM-037**: When the cortex stops, it shall close each streamed line whose reply had not closed with `cut` `stopped`, as far as the bus still accepts the publish.
- [x] **CTX-REL-001**: The cortex shall count as progress of a line every `voice.utterance`, `voice.playing` and `voice.done` for its `id` from any publisher, and the publication of a clause while none of the line's clauses is outstanding; a clause is outstanding from its publication until every player of the line has reported a `voice.done` for it or a `final` done, so a player that never completes keeps the no-progress valve running.
- [x] **CTX-REL-002**: When a `voice.playing` for a pending streamed line arrives, the cortex shall record its `sink` as a player of the line.
- [x] **CTX-REL-003**: The cortex shall record a player of a streamed line as having completed it when the player has reported a `voice.done` for the line's last produced clause, or a `voice.done` flagged `final` or `interrupted`.
- [x] **CTX-REL-004**: The cortex shall take a streamed line's last produced clause to be the highest `seq` it published, and, when a `voice.done` flagged `final` arrives from the sink `error` with `seq` k, to be k minus 1.
- [x] **CTX-REL-005**: Whenever a streamed line's last produced clause changes, the cortex shall judge each player's completion again against the new value.
- [x] **CTX-REL-006**: When a streamed line has at least one player, its reply has closed and every player of the line has completed it, the cortex shall release the line.
- [x] **CTX-REL-007**: When a streamed line's reply has closed, no sink has published `voice.playing` for it, and either its failure condition (CTX-REL-012) holds or a `voice.done` flagged `final` from the sink `error` for clause 0 has been read, the cortex shall release the line.
- [x] **CTX-REL-008**: While another player of a streamed line has not completed it, a `voice.done` flagged `final` from one sink shall not release the line.
- [x] **CTX-REL-009**: When completion or a `voice.stop` releases a streamed line and leaves no line pending and no think is in flight, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if it is muted or paused (a release by the valve is CTX-GATE-009).
- [x] **CTX-REL-010**: When a think ends or its reply closes and no line is then pending, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` `idle`, or `muted` if it is muted or paused; when a line is pending it shall publish no status.
- [x] **CTX-REL-011**: The cortex shall treat a streamed line's claim window as ended 1500 ms (the claim window of VOICE-SINK-002) after it read the line's first `voice.utterance`.
- [x] **CTX-REL-012**: The failure condition of a streamed line shall hold when at least one `voice.failed` or `final` `voice.done` has been read for it, its claim window has ended and every sink that published `voice.playing` for it has ended it with a `final` done or a `voice.failed`; with no such sink it shall hold when a failure has been read and the window has ended.
- [x] **CTX-REL-013**: When the failure condition of a streamed line holds, the cortex shall close the line with `cut` `failed`.
- [x] **CTX-REL-014**: While the failure condition of a streamed line does not hold, a `voice.failed` or `final` `voice.done` from one sink shall cut nothing: the cortex shall keep reading the stream and publishing clauses.
- [x] **CTX-REL-015**: When a `voice.done` flagged `final` arrives from the sink `error` for a streamed line whose reply has not closed, the cortex shall close the line with `cut` `failed` and `through` set to the clause before the failed one (0 when that is clause 0).
- [x] **CTX-REL-016**: When a `voice.failed` arrives for a streamed line, the cortex shall apply the working-memory mark and the pending glitch (CTX-FALL-008) once the line's closing `brain.clause` has been read back, or at once when the reply closed earlier.

## The tool loop (TOOL)

- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-001**: At the start of a think, after recall, the cortex shall use the latest `tool.catalog` only while it arrived within three times its `intervalMs`, and shall otherwise offer no tools and wait for nothing.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-002**: When the cortex derives a reply's origin, it shall do so from the focus kind whether or not the focus carries `injected`: for a `speech` focus `speech` when `tools.owner_sources` is `all` or lists the focus's `source` (the `source` an injected speech line names, none when it names none) and `guest` otherwise; `chat`, `vision` or `reminder` for those kinds; and `idle` when there is no focus object.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-003**: The cortex shall offer the model the tools of the current catalog that are `offered` and that the origin allows under TOOL-ORIG-002 to TOOL-ORIG-005.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-004**: The cortex shall fix the offer for a think, shall pass `tools` to `llm.stream` only when the offer is not empty, and shall let a catalog that goes stale during the think change nothing for it.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-005**: When the cortex calls `llm.stream` with tools, it shall pass `maxTokens` equal to `tools.reply.max_tokens` (default 512) in place of the provider's `llm.max_tokens` default and no option other than that and its `signal`.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-006**: When the cortex reads a round that offers tools, it shall read to the end of the stream, discard the text after the first newline and keep the tool calls.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-007**: When a round ends in tool calls, the cortex shall cut the text left in the buffer as a clause whatever its length and put it through the checks of CTX-GATE-013.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-008**: The cortex shall take the line's emotion and gesture from the first thing it publishes for the reply (the emotion `think` when that is the filler) and shall remove the leading tags of every later text as bracketed text.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-009**: The cortex shall number the clauses of a reply continuously across rounds, and where the critic is `rules` shall count every clause of the reply, the filler and the built-in line included, toward `brain.cortex.max_clauses`, ending the reply with `cut` `length` and running none of that round's calls when a round reaches it; where the critic is `llm` or `http` the count applies to the collected reply once.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-010**: When the cortex starts a round after the first, it shall append the previous round's assistant message unchanged and one `tool` message for each call, in the order of the calls, to the messages of round 1.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-011**: When a round ends with `finish` `tool_calls`, the cortex shall take its calls in the order asked, up to `tools.reply.max_calls` (default 3), and shall answer each call beyond that with the error `tool` message `Too many calls in one turn.` and publish no request for it.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-012**: When a call has `invalid` arguments, the cortex shall answer it with the error `tool` message `The call was not valid.` and publish no request for it.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-013**: When a call names a tool that was not offered, the cortex shall answer it with the error `tool` message `That tool is not available.` and publish no request for it.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-014**: When the cortex publishes a call, it shall publish `tool.request` with a fresh uuid `id`, the tool's name, its arguments, the reply's origin, `round` (the number of the round that asked, from 1) `listed` (true when `tools.owner_sources` is a list that names the reply's speech source, and always false when the focus carries `injected`) and `injected` (true when the focus carries `injected`).
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-015**: Where every call of a round has the effect `read` in the catalog, the cortex shall publish the requests together and wait for all of them.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-016**: Where any call of a round has the effect `acts` in the catalog, the cortex shall publish the requests one at a time in the order asked, each after the previous result.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-017**: When a round ends with `finish` `length`, the cortex shall run none of its calls, shall publish the text of the round as a clause, and shall end the reply with `cut` `length`, except that a round with no text and nothing published ends the think without a line as CTX-GATE-012 says.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-018**: When a tool call is cut off by the token cap, the cortex shall treat it as no call, log a warning and set its heartbeat detail as CTX-HEALTH-009 says.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-019**: The cortex shall wait for a result at most `tools.timeout_ms` plus 2000 ms, plus `tools.confirm_timeout_ms` when the catalog's effect for the tool is `acts` and the tool is not `timer`, and shall treat a call with no result by then as a timeout with the error `tool` message `The tool did not answer in time.`
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-020**: When the cortex has a result, it shall make it a `tool` message with `isError` true for every status but `ok`.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-021**: When a `tool.result` arrives for an `id` the cortex is not waiting for, or a second one for an `id` already resolved, the cortex shall ignore it.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-022**: At the start of each tool wait and each round, and while one lasts, the cortex shall publish `brain.status` with the detail `waiting for <tool>` during a wait and `tool round <n>` during a round: `speaking` every `brain.cortex.speaking_timeout_ms` divided by 2 when a clause of the reply has been published, and `thinking` every `brain.salience.thinking_timeout_ms` divided by 2 when none has, and shall end the renewals of a round when its stream ends, fails or times out under the first-output and gap bounds of `llm.timeout_ms` (LLM-STREAM-015), a failed round ending as CTX-TOOL-031 says.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-023**: While the cortex waits for a tool, it shall run no no-progress valve for the reply's line, and it shall count the end of the wait as progress of the line.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-024**: When `tools.reply.filler_after_ms` (default 1500) has passed since the reply's first `tool.request`, a call is outstanding and no clause of the reply has been published, the cortex shall publish a `brain.say` carrying `more`, with the fields of CTX-STREAM-017 (the injection flags of the focus included), the emotion `think` and a text picked at random from `tools.reply.filler_lines`, never the line used last when the list holds another.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-025**: The cortex shall put the filler through the mute and busy re-check only, count it toward `max_clauses` where the critic is `rules`, and show it neither to the critic nor to the repetition check.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-026**: The cortex shall publish at most one filler per reply, none when a clause of the reply has been published, and none when `tools.reply.filler_lines` is empty.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-027**: When `tools.reply.max_rounds` (default 3) rounds have ended in calls, or `tools.reply.budget_ms` (default 30000) has passed (wall time) since the reply's first `tool.request`, a wait or call in progress then still ending within its own bound, the cortex shall make the next round, the final round, with the same `tools`, and shall never send a request with tool history and no tool definitions.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-028**: When the model makes calls in the final round, or a call of the round in progress was not yet published when the budget ran out, the cortex shall answer each with the error `tool` message `No more tool calls.` and publish no request for it.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-029**: When the model made calls in the final round, the cortex shall make one more round with the same rule, and when that round makes calls again shall end the reply with `cut` `tools`, after what has been said or after the built-in line (CTX-TOOL-031) when nothing has been.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-030**: The cortex shall check `tools.reply.budget_ms` before each request and each round and shall cut no round and no call that is running.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-031**: When a round that follows a tool wait fails or ends with no speakable text, the cortex shall publish the line `Sorry, I couldn't find that out.` as the next clause behind the mute and busy re-check (a `brain.say` carrying `more` with the emotion `confused` when it is the first clause), show it neither to the critic nor to the repetition check, and close the reply with an empty closing `brain.clause` whose `through` is that clause and whose `cut` is `failed` for a failed stream, `tools` when it follows the hard stop of CTX-TOOL-029, and absent for an empty round.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-032**: Where the critic is `llm` or `http`, the cortex shall collect the text of every round, joined by single spaces, publish none of it while rounds and waits run, and when the last round ends criticise, check and publish it as one collected reply.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-033**: The cortex shall give the critic the reply's text and the transcript and no tool result.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-034**: When the critic vetoes a reply after the filler was spoken, the cortex shall close the line with an empty closing `brain.clause` with `cut` `vetoed` and `through` the filler's clause, and when nothing was published it shall end the think without a line.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-035**: Where the critic is `rules`, the cortex shall run the repetition check on the reply's model text so far across rounds, and when a clause before a call hits it, shall end the reply as CTX-GATE-012 and CTX-STREAM-021 say and run none of that round's calls.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-036**: The cortex shall run the mute and busy re-check at the start of each round and before each `tool.request` is published, ending the think on a mute or pause (without a line when nothing was published, as CTX-CTRL-008 says when a line exists) and ending the reply `cut` `busy`, or dropping it when nothing was published, on another's line.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-037**: When a mute or pause lands during a tool wait, the cortex shall stop the reply's line as CTX-CTRL-008 says when one exists, or abandon the wait at once and end the think without a line publishing `muted` when none does, and shall ignore results that arrive later.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-038**: When a `voice.stop` that names the reply's line or carries no `ids` arrives during a tool wait, the cortex shall end the reply as CTX-STREAM-035 says, abandon the wait and start no further round.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-039**: The cortex shall not cancel a `tool.request` it has published.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-040**: If the first round fails with nothing published, then the cortex shall treat the think as failed as CTX-STREAM-030 says.
- [ ] **CTX-TOOL-041**: The cortex shall record no tool call and no tool result in working memory or in memory.

- [x] **CTX-FALL-008**: When a `voice.failed` carrying `fallback` arrives for a streamed line and no other sink has published `voice.playing` for it when the cortex applies it, the cortex shall mark the working-memory line with the failed `seq` and the fallback text (`markFallback`), and shall keep a pending glitch holding the text of the clauses published from that `seq` on, the fallback text and that `seq`, replacing any earlier pending glitch.
- [x] **CTX-FALL-009**: When a `voice.failed` arrives for a line and another sink has published `voice.playing` for it when the cortex applies it, the cortex shall not mark the working-memory line, keep no glitch and record no heard text, because the line was heard on another output.
- [x] **CTX-FALL-010**: Where a pending glitch began after the first clause of its line, the prompt hint shall say "the rest of your line" in place of "your line" and quote only the clauses that were not heard.
- [x] **CTX-FALL-011**: When a `voice.failed` without `fallback` arrives for a streamed line and no other sink has published `voice.playing` for it, the cortex shall record the clauses before the failed `seq` as the heard text of the working-memory line (`markHeard`), the empty string when that `seq` is 0, and shall change neither the pending glitch nor its speaking state.
- [x] **CTX-CTRL-008**: When `control` `mute` or `pause` is addressed to the cortex while it has a reply line of its own pending (a line a think published, streamed or not), the cortex shall stop that line, closing it with `cut` `muted` if it is streamed and its reply had not closed.
- [x] **CTX-CTRL-009**: When `control` `mute` or `pause` is addressed to the cortex while it has reply lines pending, it shall publish one `voice.stop` whose `ids` name those lines and whose `reason` is `muted` for a mute and `paused` for a pause, and shall release those lines.
- [x] **CTX-CTRL-010**: An operator `say` line, a salience reflex line and a line of another source shall not be stopped by a `mute` or `pause`.
- [x] **CTX-CTRL-011**: When the cortex has no reply line pending, `control` `mute` or `pause` shall publish no `voice.stop`.
- [x] **CTX-EVT-013**: When a `voice.stop` the cortex published for a mute or pause is read back, the cortex shall release the lines it names as it does for any `voice.stop`.

## Failure on clause 0

Design: LLD Releasing a streamed line. Voice publishes no `voice.utterance` for a clause that fails to synthesize, so the claim window never starts.

- [x] **CTX-FALL-012**: When a `voice.done` flagged `final` from the sink `error` for clause 0 of a streamed line is read, the cortex shall record the working-memory line as heard by nobody (`markHeard` with the empty string), set no pending glitch, and close the line with `cut` `failed` and `through` 0.
