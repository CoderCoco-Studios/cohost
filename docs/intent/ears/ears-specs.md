# ears specs

Segment `ears` (prefix `EARS`). Mapped 2026-10-31 at git `686cc8f`; verified against the code in the audit pass of 2026-11-01. Owner decisions of 2026-11-01 applied at git `1b57790`; reworded for the listening and speaking split, built 2026-11-11. LLD: [ears-design.md](ears-design.md). Markers: `[x]` implemented, `[ ]` intended but not yet implemented (gap), `[D]` deferred.

The listening and speaking split moved the playback sink and the spoken fallback line to [mouth](../mouth/mouth-specs.md). Retired with it, numbers not reused: EARS-AUD-002 (now SHARED-AUDIO-006), EARS-PLAY-001 to 009 (now MOUTH-PLAY-001 to 009) and EARS-FALL-001 to 019 (now MOUTH-FALL-001 to 019).

## Startup plans

- [x] **EARS-START-001**: When `ears.source` is `off`, the ears service shall create no Discord client, require no Discord environment variables, publish no `speech.heard` other than injected ones, and stay running idle with heartbeat mode `off` and state `ready`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:16 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Off source -->
- [x] **EARS-START-002**: When `ears.source` is `discord` and `EARS_DISCORD_TOKEN` is unset or empty, the ears service shall not exit and shall create no Discord client, shall log an error at startup and a warning every 60 s, and shall report heartbeat state `degraded` (detail `EARS_DISCORD_TOKEN is not set`) with mode `live` and `deps.discord` `down`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:7 --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:49 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Discord session -->
- [x] **EARS-START-003**: The ears service shall subscribe its consumer group `ears` to `inject.request` only, in every startup plan, and shall publish no `voice.playing`, `voice.done` or `voice.failed`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:34 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Off source -->
- [x] **EARS-START-004**: The ears service shall choose its startup plan with the pure function `earsPlan(source, hasToken)` returning `off` for source `off` regardless of token, `no-token` for `discord` without a token, and `discord` otherwise, where `hasToken` is true only when `EARS_DISCORD_TOKEN` is non-empty and differs from `DISCORD_TOKEN`, and shall load discord.js only when the plan is `discord`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:24 --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:32 --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:41 -->
- [x] **EARS-START-005**: When the process receives a shutdown signal, the ears service shall, in the `off` and `no-token` plans, clear the idle warning timer, stop the bus and exit with code 0 (the `discord` plan's sequence is EARS-SESS-008). <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:89 -->
- [x] **EARS-START-006**: The ears service shall subscribe its consumer group `ears` with start mode `latest`, so the group is reset to the newest entry on every start and no `inject.request` published while ears was down is handled. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:76 -->
- [x] **EARS-START-007**: If `ears.source` is `discord` and `EARS_DISCORD_TOKEN` equals the non-empty `DISCORD_TOKEN`, then the ears service shall create no Discord client, log an error at startup and a warning every 60 s, and report heartbeat state `degraded` with mode `live`, `deps.discord` `down` and detail `EARS_DISCORD_TOKEN is the mouth bot's DISCORD_TOKEN; ears needs a bot of its own`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:41 --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:49 --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:59 -->

- [x] **EARS-START-008**: The ears service shall start through the shared service bootstrap (`runService`, SHARED-BOOT-*) and shall run its `off` and `no-token` plans through the shared idle plan (`startIdle`, SHARED-IDLE-*), with the idle reason text of EARS-START-002 and EARS-START-007. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->

## Discord session

- [x] **EARS-SESS-001**: If Discord login fails when the ears plan is `discord`, then the ears service shall log the error and exit the process with code 1. <!-- openspec: ears § Discord session -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-002**: When the Discord client is ready in the `discord` plan, the ears service shall join the voice channel `ears.discord.voice_channel_id` of guild `ears.discord.guild_id` (EARS-SESS-007) self-muted and not self-deafened, and wait up to 20 s for the connection to reach Ready. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:49 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Discord session -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-003**: If the configured channel is not a voice-based channel, then the ears join attempt shall fail with the error "ears.discord.voice_channel_id is not a voice channel". <!-- openspec: ears § Discord session -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-004**: When the ears voice connection reaches Ready, the ears service shall mark `deps.discord` `ok` and report the heartbeat `ready`, attaching no audio player to the connection. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:49 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Heartbeat (add-service-health) -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-007**: When joining in the `discord` plan, the ears service shall select the guild and voice channel from the configuration keys `ears.discord.guild_id` and `ears.discord.voice_channel_id` (which the example configuration fills from `DISCORD_GUILD_ID` and `DISCORD_VOICE_CHANNEL_ID`), not by reading those environment variables directly. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:7 -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-008**: When the process receives a shutdown signal in the `discord` plan, the ears service shall run one shutdown sequence that destroys the voice connection and the Discord client and then stops the bus before exiting. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:89 -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-009**: The ears service shall log in to Discord with the token in `EARS_DISCORD_TOKEN` and shall never log in with `DISCORD_TOKEN`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:32 -->
- [x] **EARS-SESS-010**: The ears service shall hold its voice connection through the shared Discord voice session (shared-runtime SHARED-DVS-*), self-muted and not self-deafened, with join retry base 5 s and cap 60 s; when the session reports a failed join it shall log the failure, report heartbeat `degraded` with `deps.discord` `down` and keep its bus subscription running, and when the session disconnects it shall set `deps.discord` `down`. (Replaces EARS-SESS-005 and EARS-SESS-006, retired, numbers not reused.) <!-- test: services/ears/test/discord.test.ts -->

## Listening and transcription

- [x] **EARS-HEAR-001**: When a user whose Discord account is not a bot account (judged by the user's `bot` flag, fetched from Discord before capture when the user is not cached) starts speaking in the ears voice channel and is not already being captured, the ears service shall subscribe to that user's Opus stream, ending the capture after `ears.silence_ms` (default 700) of silence, and shall not open a second subscription while one is active; it shall capture no audio from any bot account, including its own bot and mouth's bot. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:54 --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:70 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-002**: When a speaker's captured Opus stream ends, the ears service shall decode it to 48 kHz stereo PCM, label it with the speaker's guild display name (or user id when no member is cached), and hand it to transcription. <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-003**: If the Opus decoder for a speaker errors, then the ears service shall remove the speaker from its listening set and log the warning "decoder error". <!-- openspec: ears § Per-speaker capture -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-004**: When a captured utterance is shorter than `ears.min_speech_ms` (default 400), the ears service shall neither call speech-to-text nor publish anything. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:29 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-005**: When a captured utterance is long enough, the ears service shall convert it to 16 kHz mono WAV and POST it as multipart form data to `<STT_URL>/v1/audio/transcriptions` with fields `file`, `model` set to `ears.stt_model` (default `Systran/faster-whisper-small`), `language` set to `en`, and `response_format` set to `json`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:21,48 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-007**: If the transcript is empty or contains no word character (a Unicode letter or number), then the ears service shall publish nothing. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:29 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-006**: When the transcript contains at least one word character (a Unicode letter or number, in any script) and is not a drop phrase (EARS-HEAR-010), the ears service shall publish `speech.heard` with `speaker`, `speakerId`, `text` and `durationMs` (rounded to whole milliseconds). <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:21 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-011**: The ears service shall publish `speech.heard` `speakerId` as the speaker's Discord user id (the id of the captured stream), unmodified, so downstream services can tell two people with the same display name apart; it shall keep no identity mapping of its own. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-008**: If speech-to-text fails (non-2xx response or thrown error), then the ears service shall log the warning "transcription failed", publish no event, and keep running. <!-- test: services/ears/test/ears.test.ts:40 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Transcription and speech.heard -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-010**: When a live captured utterance transcribes to a transcript that, compared case-insensitively after trimming whitespace and every leading and trailing non-letter, non-number character and collapsing inner whitespace, equals an entry of `ears.drop_phrases` (default empty, compared the same way), the ears service shall publish nothing for it and log it at debug level. Injected clips are not filtered. <!-- test: services/ears/test/filter.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/transcript.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-HEAR-009**: The ears service shall not subscribe to `control`: it publishes `speech.heard` regardless of `mute` or `pause`, which cortex and salience enforce upstream. <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:44 -->

## Capture sources

- [x] **EARS-CAP-001**: The ears service shall read each clip from a capture source through the capture contract (`CaptureAdapter`, `decodeClip`) and pass it to `Hearing.heardClip`, which shall resample it to 16 kHz mono, drop it when shorter than `ears.min_speech_ms`, transcribe it, drop a transcript with no word character or equal to an `ears.drop_phrases` entry, and otherwise publish `speech.heard` with the given `speaker`, `text` and `durationMs`, without `injected` and without `speakerId`, returning the outcome (`heard`, `too-short`, `empty`, `drop-phrase` or `stt-error`). <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-002**: The ears service shall decode an uploaded clip with the shared `parseWav` when its content type is `audio/wav`, `audio/x-wav` or `audio/wave`, and as raw little-endian 16-bit PCM when it is `audio/L16` or `audio/pcm` with `rate` and `channels` parameters defaulting to 16000 and 1, and shall refuse any other type and any audio that does not decode (including non-16-bit WAV). <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-003**: Where `ears.source` is `http` or `dir`, the ears service shall create no Discord client, load no `discord.js`, require no Discord environment variable, subscribe its consumer group `ears` to `inject.request` only (EARS-START-003, EARS-START-006) and still handle injection (EARS-INJ-009). <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-004**: The ears service shall choose its plan with `earsPlan(source, hasToken, hasDirPath)` returning `http` for source `http` and `dir` for source `dir` regardless of token, `no-dir` for source `dir` with an empty `ears.dir.path`, and the plans of EARS-START-004 otherwise. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-005**: Where the ears plan is `http`, when a `POST /audio` carries a decodable clip, the ears service shall pass it to `Hearing.heardClip` with the `speaker` query parameter (default `http`) and answer 200 with JSON `{ heard, text?, reason? }` after the clip has been processed, and shall answer 404 for another path, 405 for another method, 415 for an unsupported content type, 400 for audio that does not decode and 502 when speech-to-text failed. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-006**: The ears `http` source shall refuse with 413 a body larger than `INJECT_MAX_BYTES` without buffering past that cap, cut off a body that takes longer than `INJECT_READ_TIMEOUT_MS`, process one clip at a time, and refuse with 503 a request that arrives while one clip is being processed and `INJECT_QUEUE_LIMIT` (shared-runtime SHARED-INJ-010) others are waiting. `INJECT_MAX_BYTES` and `INJECT_READ_TIMEOUT_MS` are the limits of EARS-INJ-010. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-007**: Where the ears plan is `http`, the ears service shall listen on `ears.http.host` and `ears.http.port`, report the heartbeat `ready` once it is listening, and, when the port cannot be bound, stay running with the heartbeat `degraded` and `deps.http` `down`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts, services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-008**: Where the ears plan is `dir`, the ears service shall poll `ears.dir.path` every `ears.dir.poll_ms`, create the directory when it is missing, and process each regular file whose name ends in `.wav` (any case) once, only after its size is unchanged between two consecutive polls, through `Hearing.heardClip` with speaker `dir`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-dir.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-009**: After a `dir` file is processed, the ears service shall rename it with the suffix `.done`, or `.rejected` when it is larger than `INJECT_MAX_BYTES` (never read) or does not decode, or `.failed` when speech-to-text failed; if the rename fails it shall remember the name in memory and never read the file again. It shall take at most `INJECT_QUEUE_LIMIT` files per poll, in name order, one at a time. <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-dir.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-010**: Where `ears.source` is `dir` and `ears.dir.path` is empty, the ears service shall not exit, shall create no poller, and shall idle `degraded` with the detail `ears.dir.path is not set` (shared idle plan), still subscribed to `inject.request`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-011**: Where the ears plan is `http` or `dir`, the ears heartbeat shall report mode `live` (`mock` with the stub engine), `deps.discord` `n/a` and `deps.stt` as in EARS-HLTH-003, plus `deps.http` or `deps.dir` `ok` while the listener is bound or the directory is being polled and `down` otherwise. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-CAP-012**: When the process receives a shutdown signal in the `http` or `dir` plan, the ears service shall close the listener or stop the poller, wait for the clip in flight, and then let `runService` stop the bus (one `onStop` step). <!-- test: services/ears/test/capture-http.test.ts, services/ears/test/capture-dir.test.ts -->

## Audio injection

- [x] **EARS-INJ-005**: When an `inject.request` for ears with `kind` `audio` and an upload id arrives, the ears service shall send the bytes read from Redis unchanged to speech-to-text with the request's `contentType` as hint, without applying `ears.min_speech_ms`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-006**: When an injected clip transcribes to a transcript with at least one word character (a Unicode letter or number), the ears service shall publish `speech.heard` with `speaker` set to `meta.user` or `injected`, `durationMs` from the WAV header (0 for non-WAV), `injected` true and the request's `remember`, followed by `inject.result` with `ok` true and the text as detail. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:27,36,46,91 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-007**: While the ears plan is `discord`, the ears service shall start handling `inject.request` for ears before it logs in to Discord and keep handling it whether or not the voice-channel join succeeds. <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio (MUST work when Discord is not connected) --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/session.test.ts:26 -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-008**: If an injected clip's transcript is empty or contains no word character (a Unicode letter or number), or speech-to-text or the upload read throws, then the ears service shall publish `inject.result` with `ok` false (detail "empty transcript" or the error text) and no `speech.heard`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject.test.ts:54,64 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-009**: Where the ears plan is `off` or `no-token`, the ears service shall still handle `inject.request` for ears. <!-- openspec: ears § Injected audio -->
- [x] **EARS-INJ-010**: The ears service shall handle `inject.request` through the shared inject handler (shared-runtime SHARED-INJ-*) with target `ears`, the single kind `audio`, a byte cap of 26214400 (the dashboard's default audio upload cap) and an upload read timeout of 30000 ms, taking the upload through its bus connection (`Bus.takeUpload`). (EARS-INJ-001 to 004 are retired, now SHARED-INJ-001 to 005; numbers not reused.) <!-- test: services/ears/test/inject-limits.test.ts -->

- [x] **EARS-INJ-011**: While a clip from an `inject.request` is being read or transcribed, the ears loop shall already have returned from that request, so the next `inject.request` is read without waiting for speech-to-text. <!-- test: services/ears/test/loop.test.ts -->

## Stub transcriber

- [x] **EARS-STUB-001**: Where `ears.stt_engine` is `stub`, the ears service shall transcribe every captured or injected clip without any network request, returning the text `stub transcript (<n> ms)` where n is the clip's duration (0 when unknown). <!-- test: services/ears/test/stub.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/filter.test.ts -->
- [x] **EARS-STUB-002**: Where `ears.stt_engine` is `stub`, the ears heartbeat shall report mode `mock` (or `off` when `ears.source` is `off`) and `deps.stt` `n/a`, and the service shall log a startup warning that transcripts are stubbed. <!-- test: services/ears/test/stub.test.ts --> <!-- test: services/ears/test/service.test.ts -->

## Health

- [x] **EARS-HLTH-001**: The ears service shall publish heartbeats with `service` `ears`, mode `off` when `ears.source` is `off`, `mock` with the stub engine and `live` otherwise, and `deps` `{discord, stt}` (plus `http` or `dir` for those sources, EARS-CAP-011), where both are `n/a` for the `off` plan. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:7,16,69 --> <!-- openspec: ears § Heartbeat (add-service-health) -->
- [x] **EARS-HLTH-002**: While the ears plan is `discord`, the ears service shall report `deps.discord` `ok` only while the voice connection is Ready and `down` otherwise (always `down` for `no-token`). <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:7,69 -->
- [x] **EARS-HLTH-003**: When a speech-to-text call (live or injected) fails, the ears service shall set `deps.stt` to `down`, and when the next call succeeds it shall set it back to `ok`. <!-- test: services/ears/test/health.test.ts:69 -->
